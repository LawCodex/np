Act No. 15 of the year 2045 (1988)

An Act Made to Make Provisions Relating to Seeds

Preamble : Whereas, it is expedient to maintain the convenience and economic interest of the general public by providing the Seeds of quality-standards in a well planned manner upon producing, processing and testing the Seeds of high quality-standards to have the production of different crops increased.

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev, on the advice and with consent of the Rastriya Panchayat.

1. Short Title and Commencement:

1.1 This Act may be called as “Seeds Act, 2045 (1988)”

1.2 This Act shall come into force in such areas on such dates as may be appointed by Government of Nepal by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette2.

2. Definitions:

2.1 Unless, the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act;-

2.1.1 “Seeds” means a matured ovule having embryonic plant, food substance and protective cover or seeds or germ which can be used in sowing or planting to produce crops by reproducing in a sexual or asexual mode.

2.1.2 “Crops" means crops such as fruits, cereals, vegetables, cash crops and grass crops.

2.1.3 “Agricultural Activity” means the activity of producing cereals, fruits, legumes, oil crops, vegetables, cash crops and grass.

2.1.4 Board” means the National Seeds Board constituted pursuant to Section 3. <sup>3</sup>2.1.5 “Laboratory” means the Seeds Testing Laboratory established pursuant to Section 9.

2.1.6 “Notified Seeds” means the seeds prescribed pursuant to Section 11.

2.1.7 “Container” means a box, tin can, bottle, bag, jute-bag, bin, barrel or similar other container used to store the seeds.

2.1.8 “Breeder” means a person, organization or body which brings into use any variety of the crops by 4breeding or selecting it for the first time. <sup>5</sup>2.1.9 "Type" means all close species or sub-species of any plant that is identified or identifiable by the same general name.

<sup>6</sup>2.1.10 “Variety” means a plant grouping within a single botanical taxon of the lowest known rank, which can be defined by the expression of the characteristics resulting from a given genotype of that plant grouping, distinguished from any other plant grouping by expression of at least one of the said characteristics and considered as a unit with regard to its suitability for being propagated, which remains unchanged after such propagation."

2.1.11 “Label” means a description relating to the Seeds 7label affixed on the Container in a visible manner.

2.1.12 “Prescribed” or “As Prescribed” means prescribed or as prescribed in the Rules framed under this Act.

3. Constitution of National Seeds Board:

3.1 There shall be constituted a National Seeds Board to formulate and implement policies relating to the Seeds and to give necessary advice to Government of Nepal on the matters pertaining to the Seeds. <sup>8</sup>3.2 The following chairperson and members shall comprise of the Board:

3.2.5 General Manager, Agricultural Development Bank - Member 3.2.6 General Manager, National Seeds Organization LTD. - Member 3.2.7 Three Scientist includes woman related with agriculture, veterinary and forest science, nominated by Government of Nepal. - Member 3.2.8 Seeds Expert (At least Assistant professor), Agriculture and Vetenary Science Study Institute, Tribhuvan University. - Member 3.2.9 Two Seeds Entrepreneurs includes woman nominated by Government of Nepal - Member 3.2.10 Two Seeds producer and farmer includes woman nominated by Government of Nepal - Member 3.2.11 Chief, Seeds Quality Control Centre -Member- Secretary <sup>9</sup>3.3 The tenure of the Member nominated by Government of Nepal pursuant to Sub-sections 3.2.7, 3.2.9 and 3.2.10 shall be Two years.

3.4 Notwithstanding anything contained in Sub-section 3.3, Government of Nepal may remove any member nominated pursuant to Sub-sections 103.2.7, 3.2.9 and 3.2.10 prior to the expiry of his/her term of office.

3.5 Government of Nepal may, by publishing a notification in the Nepal Gazette, make necessary addition or subtraction or alteration in the membership of the Board, if it deems necessary.

3.6 The Board may, if it deems necessary, invite any native or foreign expert or adviser to take part in a meeting of the Board as an observer.

4. Meeting and Decision of the Board:

4.1 The meeting of the Board shall be held on such date, in such time and place as appointed by the Chairperson of the Board

4.2 If fifty percent Members of the total number of Members of the Board are present at a meeting of the Board, it shall be deemed to constitute a quorum for the meeting.

4.3 The meeting of the Board shall be chaired by the chairperson of the Board and in the event of his absence, it shall be chaired by a Member selected by the Member's present at the meeting from amongst themselves.

4.4 The opinion of the majority shall prevail at the meeting of the Board, and in the event of ties, the Chairperson shall exercise the casting vote

4.5 The decision of the Board shall be authenticated by the Member- Secretary.

4.6 Other procedures relating to the meetings of the Board shall be as determined by the Board itself.

5. Functions, Duties and Powers of the Board:

5.1 To provide advice to Government of Nepal to formulate the national policy relating to the Seeds

5.2 To maintain co-ordination between the private and government sectors in the activity of the production and distribution of the Seeds.

5.3 To encourage the private sector to make investment in the Seeds industry. <sup>11</sup>5.4 To regulate or control the quality-standards of the Seeds to be produced within Nepal or imported outside the country.

5.5 To approve, release and register the Seeds of new variety as prescribed.

5.6 To test the specialty, uniformity and permanency of the Seeds of new Variety and grant the right of ownership to the Breeder as prescribed.

5.7 To determine the quality-standards of the Seeds.

5.8 To approve the quality-standards of the Seeds determined by native or foreign bodies as per necessity.

<sup>12</sup>5.8a To give advice to Government of Nepal on service charges relating to Seeds, <sup>13</sup>5.8b To give advice to Government of Nepal on the notification of the type and variety of crops. <sup>14</sup>5.8c To cancel the license who has conducted laboratory without renewal. <sup>15</sup>5.8d To take examination of Seeds Inspector, Seeds Sample Collector or Seeds Analyst for license to work.

5.9 To carry out other necessary activities relating to the Seeds.

6. Power to Constitute Sub-Committees:

6.1 The Board may, as per necessity, constitute different sub-committees for the purpose of making available the qualitative Seeds by smoothly carrying out the act of production, processing and sale and distribution of the Seeds.

6.2 The functions, duties and powers as well as procedures relating to the meetings of the sub-committees shall be as prescribed.

#### 11. Powers to Notify the Type or Variety of the Seeds

If Government of Nepal deems it necessary to regulate and control the quality standards for any Type or Variety of the Seeds to be used for the Agricultural Activity, it may, in consultation with the Board, prescribe the Seeds of such Type or Variety as the Notified Seeds, by publishing a Notification in the Nepal Gazette, and while so prescribing it may also prescribe the Type or Variety of the Seeds appropriate for different regions.

11A.2 Seeds inspector shall seize such seeds if the sales and distribution of seeds has been made without taking license pursuant to Sub-section 11A.1. 30 11B. Not notified seeds shall not be sold or distributed:

11B.1 Except for the purpose of agriculture investigation, nobody may sell and distribute seeds which is not notified pursuant this Act.

11B.2 Seeds inspector shall seize such seeds if the sales and distribution of seeds has been made beyond the Notification as pursuant to Sub-section 11B.1.

15. To Obtain Permit to Export or Import the Notified Seeds:

15.1 Any person desiring to export or import the Notified Seeds of any Type or Variety shall have to submit an application, accompanied by 34the fees and the details as prescribed, to the prescribed authority for a permit to export or import the same.

15.2 The prescribed authority may upon receiving an application pursuant to Sub-section 15.1 provide approval.

15.3 Notwithstanding anything contained in Sub-section 15.1, an industry established with an objective of exporting or importing the Seeds may export or import the Notified Seeds by giving such details of the Notified Seeds intended to be exported or imported to the prescribed authority.

16.2 In cases where Seeds Inspector, Seeds Sample Collector and Seeds Analyst have not been so appointed, Government of Nepal may designate any civil employee to carry out the functions of such Inspector, Seeds Sample Collector or Analysts.

16.3 Except otherwise provided in Sub-section 16.1 and 16.2, nobody should inspect, collect sample and analyze the seeds.

16.4 A person who intends to obtain a license and who has the qualification as prescribed pursuant to Sub-section 16.3, has to pass the examination to be taken by the Board.

16.5 A person who has passed the examination pursuant to Sub-section 16.4 has to make an application in a prescribed format and along with the prescribed fees to the Board for a license to carry on the business as the Seeds Inspector, Seeds Sample Collector or Seeds Analyst.

16.6 If an application is made pursuant to Sub-section 16.5, the Board shall issue a license in a prescribed format and upon receiving the prescribed fees maintaining the prescribed conditions and such license shall be valid for a period of Two years.

16.7 The license issued pursuant to Sub-section 16.6 shall be renewed as prescribed.

16.8 In case such license is not renewed pursuant to Sub-section 16.7, such license shall ipso facto be cancelled.

16.9 Other procedure relating to examination pursuant to Sub-section 16.4 shall be as prescribed.

#### 18. Recognition by Government of Nepal

Government of Nepal may, in consultation with the Board and by publishing a Notification in the Nepal Gazette, grant recognition to a foreign or international or national organization which examines or authenticates seeds or examines the variety of the seeds for the purpose of the Act.

19.3 Any person who sells, distributes or causes for the same without setting out the descriptions as referred to in Section 13 shall be fined from Ten Thousand Rupees to Fifteen Thousand Rupees.

19.4 Any person who carries out act in contravention of Section 16.3 shall be fined Twenty Five Thousand Rupees.

19.5 Any person or organization who carries out any act in contravention of this Act and the Regulation framed hereunder, save the activity set forth in the above Sub-sections 19.1, 19.2, 19.3 and 19.4 shall be fined from Ten Thousand Rupees to Twenty Thousand Rupees.

#### 20. Government to be plaintiff

Government of Nepal shall be the plaintiff in the cases under this Act.

21. Investigation and Institution of Cases.

21.1 The 41Seeds Sample Collector shall investigate the cases relating to the offences punishable pursuant to this Act and, shall after the completion of such investigation institute the case before the Adjudicating Authority empowered to hear the case.

21.2 While investigating and instituting the case pursuant to Sub-section 12.1, the 42Seeds Sample Collector may seek opinion of the Government Attorney. After the institution of the case, the Government Attorney shall carry out the pleading, dealing with and appealing in the case.

22. Power to Hear Cases:

22.1 The authority as prescribed shall have the power to hear the case under this Act.

22.2 In hearing and settling the case pursuant to Sub-section 22.1, the prescribed authority shall follow the procedures as referred to in the Special Court Act, 2059 (2003).

#### 23. Saving of Actions Carried Out in Good Faith

Seeds Inspector or <sup>43</sup>Seeds Sample Collector or Seeds Analyst or any employee authorized to act under this Act shall not be personally liable for any act and action carried out in good faith in the course of discharging his/her functions pursuant to this Act.

Provided that, the concerned person shall be liable for any act carried out by him/her with an intention of causing unnecessary harm, harassment or trouble to anyone.

#### 24. Power to Frame Rules

Government of Nepal may frame necessary Rules in order to carry out the objectives of this Act. ′
