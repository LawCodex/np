Preamble : Whereas it is expedient to maintain the economic interest and convince of the general public by further bringing about dynamism in the development of agriculture of the nation, and

Whereas, it is expedient to establish an efficient Agricultural Development Bank through the merger of the existing Co-operative Bank with a view to making available capital and loans to the agricultural sector,

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev on the advice and with the consent of the Rastriya Panchayat.

### Chapter - 1

Preliminary

#### 1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called "Agricultural Development Bank Act, 2024 (1967)".

(2) It shall extend to the whole of the Kingdom of Nepal.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as His Majesty's Government may, by Notification published in the Nepal Gazette, specify.1

Unless the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act-

(a) "Bank" means the Agricultural Development Bank established under this Act.

(b) "Board" means the Board of Directors of the Bank.

### Chapter - 5

Fund, Accounts and Audit

#### 24. Fund of the Bank

The Bank shall have a separate fund of its own.

Any proceed to be obtained by the Bank from any source shall be deposited in that fund and any expenditure to be incurred on behalf of the Bank shall be borne out of that fund.

35 25. Management of the Fund: (1) The Bank shall establish a Reserve Fund by earmarking a minimum of Twenty percent out of its net profit of each year. Such amount shall be required to be continuously earmarked until it is equivalent to the paid up share capital of the Bank.

(2) No proceeds of the Reserve Fund may be given as loan or incurred otherwise except for investing them in a secured manner.

(3) The Bank shall have the right to establish separate funds for carrying out various activities and may provide loans or incur expenses out of such funds.

36 25A.Risk-bearing Fund : The Bank shall be required to maintain a Risk- Bearing Fund in such ratio as specified by the Rastra Bank and in such manner as to cover the risk on the liability of its business not covered by its total assets or the balance-sheet.

#### 36. Penalties

(2) Whosoever uses the name of the Bank in any statement or advertisement without its written approval, he shall be punished with imprisonment for a term not exceeding Six months or with fine not exceeding Two Hundred Fifty Rupees or with both.

(3) Whosoever deliberately or with mala fide intention causes harm or attempts to cause harm to the Bank by way of omission of any act required to be committed by him under this Act or Rules made thereunder or by way of doing any act prohibited by this Act or Rules made thereunder, he shall be punished with imprisonment for a term not exceeding Three years or with fine not exceeding Three Thousand rupees or both and the damaged value shall recovered from the offender.

(4) A Director or employee of the Bank who is in violation of any order or instruction issued under this Act or Rules made thereunder, or deliberately makes a mistake in the course of carrying out such order or instruction, he shall be punished with fine not exceeding Five Hundred Rupees and if such violation or mistake is persistent, he shall be punished with additional fine at the rate of Fifty Rupees for each day during the period of such occurrence.

(5) Whosoever violates any other provision of this Act or Rules made thereunder other than those as stipulated in Sub-section (4) above, he shall be punished with fine not exceeding One Hundred Rupees.

#### 43. Co-operative Bank to be Merged

The Co-operative Bank established under the Co-operative Bank Act, 1963 shall be deemed to have been merged with the Bank to be established under this Act from the date of the establishment, and all share capital, assets and liabilities of the Co-operative Bank shall be deemed to have been transferred in the name of this Bank.

#### 44. Power to frame Rules and Bye-laws

(1) The Bank may frame Rules and Bye-laws in order to run its business in a systematic manner and to carry out the objectives of this Act:

Provided that, Rules or Bye-laws so framed shall not be effective without the approval of His Majesty's Government.
