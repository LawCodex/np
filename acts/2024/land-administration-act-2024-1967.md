# Land Administration Act, 2024 (1967)

Land Administration Act, 2024 (1967) Date of Authentication 2024.7.6(23 October 1967) Amendment Administration of Justice Act, 2048 (1991) 2048.2.16(30 May 1991) Act number 14 of the year 2024 (1967) An Act Made to Provide for Land Administration Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to maintain convenience and economic interests of the general public by maintaining inventories of the lands, landowners and tenants in a modern fashion and making timely improvements in the provisions pertaining to current cultivation of the lands subject to land revenue;

Be it enacted by His Majesty King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev, on the advice and with the consent of the Rastriya Panchayat.

### Chapter -1

Preliminary 1. Short title and commencement: (1) This Act may be cited as the "Land Administration Act, 2024 (1967)."

(2) This Act shall come into force in such District or Districts and on such date as may be specified by Government of Nepal by a notification in the Nepal Gazette.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act:

(a) "Land Administration Office" means the Land Administration Office established under Section 3.

(b) "Land revenue" includes such land revenue and any other cess equivalent thereto as required to be paid by a landowner to Government of Nepal pursuant to the Nepal law.

(c) “Land Administrator” means the chief officer of the Land Administration Office.

(d) “Notified order” means an order published by Government of Nepal in the Nepal Gazette.

(e) “Prescribed” or “as prescribed” means prescribed or as prescribed in the Rules framed or notified order issued under this Act.

### Chapter 2

Provisions relating to Land Administration Office and Registration of Land 3. Establishment of Land Administration Office: (1) Government of Nepal shall, by a notification in the Nepal Gazette, establish a Land Administration Office in every District where this Act has come into force.

(2) The Land Reform Office and the Land Revenue Office existing in a District where the Land Administration Office has been established pursuant to Sub-section (1) at the time of its establishment shall be deemed to have ipso facto been dissolved.

(3) It shall be the duty of the concerned employees of the Land Reform Office and the Land Revenue Office dissolved pursuant to Sub-section (2) to hand over all documents related with lands and other instruments retained in such Land Reform Office and Land Revenue Office to the Land Administrator or the employee designated by him/her within two months after the date of such dissolution.

(4) The Land Administrator and the Land Administration Office shall, after the commencement of this Act and subject to this Act, exercise and perform all of such powers and duties as may be performed by the prescribed authority and Land Reform Office respectively, under the Lands Act, in a District where this Act has come into force.

(5) The Land Administration Office and the Land Administrator shall, after the commencement of this Act and subject to this Act, exercise and perform all of such powers and duties as conferred to the Land Revenue Office and the In-charge of Land Revenue Office, respectively, under the prevailing Nepal law, in a District where this Act has come into force.

(6) Any such act and action initiated by the Land Revenue Office or In-charge of the Land Revenue office or other office or officer dealing with the act and action relating to land revenue recovery or current cultivation of land in accordance with the Nepal law prevailing immediately at the time of commencement of this Act but is yet to be completed may be carried on by the Land Administrator and the Land Administration Office after the commencement of this Act; and all the powers and duties conferred to such Office and officer under that law in respect of such act and action shall be exercised and performed by the Land Administration Office and the Land Administrator, respectively.

#### 4. Registration of land

(1) The Land Administration Office shall register each land within its jurisdiction, setting out the following details, in such format as prescribed:

(a) Number, boundaries, area, type of the land and Village Development Committee or Municipality where the land is situated,

(b) Full name, surname and address of the landowner,

(c) In the case of a land subject to tenancy, full name, surname and address of the tenant, year and date of tenancy and rent to be paid by the tenant to the landowner.

(2) In maintaining a register of lands for the purposes of Sub-section (1), the register shall be maintained in accordance with an inventory prepared after the survey and measurement of lands in the place where such survey and measurement has been carried out and in accordance with the inventory received from the Land Reform Office and the Land Revenue Office where such an inventory has not been prepared or where survey and measurement of land has not been carried out.

### Chapter-3

Recovery of Land Revenue and Procedures Pertaining Thereto 5. Powers to recover land revenue: The Land Administrator shall have functions, duties and powers to recover the land revenue pursuant to this Act.

Provided that, Government of Nepal may, by a notified order, confer such functions, duties and powers to recover the land revenue to the local bodied or such local body or any other person as prescribed.

#### 6. Payment of land revenue

A landowner shall pay the land revenue fixed pursuant to the Nepal law in respect of each land other than the land prescribed or as prescribed.

#### 7. Time limit for payment of land revenue

Every landowner who is required to pay the land revenue pursuant to Section 6 shall pay the land revenue payable by him/her within the last day of Baisakh (around 13 May) each year.

#### 8. Return of land in respect of which land revenue is due

The local body or any other prescribed person responsible for recovering the land revenue shall prepare a return, setting out the following details, of a land in respect of which the land revenue is due and payable by the time limit as referred to in Sub-section (1) of Section 7 within the last day of Jestha (around 13 June) each year. Where the local body or any other prescribed person, except the Land Administrator is responsible for recovering the land revenue, such local body or any other prescribed person shall send a copy of such return to the Land Administration Office:

(a) Village Development Committee or Municipality where the land is situated,

(b) Name, surname and address of the landowner,

(c) Plot number and area of the land,

(d) Land revenue chargeable,

(e) Land revenue recovered,

(f) Land revenue due and recoverable, and (g) In the case of a land subject to tenancy, full name, surname and address of the tenant tilling such land.

#### 9. Final return of land in respect of which land revenue is due

The Land Administrator shall prepare a final return of the lands in respect of which net land revenue is due and payable, other than the lands in respect of which the landowners have paid the land revenue directly to the Land Administration Office or through a bank in favor of the Land Administrator, out of the lands recorded as the lands in respect of which the land revenue is due and payable, based on the return prepared pursuant to Section 8; a notice setting out the following details in respect of such land shall be published as prescribed for auction within the 15th day of Ashad (around 28 June) each year.

(a) Village Development Committee or Municipality where the land is situated,

(b) Name, surname and address of the landowner,

(c) Plot number and area of the land,

(d) Land revenue due and recoverable,

(e) Venue, date and time for auction, and (f) In the case of a land subject to tenancy, full name, surname and address of the tenant tilling such land.

#### 10. Action to be taken in event of land revenue falling due and payable

(1) The Land Administrator may withhold the land owned by a landowner who fails to pay the land revenue which appears due and payable from the return as referred to in Section 8, until the due and payable land revenue is paid up or auction sale is made for the same. The landowner may cultivate as previously the land so withheld.

(2) The officer responsible for recovering land revenue along with an interest by one percent, from a landowner who pays the land revenue until a notice is published pursuant to Section 9 after expiry of the time limit referred to in Section 7 or the time limit extended pursuant to Section 12.

(3) The Land Administrator shall recover the land revenue, along with an interest by five per cent, from a landowner who pays the due and payable revenue prior to auction sale after a notice has been published pursuant to Section 9.

(4) The Land Administrator shall auction such land, out of the land withheld, as may be required for the recovery of land revenue in order to recover the land revenue due and payable by a landowner who fails to pay the land revenue and chargeable fine prior to the auction sale after the publication of a notice pursuant to Section 9.

Provided that, even if such land belonging to a landowner who tills it on his/her own is auctioned, his/her right to tenancy over that land shall continue to exist, subject to the prevailing law.

(5) The Land Administrator shall refund such amount as remains after deducting the due land revenue and chargeable fine from the proceeds of the auction sale made pursuant to Sub-section (4) to the landowner.

#### 11. Payment of recovered land revenue

(1) Where the local body or any other prescribed person, except the Land Administrator is responsible for recovering the land revenue, that local body or any other prescribed person shall pay the amount of recovered land revenue to the Office of Land Administrator within the prescribed time limit. Provided, however, that if such amount exceeds one thousand five hundred rupees, it shall be paid within five days.

(2) The Land Administrator shall also pay the amount so paid to him/her to the office or bank as prescribed within three days.

#### 12. Powers to extend time limit for payment of land revenue

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in Section 7, if the Land Administrator thinks that there exists a circumstance in any area where land revenue cannot be paid in any year within the time limit prescribed in that Section and makes recommendation, accompanied by the reason for the same, for the extension of time limit for payment of land revenue, Government of Nepal may, by a notified order, extend the time limit for payment and recovery of land revenue.

(2) Where the time limit is extended pursuant to Sub-section (1), action shall be taken pursuant to Sections 8, 9 and 10 in the case of the area in respect whereof the time limit is so extended only after the expiration of that extended time limit; in such a case, the time limit shall be set by also adding those days of the extended time limit to the time limits mentioned in these Section for taking various actions.

#### 13. Payment of land revenue through bank

Notwithstanding anything contained in the other Sections of this Act, a landowner may pay the land revenue required to be paid by him/her, setting out his/her name, surname, address, Village Development Committee or Municipality, plot of his/her land and the Village Development Committee or Municipality where the land is situated, to the Nepal Rastra Bank in an area where this Bank is available and to the prescribed bank or District Administration Office in a place where the Nepal Rastra Bank is not available, in favour of the concerned Land Administration Office within the time limit as referred to in Section 7 or the time limit extended pursuant to Section 12.

Provided that, if a land is auctioned because a voucher or receipt of payment of land revenue by its landowner accordingly does not reach the Office of Land Administrator prior to such auction, such auction sale shall not be void. The Land Administrator shall refund the amount paid to the bank to the landowner.

#### 14. Issuance of receipt

The Land Administrator or officer recovering land revenue shall issue a receipt, as prescribed, of the paid land revenue to the landowner or the person making payment on his/her behalf.

#### 15. Remission of land revenue in the event of river cutting, sand piling (Baluburj) or flood, landslide or hailstone

(1) If a land owned by any person fails to yield crops because it is cut by the river or stream or becomes sandy land because of the river or flood or becomes subject to landslide or overrun with landslide or because of hailstone, that person shall make an application setting out the following details to the officer recovering land revenue within fifteen days except the time required for journey:

(a) Village Development Committee or Municipality where the land is situated,

(b) Plot number of the land,

(c) Land revenue chargeable,

(d) Area of such portion of the land as has failed to yield crops because it has been cut by the river, sandy land because of the river or flood, become subject to landslide or been overrun with landslide or because of hailstone,

(e) Land revenue that has to be remitted, and (f) Full name, surname and address of the landowner.

Provided that, in the case of a land yielding crops, such application shall be made prior to the cultivation of such crops.

(2) Where any application is made pursuant to Sub-section (1), in the case of a place other than a place where the Land Administrator is responsible for recovering land revenue, the office recovering land revenue shall forward all such applications, accompanied by the matters which he/she has seen and known about that matter as well as his/her opinion, to the Office of Land Administrator.

(3)The Land Administrator shall enquire into the matter by deputing a doer mission (mobile team), as required, to find out the reality of the matters contained in the application made pursuant to Sub-section (1) and the documents submitted pursuant to Sub-section (2). If it appears from such inquiry that land revenue should be remitted, the Land Administrator shall determine to what extent the land revenue is to be remitted, remit the same accordingly and give information thereof to Government of Nepal within seven days.

(4) If it appears from the inquiry as referred to in Sub-section (3) that any land has become useless forever, the Land Administrator shall make a submission to Government of Nepal for crossing off the records of such land; and Government of Nepal shall, if it thinks reasonable, order the Land Administrator to cross of the records of that land.

#### 16. Remission of land revenue in the event of non-yielding of crops because of drought or other natural calamity

(1) Where in any year the crops are destroyed in any area because of drought, flood, hailstone, Salaha, or any other natural calamity and the Land Administrator concludes following necessary inquiry that land revenue should be remitted in full or in part, the Land Administrator shall make a submission, accompanied by his/her opinion, to Government of Nepal.

(2) Where a submission is made pursuant to Sub-section (1) or information of such situation comes to the knowledge of Government of Nepal, Government of Nepal may also inquire the local gentlepersons, if so required, and remit the land revenue to such extent as appears necessary in that area.

#### 17. Rights, facilities, powers, duties and remuneration of officer recovering land revenue

The rights, facilities, powers, obligations, duties and remuneration of officer recovering land revenue, other than those specifically mentioned in this Act, shall be as prescribed.

#### 18. Responsibility of officer recovering land revenue for violation of law

Where the commission of any act by the officer recovering land revenue in violation of this Act or any other prevailing law with intention to cause any illegal benefit or loss knowingly or with mala fide intention in the recovery of land revenue or the failure of that officer to perform such function or duty as required to be performed pursuant to law results in any kind of loss or damage to Government of Nepal or any other person, that officer shall be personally liable to bear the amount of such loss or damage or other liability; and where the authority recovering land revenue is a local body, the members of that local body shall bear such liability both collectively and personally.

Provided that, a member who is not involved in any such act violating law and give information thereof to the Office of Land Administrator in time shall not be required to bear liability as referred to in this Section.

#### 19. Punishment to person who causes obstruction in recovery of land revenue

(1) If any person causes obstruction in the recovery of land revenue, the Land Administrator may punish such person with a fine not exceeding Five Hundred Rupees, upon inquiring the local body into the matter and executing a summary memo.

(2) An appeal may be made in the Court of Appeal against a decision made by the Land Administrator pursuant to Sub-section (1).

### Chapter-4

Provisions relating to Cultivation of New Land 20. Prohibition on cultivation of land: (1) No person shall cultivate or cause to be cultivated any road in use since long ago, highway, pasture land (Gauchar), water-bank, inn, charitable rest houses (pati, pauwa), graveyard, cremation yard and any land which has been in public use for similar other public activity or which is adjoining (Sandhisarpan).

(2) If any person gets registered and cultivates any such land as is prohibited from being registered and cultivated pursuant to Sub-section (1), the Land Administrator shall impose on that person a fine that is two fold of the amount of land revenue chargeable pursuant to the prevailing law from the year in which that person has started cultivating the land and gets that land to remain barren.

#### 21. Cultivation of registered land

(1) No person shall cultivate, or cause to be cultivated, any land that is not registered, without obtaining permission of the committee prescribed pursuant to this Section.

(2) A person who does not own land up to four Bigahas in the capacity of landowner makes an application as prescribed to the prescribed committee through Land Administration Office, the prescribed committee may give permission to such person to cultivate any land as referred to in Sub-section (1), subject to this  Amended by Administration of Justice Act, 2048 (1991) Act and the Rules framed under this Act and other prevailing Nepal law. The terms and conditions for the distribution and cultivation of such land and the fees for the same shall be as prescribed.

Provided that,

(a) Nothing contained in this Sub-section shall be deemed to confer powers to such committee to give permission to cultivate such land as is prohibited from being cultivated pursuant to Section 20.

(b) If separate applications are made by more than one person under this section for permission to cultivate any one land, permission shall be given according to priority as prescribed.

(3) If any person cultivates or causes to be cultivated any land as referred to in Sub-section (1) without obtaining approval pursuant to Sub-section (2), the Land Administrator shall impose on such person a fine not exceeding ten-fold of the amount of land revenue chargeable pursuant to the prevailing law right from the year in which that person has started cultivating such land and forfeit such land. The land so forfeited shall be subject to the provisions as prescribed.

(4) The Land Administrator shall implement any decision or order made by the prescribed committee pursuant to this Section.

### Chapter-5

Miscellaneous 22. Powers to frame rules: Government of Nepal may frame rules in order to accomplish the objective of this Act.

#### 23. Prevalence of this Act

This Act and the Rules framed hereunder shall apply on the matters mentioned herein and in other matters prevailing Nepal law shall apply.
