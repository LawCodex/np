Act Number 15 of the year 2026 An Act made to provide for managing production, exhibition and distribution of motion picture.

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to manage the production, exhibition and distribution of motion picture throughout Nepal for the convenience of the people in general Now therefore, His Majesty's King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev has made this Act on the advice and with the consent of the Rastriya Panchyat.

#### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the "Motion Picture (Production, Exhibition and Distribution) Act, 2026 (1969)."

(2) This Act shall extent throughout Nepal.

(3) This act shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires; in this Act,

(a) " Film Consort Board" means the board constituted pursuant to Section 7.

(b) "Cinema Hall" includes the main cinema hall and other houses within its premises to exhibit motion picture.

(c) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed or as prescribed in the Rules framed under this Act.

#### 9. Documentary also to be exhibited

(1) At the outset and interval of the exhibition of a Film, Nepalese record and documentaries relating to scientific invention or education or current national or international incident having immense value shall also be required to be played and shown to the extent possible.

(2) Government of Nepal may issue an order to the license holder to exhibiting of motion picture, to exhibit the motion picture made only Government of Nepal or any documentary motion picture obtained to Government of Nepal and it shall be the duty of such motion picture exhibitor to abide by such order.

#### 10. Ticket Rate and Class

The various provisions regarding classes and ticket rates, to be maintained in the Cinema Hall, shall be as prescribed.

#### 10A. Formation of the Film Development Board

(1) Government of Nepal shall, for the development and promotion of motion picture sector, form a Film Development Board representing by the concerned person or institution related with such sector.

(2) The formation procedure and other functions of the Film Development Board to be constituted pursuant to Sub-section (1) shall be as prescribed.

#### 11. Authority of Government of Nepal

(1) Government of Nepal may issued an order to stop the exhibition of any motion picture of it thinks necessary to do so for the security of Nepal or public peace or order.

Provided that, reasonable time shall be given to the concerned person to put his/her defense prior to issuance of such order.
