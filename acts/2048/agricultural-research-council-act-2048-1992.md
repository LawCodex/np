Act number 3 of the year 2048 (1992)

An Act Made to Provide for the Establishment and Management of Nepal Agricultural Research Council

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to establish and manage the Nepal Agricultural Research Council in order to enhance the economic standards of the general public by doing study and research works on the problems of the agricultural sector and finding out solutions to the problems;

Now, therefore, His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev has, on the advice and with the consent of the Council of Ministers, enacted this Act, in accordance with Article 129 of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, 2047 (1990).

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the "Nepal Agricultural Research Council Act, 2048(1992)".

(2) This Act shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act:

(a) "Council" means the Nepal Agricultural Research Council established under Section 3.

(b) "Committee" means the executive committee formed pursuant to Section 7.

(c) "Chairperson" means the Chairperson of the committee.

(d) "Member" means the member of the committee and this term includes the chairperson and member secretary of the committee.

(e) "Executive director" means the Executive Director appointed or designated pursuant to Section 13.  (e1) "National agricultural policy" means the agricultural policy put forward by Government of Nepal in order to implement the long-term agricultural plan set by the National Planning Commission and the agricultural plan included in the Five-Year Plan.

(f) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed or as prescribed in the Bye-law framed under this Act.

#### 3. Establishment of Council

(1) The Nepal Agricultural Research Council is, hereby, established to do, or cause to be done, high-level study and research works on agriculture for the purpose of finding out the problems in the field of agriculture and measures to solve such problems.

(2) The office of the Council shall be situated in the Kathmandu Valley.

#### 15. Submission of report to Government of Nepal

The Council shall submit an annual report of the activities carried out by it to Government of Nepal within Six months after the expiration of every fiscal year.

#### 16. Powers of Government of Nepal to give direction

Government of Nepal may give necessary direction to the Council and it shall be the duty of the Council to observe such direction.

#### 17. Liaison with Government of Nepal

The Council shall liaise with Government of Nepal through the Ministry of Agriculture.

#### 18. Dissolution of Council

(1) Government of Nepal may, for any reasonable reason, dissolve the Council, by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette.

(2) If the Council is dissolved pursuant to Sub-section (1), the powers and obligations of the Council shall devolve on Government of Nepal.

#### 19. Powers to frame Bye-law

The Council may, with the approval of Government of Nepal, frame necessary Bye-laws so as to carry out the objectives of this Act.
