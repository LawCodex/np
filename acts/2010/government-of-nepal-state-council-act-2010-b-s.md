# Government of Nepal State Council Act, 2010

Government of Nepal State Council Act, 2010

Preamble: Whereas it is expedient to constitute a State Council, for we, His Majesty the King is not physically well and we have to go abroad, for the diagnosis of the disease and appropriate treatment, for a while upto we stay in abroad; and not to cause any hindrance in the day to day administration of the country.

Now therefore, be it enacted, on the advice of the cabinet, by His majesty the King Tribhuvan Birbickram Shah Dev.

#### 1. Short title

This Act shall be called "Government of Nepal State Council Act, 2010 B.S." 2. Extent and time period: (1) The Act shall be enforced all over Nepal.

(2) This Act shall come into force immediately and shall be enforced until we return back to the country.

#### 3. Oath

(1) There shall be a State Council consisting of the following members, till we stay outside the territory of Nepal:

(1) Her Majesty First Queen Kanti Rajaya Laxmi Devi.

(2) Her Majesty Youngest Queen Ishwori Rajaya Laxmi Devi.

(3) His Majesty the Crown Prince Mahendra Bir Bickram Shah Dev.

(2) Before taking the responsibility of the State Council, the members thereof shall take an oath as prescribed in the Schedule, before a person we designate for the same.

#### 4. Meetings, quorum and majority decision

(1) The members of the council shall determine the time, date and place of the meetings by themselves, and it shall be followed accordingly. If the time, date or place

of the meeting has to be changed, it may be changed by the meeting of the council.

(2) At least Two members shall have to be presented to meet the quorum of the meeting. The meeting of the council shall not be stopped if Two members are present in a case a Notice for the meeting is received by the members and even could not attend such meeting for any reason.

(3) Majority decision of the members shall prevail in the case of dissenting opinion of the members of the State Council. In the case of a tie, the opinion of a senior member shall prevail.

#### 5. Authentication by the seal

(1) There shall be a separate seal of the State Council and the council shall cause to take into custody such a seal to any of the members of the council.

(2)The decision of the council shall chronologically registered in a register by the officer who is recommended by the cabinet for such task. In case of dissenting opinion, the members and such an officer shall maintain and sign it accordingly. In a case where it is done accordingly, the decision shall be deemed valid (Pucca).

#### 6. Authority

(1) His Majesty has authorized the State Council to perform only the following duties:

(a) To provide Birta and to affix Royal seal on the matters (documents) recommended by the cabinet except otherwise provided by this Act.

(b) To provide approval to the minister or the cabinet regarding the recruitment, transfer, promotion, removal, departmental actions, suspension of a government employee etc. as authorized by the Act, Sawal or Rule, where our approval is a must in the matters of the royal commission of the statutory posts.

(c) Except otherwise provided in Sub-sections (a) and (b), to provide consent or approval in those matter which are decided by the Minister or cabinet to run the government business pursuant to the prevailing Act, Sawal and Rules where our formal approval is a must.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in Sub-section (1), the State Council has not been authorized to perform the following tasks:

(a) To dissolve the cabinet,

(b) To appoint new Minister or to remove any sitting Minister without the approval or recommendation of the Prime Minister,

(c) To decide anything against the will (decision) of the cabinet.

(3) Except otherwise provided in the foregoing Sections, all other state powers shall remain on us (His Majesty) and in case there is any difference of opinion between the State Council and the cabinet, such matters shall be presented before us and it shall be followed as we decide.

(4) In our absence the Royal Seal shall be taken in to custody by Her Majesty the First queen.

#### 7. Power of His Majesty the King to dissolve or alter

(1) The power to dissolve the State Council constituted by us pursuant to this Act or the power to alter the member, decrease or increase the number of its members shall remain on us.

(2) Any order which we forward from the foreign country, shall be recognized when it shall be in a format along with this Act and a Special Seal, affixed on the side of this Section is put and our signature is there in the document.

Sunday, Aswin 4, 2010 B.S.
