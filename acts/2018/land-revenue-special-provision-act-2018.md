Act No. 15 of the year 2018 (1961)

An Act Made To Provide for Special Provision relating to Land Revenue

Preamble : Whereas, it is expedient to make special provisions to extend the time limit for payment of the land revenue by landholder Jimidar and to auction off the land of that landholder and Jimidari of that Jimidar who do not pay the land revenue within the extended time-limit;

Now, therefore, His Majesty's the King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev has enacted this Act, pursuant to Article 93 of the Constitution of Nepal.*

#### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called as the "Land Revenue (Special Provision) Act, 2018 (1961)".

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act, -

(a) "land revenue" means cash or in-kind required to be paid to His Majesty's Government pursuant to the Nepal law in respect of the land of any type whatsoever.

(b) "land" means any land subject to the land revenue.

(c) "Jimidari" includes the Jirayat (taxable) land held by the Jimidar.

(d) "Raiti (landholder) in respect of any land" means the Raikar landholder (Raitan Nambariwala) who pays the land revenue in respect of that land and includes a person who is legally entitled to possess that land as a Raikar landholder (Nambariwala) who pays the land revenue in respect of that land.

(e) "Revenue office" means the local Revenue office and includes the local Guthi (trust) offices.

%(f) Jimmawali or Talukdari Mukheli" includes the paddy land (khet land) being used instead of the Khangi (income from Jagir land allotment) entitled to be used for performing Jimmawali or Talukdari Mukheli.
