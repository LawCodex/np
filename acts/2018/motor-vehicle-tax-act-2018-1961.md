Act Number 11 of the year 2018 (1961)

An Act made to Provide for Recovering of Transport Tax

Preamble : Whereas, it is expedient to manage modus operandi of levying and recovering transport tax in order to maintain economic interest of the general people;1

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev has, pursuant to Article 93 of the Constitution of Nepal.

#### 1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called as "Motor Vehicle Tax Act, 2018 (1961)".

(2) It shall extend throughout the Kingdom of Nepal.

#### 2. Definition

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act,-

(a) "Transport Tax" means the transport tax levied under this Act.

(b) "Motor Vehicle" includes a jeep, car, station wagon, passenger bus, lorry and truck.

(e) "Cart" includes a cart pulled by ox, he-buffalo, she-buffalo or horse.

(d) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed in the Rules framed under this Act.

#### 3. To levy transport tax

After the commencement of this Act, His Majesty's Government may, from time to time, upon a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette, levy transport tax at such rate and to such public route as may be specified in that Notification.

#### 4. Power to realize transport tax and divide public route

(1) The transport tax shall be realized in accordance with the provisions made hereinafter from those who operate a motor vehicle, cart on the public routes specified in the notification published under Section 3.

(2) His Majesty's Government may, for the purposes of this Act, divide each public route subject to the transport tax into various alignments.

#### 5. Payment of transport tax

(1) The transport tax has to be paid to such authority or office authorized to realize such tax, by His Majesty's Government upon Notification published in the Nepal Gazette.

<sup>2</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np (2) The transport tax has to be paid every time of plying a motor vehicle or cart on the route specified under this Act.

#### 6. No transport tax to be levied

No transport tax has to be levied for plying a motor vehicle or cart within only one alignment in exercise of the powers conferred by this Act.

#### 7. Exemption from transport tax

(1) His Majesty's Government may exempt any motor vehicle or cart plying on any route for the purposes of any public work or any benevolent or public interest or public construction work from the transport tax.

(2) His Majesty's Government may, upon Notification in the Nepal Gazette, exempt a motor vehicle or cart belonging to His Majesty's Government or used in the business of His Majesty's Government or used in the course of any project in the Kingdom of Nepal upon agreement with His Majesty's Government from the transport tax wholly or partly.

Provided that, no exemption referred to in Sub-section (1) or (2) shall be granted in respect of a motor or cart to be used for business/ trade or profit.

#### 8. Power to hold motor vehicle or car until payment of tax

In cases where provision has been made that the transport tax has to be paid while plying a motor vehicle or car on any route no motor vehicle or cart belong to any person shall be allowed to be plied on that route until the payable transport tax is paid.

#### 9. Power to withhold and auction of motor vehicle or cart

(1) The competent authority or office may, in order to realize the transport tax payable by any person for plying a motor vehicle or cart on a route subject to the transport tax, withhold the motor vehicle or cart

<sup>3</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np belonging to that person and so plied on that route until such transport tax or fine imposed under this Act is paid up.

(2) If any person fails to pay the transport tax or fine payable by or imposed on him/her and get the withhold motor vehicle or cart released even within Thirty Five days from the date of its withholding under Sub-section (1), the authority or office realizing such tax may auction such vehicle or cart and realize the payable transport tax or fine, and so debit any over plus amount into the governmental deposit that such over plus amount shall be returned to such person as and when such person comes to claim that amount.

Provided that -

(1) In cases such person comes to pay the transport tax or fine payable by or imposed on such person and get back own motor vehicle or cart prior to the completion of auction sale, the vehicle or cart shall not be auctioned and shall be returned to such person having realized levied amount whatever is payable by such person.

(2) In cases where such person does not claim such over plus amount debited into the deposit even within one year from the date of debit, it shall be forfeited and debited into the income of His Majesty's Government.

#### 10. Punishment

(1) In cases where provision has been made that the transport tax is payable while plying a mother vehicle or cart on any route, and where any person is held to have played a motor vehicle or cart on such route clandestinely, a fine of upto Twenty Five Rupees for each cart and that of up to Fifty Rupees for each motor vehicles shall be

<sup>4</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np imposed on such person by the order of the competent authority or Chief of the Office entitled to realize such tax.

(2) In cases where provision has been made that the transport tax is payable while plying a motor vehicle or cart on any route, if any person is proved to have forcefully plied a motor vehicle or cart despite prohibition or plied such motor vehicle or cart withheld without paying the transport tax, a fine of upto Five Hundred Rupees for plying each motor vehicle and upto One Hundred Rupees for plying each cart shall be imposed on such person with the order of the competent authority or Chief of the Office entitled to realize such tax.

#### 11. Recovery of tax or fine from other assets

If any person does not pay the transport tax or fine payable by or imposed on such person and even the motor vehicle or cart belonging to such person could not be withheld, the competent authority or Chief of the Office entitled to realize such tax shall be entitled to take possession of other necessary assets of the person plying such motor or cart or of the owner of the motor vehicle or cart who has caused it to be plied, and to realize such tax or fine by auctioning such assets.

#### 12. Powers and duties of police

(1) Each police on duty on a route, subject to the transport tax, may check as to whether the transport tax payable on a motor vehicle or cart being plied on such route has been paid or not.

(2) It shall be the duty of each of such police personnel to arrest the driver who does not produce on slip of payment of the transport tax or a slip against payment of such tax or does not show any reasonable cause while doing check under Sub-section (1) and motor vehicle or cart and hand over the same to the nearby competent authority or Chief of the Office entitled to realize such tax.

#### 13. Appeal

2(1) An appeal may be made to the Revenue Tribunal against the holding up of a motor vehicle or cart under Section 9 or against an order imposing a fine under Section 10.

(2) If an appeal is made under Sub-section (1), the appeal hearing court may withhold the auctioning of a motor vehicle or cart, if any held up, pending the settlement of the appeal.

#### 14. Power to Frame Rules

His Majesty's Government may frame Rules in order to carry out the objectives of this Act.
