Act No. 41 of the year 2018 (1961)

An Act made to Provide for Recovery of Air Flight Tax

Preamble : Whereas, the Finance Act, 2018 (1961) has provided for levying tax on air flight and it is expedient to manage modus operandi of collecting and recovering such tax;

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev under Article 96 of the Constitution of Nepal.1

#### 1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called as "The Air Flight Tax Act, 2018 (1961)".

(2) This Act shall extend throughout the Kingdom of Nepal.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act,-

(a) "Passenger" means a person who has crossed the age of Twelve years and makes air flight ……………2 from any airport in the Kingdom of Nepal.

(b) "Air Corporation" means and includes a corporation, company, agent, firm or person who takes responsibility of 3flying

passengers by aircraft from an airport in the Kingdom of Nepal and carries on an air transport business.

(c) "Owner" in respect of any air corporation means a person or persons having final control over the business of that corporation and this expresion includes a manager or managing agent, if any, appointed to run such corporation.

(d) "Tax" means the air flight tax levied on passengers.

(e) "Tax Officer" means any Tax Officer appointed by His Majesty's Government for the purposes of this Act.

#### 3. Recovery of Tax

If any Act provides for levying the air flight tax for years or for any One year, such tax shall be collected and recovered in accordance with this Act as well as the Rules or order framed or issued hereunder.

#### 7. Reward to the informer

A person who gives information about the commission of any act by any one with an intention of concealing the tax chargeable under this Act shall, after that matter has been held to be true, be provided with a sum of Twenty Five per cent of the figure recovered from the offender, as a reward, by an order issued by the Director of the Department of Tax of His Majesty's Government. The name of the person giving such information shall be kept secret.
