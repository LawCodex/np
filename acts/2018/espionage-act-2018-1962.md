#### 1. Short title, extension and commencement

(1) This Act may be called "Espionage Act, 2018 (1962)".

(2) This Act shall extend to the whole of Nepal and also apply to all Nepalese citizens wherever they reside.

(3) This Act shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definition

Unless the subject or context otherwise provides, in this Act,-

(a) "Military Situation" means the military force of Nepal and its training or movement.

(b) "Strategic System" means an arsenal of armies, a record of arms and ammunition of armies, a strategically important target and a map, model, sketch, military code, password of such area and this term also includes the deployment or mobilization of army.

(c) "System of Internal Security" means the strength of police force of Nepal, its training, a record of arms and ammunition of such police force, police code and the deployment and also includes deployment and mobilization of police force.

(d) "Device" means a device which is used for writing, listening and speaking and this term also includes a device which takes photograph.

(e) "Information" means an act to transmit a message through any type of document or oral way, or a code or any other device.

(f) "Notified Order" means an order published in Nepal Gazette.

#### 3. Espionage

(1) It shall be deemed to have committed the offence of espionage by a person when he/she, with an intention to make an adverse effect on the interest or security of Nepal within or outside its territory; or for the interest of any foreign state by receiving ,or not, any type of enticement from such foreign state, gives a information about the military situation or strategic system or the system of internal security to anyone or he/she attempts or abets to provide such information, or collects the materials with an object to provide such information ,or creates conducive environment for the person who is trying to obtain such information, or attempts towards making such environment.

(2) It shall be deemed to have committed the offence of espionage by a person when he/she, with an intention to make an adverse effect upon the interest or security of Nepal within or outside its territory, or for the interest of any foreign country by receiving, or not, any type of enticement from such foreign country, gives a information related to any secret diplomatic document of the government of Nepal or any other secret governmental document to any one ,or attempts or abets to provide such information.

#### 4. Power to search and arrest

(1) The Government of Nepal may, on the basis of reliable suspicion that a person has committed an offence of espionage and he/she has a note, plan, model or sketch related to military situation or strategic system or the system of internal security, or a written note related to a secret governmental document, issue an warrant in order to

arrest him/her or search his/her building or land (residence) by entering at any time, or search his body or his/her suspected box, bundle, luggage, bags and baggage or any other goods; and seize the note, plan, model, sketch or any written note related to a secret governmental document, or any other equipment or goods related to espionage which is obtained in the course of search.

Provided that, if it takes some time to receive the warrant of search and arrest and there is, a possibility of absconding of an accused from the scene or, the possibility of destruction or concealment of the evidence of an offence, the Government of Nepal may, upon preparing a written order of the said matter, arrest such person or search his/her building or land by entering at any time or search his/her body or his/her suspected box, bundle, luggage, bags and baggage or any other goods ,and seize the note, plan, model, sketch or any written note related to a secret governmental document or any other equipment or goods related to espionage which is obtained in the course of search.

(2) If a person, who has committed the offence of espionage, tries to escape from the scene through any vehicle, the Government of Nepal may stop such vehicle and bring it at prescribed place.

#### 5. One who knows the commission of the offence of espionage shall inform thereof

A person who knows to have committed the offence of espionage shall inform thereof nearby police station.

3 6. Penalty : (1) A person who commits an offence of espionage mentioned in Sub-section (1) of Section 3, shall be liable to be punished a term from Five years to Life imprisonment (Janmavar).

(2) A person who commits an offence of espionage mentioned in Sub-section (2) of Section 3 shall be liable to be punished a term from Five years to Ten years imprisonment.

(3) A person who knows the commission of an offence of espionage and does not inform thereof pursuant to Section 5 shall be liable to be punished a term not exceeding Four years or with a fine upto Twenty Thousand Rupees or with both.

(4) If a person, against whom the sentence of an imprisonment or a fine has been imposed and who is not a citizen of Nepal, Government of Nepal may deport him/her from Nepal after completion of his/her Sentence.

#### 10. Jurisdiction of Court

No case under this Act shall be tried in any other court except a court authorized by Government of Nepal to try the case and an appeal against the verdict made by such court shall lie to the 5 Court of Appeal.

#### 11. Power to make Rules

Government of Nepal may make Rules in order to implement the objectives of this Act.

#### 12. No obstacle in the power to prosecute a case under other law

If any other law provides for a provision of more Sentence to the offender who is punishable under this Act, this Act shall not be a bar to try the case and to impose such more Sentence against him/her under that Act.

Provided that, no person shall be punished for the same offence more than once.
