#### 4. The Board to be Autonomous and Corporate Body

(1) The Board shall be an autonomous and Corporate Body having perpetual succession.

(2) The Board shall have its own seal to carry out its business.

(3) The Board may, as an individual, accept, use, sell or otherwise manage movable or immovable property.

(4) The Board may, as an individual, sue or be sued by its name.

#### 5. Functions, Duties and Powers of the Board

(1) The functions, duties and powers of the Board shall be as follows:-

(a) To provide advice to His Majesty's Government for formulating necessary policy to develop the Stock Exchange by protecting and promoting the interests of the investors.

(b) To make necessary arrangements to systematize and regularise the Transaction of Securities.

(c) To provide license to the Corporate Bodies to operate the Stock Exchange.

(d) To register the Securities issued by the Corporate Bodies. <sup>12</sup>(d1) To issue the certificate to carry out the Transaction of Securities of Securities Entrepreneur to the

#### 7D. License not to be Issued

Notwithstanding anything contained in Section 7C, no license for the operation of the Stock Exchange shall be issued to any Corporate Body under the following circumstances, if :-

(a) It is not necessary to operate the Stock Exchange on the basis of development of industries and enterprises as well as existing transaction.

(b) It is not appropriate to operate the Stock Exchange for the welfare and in the interest of the investors.

(c) The paid-up capital of the concerned Corporate Body is less than <sup>19</sup>Ten Million Rupees.

#### 7E. License may be Suspended or Cancelled

(1) The Board may cancel or suspend for a specified period of time, the license of any Stock Exchange under the following circumstances, if:-

(a) Fails to conduct business within Six months from the date of receipt of the license.

(b) Fails to obey the terms and conditions as required pursuant to Sub-section (2) of Section 7B.

(c) Acts in contravention of this Act or the Rules made thereunder.

#### 30. Prohibition to Take Advantage on the Basis of Insider Information

No body who is directly related to the Corporate Body, the Stock Exchange or the Transaction of Securities shall take advantages from the Transaction of Securities on the basis of non-public information which may directly or indirectly, affect the price of the concerned Securities and nor be shall avail such message or information to others.

No provision of this Act shall be applicable in respect of the Listing of the Securities issued by His Majesty's Government under Public Debt Act, 2017 (1960) or other Government Bonds.
