# Chaar Bhangayang1 Special Court Act, 2012

Chaar Bhangayang1 Special Court Act, 2012

Act Number 6 of the Year 2012 B.S.

Whereas it is expedient to control Dacoity and theft within the Chaar Bhangayang and to penalize offenders by deciding such cases in a speedy manner and to provide relief for the public from fear,

Now therefore, be it enacted, by His Majesty the king.

#### 1. Short title and extent

(a) This Act shall be called the "Chaar Bhangayang Special Court Act , 2012.

(b) This Act shall come into force within Nepal Sadar Chaar Bhangayang .

(c) This Act shall be enforced for a period of One year.

#### 2. Investigation of the crimes

(a) The following persons shall conduct investigation on the cases of homicide, Dacoity, theft, burglary (Nakabajani) Jabarjasti Chori and Rahajani.

(b) A person who is deputed for investigation may, whenever situation so demands, and with the approval of the Magistrate, take the body or house search of the person who is an alleged (Poliyaka) or a suspect of the crime; such an investigation shall be done in front of a person who at least holds a position of an Inspector in case of the police and by Dittha or Kharidar from the Magistrate Office.

(1) Police Inspector General or Police Inspector or any officers above the rank of Inspector.

(2) One officer deputed by the Magistrate where the offence was taken place.

> 1 Kathmandu Valley

#### 3. Composition and Authority of the special court

(1) A special court shall be constituted upon comprising of the commissioner working within the Chaar Bhangayang, Magistrate deputed by the Government and chief of the Appeal Court (Adda) designated by the Government on the recommendation of the Chief Justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya. The commissioner shall be the Chairperson of this court.

(2) To decide on the cases as referred to in Section 2 shall be the jurisdiction of the Special Court.

(3) The Special Court has been authorized to issue Summons or warrant or to record statement of the defender or a witness or to issue an order to produce any document or any material evidence to decide a case.

(4) In the course of deciding any case relating to the crime as referred to in Section 2 , the Special Court shall follow the Act or Rules which is followed by the other Amini, court or criminal court located in Katmandu, the capital of the country.

(5) A Bench of Two members of the Special Court may decide a case, in case One of the member of this court could not attend office due to any reason.

(6) In case there is a dissenting opinion in the Bench in deciding a case as referred to in Section 5, the case shall be re-discussed in the Bench of Three members and the majority decision shall prevail in such situation.

(7) Notwithstanding anything contained hereinabove, if the Special Court finds it appropriate to put an accused into custody till the decision is made by the court, the court may put him/her into the custody. Majority opinion shall also prevail in case of Thun chhek.

#### 4. Appeal

The party to the case who is not satisfied with the order, decision or penalty made by the Special Court may file an appeal before the Pradhan Nayalaya. The Government may also exercise this power.

#### 5. Running case file may be withdrawn by the Government

The Government may handover any running case file of a case as referred to in Section 2 hereinabove from the court of Sadar Bhangayang and handover it to the Special Court.

#### 6. Power of the Government to frame Rules

The Government may frame Rules to implement the objectives of this Act.

#### 7. Power to Act in violation of prevailing Act or Sawal

This Act shall prevail in the matters as referred to in this Act and in other matters it shall be done as prescribed in the prevailing Act, Sawal and Sanad.
