Act No. 19 of the year 2017 (2074) An Act made to make arrangements relating to National Indigenous Nationalities Commission

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to make necessary arrangements relating to National Indigenous Nationalities Commission for the identification of the glorious history and culture of Indigenous nationalities, protection and promotion of their rights and interests and the empowerment of the communities, Now, therefore, be it enacted by the Federal Parliament pursuant to Article 296 sub-article (1) of the Constitution of Nepal.

### Chapter-1

Preliminary

#### 1. Short title and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called " National Indigenous Nationalities Commission Act, 2017 (2074)."

(2) This act shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act, -

(a) "Chairperson" means the Chairperson of the Commission and the word also includes the Acting Chairperson.  (b) "Indigenous Nationalities" means the race or community as defined by the Government of Nepal on the basis of the recommendation made by the Commission.

(c) "Commission" means the National Indigenous Nationalities Commission established under Article 261 of the Constitution.

(d) "Prescribed" or "As prescribed" means laws prescribed or as prescribed under the Rules framed under this Act.

(e) "Member" means the member of the Commission and the word also includes the Chairperson.

(f) "Constitution" means the Constitution of Nepal.

### Chapter-2

Qualifications of the Chairperson and Members and Vacancy of Post

#### 3. Qualifications of the Chairperson and Members

The following persons shall be qualified to be the Chairperson and Member of the Commission: -

(a) Indigenous national having contributed for at least ten years for the protection, promotion of the rights and interests of the indigenous nationalities community, the

(b) holding a bachelor's degree in the relevant subject from a recognized university,

(c) not being a member of any political party at the time of appointment,

(d) having attained the age of forty-five years,

(e) being of high moral character.

#### 4. Vacancy of post

The post of the Chairperson or Member of the Commission shall be vacant in any of the following circumstances: -

(a) if he or she tenders resignation in writing to the President,

(b) if he or she attains the age of sixty-five years,

(c) if the tenure of the post ends pursuant to sub-article (3) of Article 261 of the Constitution,

(d) if a motion of impeachment is passed against him or her under Article 101 of the Constitution,

(e) if he or she is removed from post by the President on recommendation of the Constitutional Council on grounds of his or her inability to hold office and discharge the functions due to physical or mental illness,

(f) if he or she dies.

#### 5. Reappointment shall not be done

The Chairperson and Members appointed shall not reappointed.

Provided that a member may be appointed to the post of Chairperson, and when a member is so appointed as the Chairperson, his or her term of post shall be so computed as to include his or her term as the member.

#### 6. Not to be appointed in other government services

A person appointed as the Chairperson or a member of the Commission shall not be eligible for appointment in other government services.

Provided that, nothing in this clause shall be deemed to be a bar to the appointment to any political position or to any position which requires making investigations, inquiries or findings on any subject, or submitting advice, opinion or recommendation after carrying out a study or research on any subject.

### Chapter-3

Power, Functions and Duties of the Commission

#### 7. Power, Functions and Duties of the Commission

The power, function and duties of the Commission shall be as follows: -

(a) to conduct study on the overall situation of the indigenous nationalities community and to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal for policy, legal and institutional reforms, in that relation,

(b) to formulate national policy and programs for the promotion and protection of the rights and interests of the indigenous nationalities community and the empowerment of such community and to make recommendation to the Government of Nepal for their implementation,

(c) to conduct study and research on whether the provisions and arrangements made for the promotion and protection of the rights and interests of the indigenous nationalities community and their empowerment have been effectively implemented, and to make suggestions to the Government of Nepal for the measures to be taken for their effective implementation,

(d) to make suggestions to the Government of Nepal regarding the reforms in terms of legal, policy and institutional measures, required for the promotion and protection of the rights and interests of the indigenous nationalities community and the empowerment of such community,

(e) to review, monitor and evaluate the implementation of the policy and programs related to indigenous nationalities community,

(f) to monitor or cause to be monitored the implementation of the recommendations made or suggestions given by the Commission,

(g) to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal for the formulation and implementation of special programs for the development and empowerment of economically or socially indigent people within the indigenous nationalities community,

(h) to undertake studies and research into the language, script, culture, history, tradition, literature, art within the

indigenous nationalities communities, and to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal for the protection and development of such language, script, culture, history, tradition, literature, art of such communities,

(i) to conduct awareness generating program for the protection and promotion of the rights and interests of the indigenous nationalities communities and the empowerment of such communities,

(j) to formulate programs to end existing malpractices, superstitions and all forms of exploitation existing within the indigenous nationalities communities and the empowerment of such community and to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal,

(k) to protect and promote the traditional skill, technology and special knowledge existing within the indigenous nationalities communities and to assist in its application for commercial use,

(l) to monitor the implementation of the international treaties, agreements relating to the indigenous nationalities communities that Nepal is a party to, and to make recommendations or suggestions to the Government of Nepal for their implementation,

(m) to collect complaints against the persons or organizations that violate the rights of the indigenous nationalities communities and to make recommendation to the concerned agency to conduct investigation and prosecution over such complaints,

(n) to identify the indigenous nationalities communities by means of detailed studies and research and to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal to make lists of Surnames,

(o) to formulate policy and programs to establish and mobilize living museum for the protection and promotion of the physical, non-physical culture of the indigenous nationalities communities and to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal for their implementation,

(p) to search for and investigate the place of origin of the indigenous nationalities communities and to make recommendations to the Government of Nepal for the protection and promotion of those archaeological and ancient structures, objects and places,

#### 8. May obtain opinion and consultations

The Commission, in relation to its functions and duties, may receive opinions and seek consultations from any constitutional organ, government office or public entity.

#### 9. May obtain service of experts

The Commission may receive services from experts, pursuant to the federal law by stating the reasons thereof, if the human resource employed within the Commission is unable to perform any work that the Commission is required to perform as per the Constitution, this Act and prevailing federal law.

#### 10. May form committee or working group

(1) The Commission may form a committee or working group to perform specific tasks by formulating a procedure.

(2) The terms or scope of operation of the committee or working group formed pursuant to sub-section (1), shall be as prescribed by the Commission at the time of its formation.

(3) While forming the committee or working group pursuant to sub-section (1), there shall be representation based on the principle of proportional representation, as enshrined in the Constitution.

#### 11. Coordination and Cooperation

The Commission may on the basis of necessity, coordinate and cooperate with government agencies or public entities.

### Chapter-4

Operation of the Commission

#### 12. Office of the Commission

(1) A central office of the Commission shall be established within Kathmandu Valley for the operation of work of the Commission.

(2) The Commission may, on the basis of necessity, establish offices in the provincial level.

#### 13. Meeting of the Commission

(1) The meeting of the Commission shall decide the activities to be performed by the Commission in accordance with this Act.

(2) The meeting of the Commission shall be held on such a date, time and venue as prescribed by the Chairperson, based on necessity.

(3) The Secretary of the Commission shall finalize and notify all members of the date, time and venue of the meeting along with a list of the agenda to be discussed in the meeting, at least 48 hours prior to the meeting.

(4) Any member interested to discuss additional issues, not included within the list of agenda set to be discussed within the meeting pursuant to sub-section (3), shall notify the Secretary of the additional issue and reason for its introduction to the meeting 24 hours prior to the meeting. Upon receipt of such information, the Secretary of the Commission shall notify all members and such issue shall be considered to have been included in the list.

(5) Notwithstanding anything stated in sub-sections (3) and (4), an issue not included within the list may be introduced and decided by the Commission, with the agreement of all members present in the meeting.

(6) The quorum of the meeting shall be deemed to have been fulfilled with the presence of more than 50 percent of the immediate members of the Commission.

(7) The meeting of the Commission shall be chaired by the Chairperson of the Commission.

(8) The decision of the majority shall be the decision of the Commission and in case of a tie, the Chairperson shall give a decisive vote.

(9) The Secretary of the Commission shall create a depository of the decision of the Commission and also authenticate it with the signatures of the Chairperson and Members.

14. Not to take part in the decision making in case of conflict of interest:

If any member of the Commission has any interest, concern or benefit arising from any issue under consideration in the Commission or if the near relatives of such persons stand to benefit directly or indirectly, such member should give prior information to the Commission regarding such matters and such member shall not take part in the decision making process of the Commission concerning such matters.

#### 15. Work Division

The division of the work of the Chairperson and Member shall be as fixed by the Commission.

#### 16. Work to be performed as an acting

The senior most Member of the Commission, shall work as the acting Chairperson, if the position of the Chairperson falls vacant due to any reason, is unable to perform work pursuant to sub-article (6) of Article 101 of the constitution, has gone for a foreign visit or is on leave for more than 7 days.

#### 17. Work, performance of the Commission not to be interrupted

The vacancy of posts of the Chairperson and/ or any Member shall not interrupt the works of the Commission.

### Chapter-5

Miscellaneous

#### 18. Organizational Structure and Employees of the Commission

(1) The organizational structure and appointment of employees of the Commission shall be as prescribed by the Government of Nepal.

(2) The Government of Nepal shall make available the employees necessary for the Commission to perform its work.

#### 19. Duties and Conduct of Post

(1) The duties and conduct of the post to be adhered by the Chairperson and Members of the Commission shall be as follows:

(a) to perform the obligations of the post independently, impartially and honestly for the empowerment of the community of indigenous nationality, and to protect and promote the rights, interests of the community of indigenous nationality community

(b) Not to commit any act that would create discord in the relationship between different ethnicities, communities and identities.

(2) The Chairperson and the Members of the Commission shall adhere to the code of conduct as prescribed by this Act or prevailing federal laws.

#### 20. Cooperation to be extended

It shall be the duty of any agency or official with which the Commission seeks assistance, to cooperate with the Commission in course of the performance of its functions.

#### 21. Power to send for implementation in writing

If the Commission finds that any agency or official has has not made available necessary assistance to the Commission, it may write to the higher agency or official above such agency or official for assistance in the matter.

#### 22. Annual report

(1) The Commission shall submit an annual report of the work and activities of the Commission, to the president within three months of the date of expiry of the fiscal year.

(2) The report made pursuant to sub-section (1) shall inter-alia, include the following matters, in addition to other details:

(a) Details of implementation of annual programs of the Commission and expenses incurred,

(b) Details of suggestions, recommendations given by the Commission to Nepal Government or any other agency,

(c) The status of the implementation of the suggestions, recommendations given in accordance to clause (b),

(d) Situation of physical and economic resources of the Commission and their utilization,

(e) Details of other activities performed by the Commission in course of fulfilling the responsibilities required to be performed by it, in accordance to this Act.

#### 23. Liaison with Government of Nepal

The Ministry of Federal Affairs and General Administration shall liaise the Commission with the Government of Nepal.

#### 24. Delegation of Power

The Commission may delegate some authority accorded to it by this Act, as per necessity, with set conditions for its use and adherence, to the Chairperson, Member, Government of Nepal, State Government, official employee of any agency or Local Government.
