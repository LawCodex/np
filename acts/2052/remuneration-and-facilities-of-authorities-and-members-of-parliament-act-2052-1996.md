# Members of Parliament Act, 2052 (1996)

Remuneration and Facilities of Authorities and Members of Parliament Act, 2052 (1996) Date of Authentication and Publication

2052.10.17 (31 Jan. 1996)

Amendment:

#### 1. Republic Strengthening and Some Nepal 2066.10.7.5(21 Jan

2010) Laws Amendment Act, 2066 (2010)1

Act Number 9 of the year 2052 (1996)

An Act Made to Provide for Remuneration and Facilities of Authorities and Members of Parliament

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to make legal provisions on the remuneration and facilities of Office-bearers and Members of Parliament;

Now, therefore, be it enacted by the Parliament in the Twenty-Fourth year of the reign of His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev.

#### 1. Short tile and commencement

(1) This Act may be called as the “Remuneration and Facilities of Authorities and Members of Parliament Act, 2052 (1996)."

(2) This Act shall come into force immediately.

> 1 This Act came into force on 15 Jesta 2065, "Prasasti" and the word "Kingdom" has been deleted. As of now, "Constituent Assembly" acts as the Parliament. This Assembly does not have National Assembly so reference of National Assembly and context thereof does not match the provisions of the Interim Constitution of Nepal, 2063. This Act has not been amended in the line of Interim Constitution till the date- Nepal Law Commission.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act,-

(a) “Speaker" means the Speaker of the House of Representatives.

(b) "Chairperson" means the Chairperson of the National Assembly.

(c) "Leader of Opposition Party" means a person selected as the leader of parliamentary party by the following party:

(1) Out of the parties that have Ten percent or more than Ten percent members in the House of Representatives or National Assembly, except any party that forms or supports the formation of the Council of Ministers, a party that has the largest number of members, or

(2) If two or more parties that have Ten percent or more than Ten percent members in the House of Representatives or National Assembly have equal number of members, any one party recognized by them from amongst themselves on the basis of mutual understanding, or

(3) Failing mutual understanding pursuant to Clause (2), a party that is consented to in writing by the largest number of members in the House of Representatives or National Assembly to select the Leader of Opposition Party, except any party that forms or supports the formation of the Council of Ministers, or

(4) If there is no consent in writing pursuant to Clause (3) or there is no party that have Ten percent or more than Ten percent members in the House of Representatives or National Assembly, except any party that forms or supports the formation of the Council of Ministers, the party designated by

the Speaker or Chairperson for the House of Representatives or National Assembly.

Explanation: Where any person is being the Leader of Opposition Party, and any party, for any reasons, makes Ten percent or more than Ten percent members in the House of Representatives or National Assembly and becomes the party with the largest number of members, the person who is selected by that party as the leader of its parliamentary party shall be the Leader of Opposition Party.

(5) For the purposes of this Clause, the concerned party or member shall give the mutual understanding as referred to in Clause (2) for the first time of the holding of election to the members of the House of Representatives, within Fifteen days after the date of holding the session of the House of Representatives or National Assembly and the written consent as referred to in Clause (3) within fifteen days after the date of failure to reach the mutual understanding.

(d) "Leader of Ruling Party" means any member of the National Assembly, other than a Minister nominated by the Prime Minister to conduct government proceedings in the National Assembly on behalf of the Ruling Party.

(e) "Chief Whip of Ruling Party" means a member of the House of Representatives who is designated by the leader of parliamentary party having majority in the House of Representatives or by the Prime Minister, where there is no party that has such majority, to act as the Chief Whip of Ruling Party in the House of Representatives.

(f) "Deputy Speaker" means the Deputy Speaker in the House of Representatives.

(g) "Vice-Chairperson" means the Vice-Chairperson of the National Assembly.

(h) "Chief Whip of Opposition Party" means a member of the House of Representatives who is designated by the Leader of Opposition Party, to act as the Chief Whip of Opposition Party in the House of Representatives.

(i) "Whip of Ruling Party" means a member of the House of Representatives who is designated by the leader of parliamentary party having majority in the House of Representatives or by the Prime Minister, where there is no party that has such majority, to act as the Whip of Ruling Party in the House of Representatives.

(j) "Chairperson' means a chairperson of any committee of the House of Representatives or National Assembly.

(k) "Member" means a member of the House of Representatives or National Assembly, other than a Minister or Office-bearer.

(l) "Office-bearer" means the Speaker, Chairperson, Leader of Opposition Party, Leader of Ruling Party, Chief Whip of Ruling Party, Deputy Speaker, Vice-Chairperson, Chief Whip of Opposition Party, Whip of Ruling Party and Chairperson.

(m) "Committee" means any committee of the House of Representatives or National Assembly, and this term includes the Joint Committee of both Houses of Parliament.

#### 3. Remuneration

(1) The Office-bearers and Members shall be entitled to a monthly remuneration as specified in Schedule-1.

Provided that, the Office-bearers and Members who are incumbent at the time of commencement of this Act shall be entitled to the remuneration as referred to in this Section only after the date of commencement of this Act.

(2) If any Office-bearer or Member ceases to hold his or her office because of his/her death or resignation from office or dissolution of the House of Representatives prior to the completion of his or her term of office, the Office-bearer or Member shall be entitled to an additional amount equal to his or her remuneration of One month, in addition to the remuneration to which he or she is entitled up to the day on which his or her membership exists.

(3) The remuneration and additional remuneration receivable by the Office-bearers or Members pursuant to Sub-section (2) shall be given to them or their respective nominees, as the case may be. If an Office-bearer or Member dies and he or she has not nominated any person or if the nominee is not alive, such remuneration shall be provided to his or her near heir.

(4) If any Office-bearer or Member ceases to hold his or her office because of lack of qualification, such an Office-Bearer or Member shall not be entitled to the additional amount as referred to in Sub-section (2).

#### 4. Allowance for the Office-bearers and Members

(1) A sum of Two Hundred Rupees shall be provided, in consideration for meeting allowance, to the Office-bearers and Members for each day of attending a meeting of any House or any Committee or the joint meeting of both Houses.

(2) The allowance as referred to in Sub-section (1) shall also be provided for the following period:

(a) During the period of adjournment of the meeting of the current Session of the House,

(b) During the period of adjournment of the meeting of the Committee, if any,

(c) During the period of leave approved out of the leave allowable pursuant to the Rules of the House of Representatives or National Assembly.

(3) An Office-bearer or Member who is entitled to take part in any meeting of any House of Parliament or any Committee shall be provided with an allowance at the rate as referred to in Sub-section (1) for each day for up to Three days before the commencement of the meeting and up to Three days after the closure of the meeting.

Provided that, an Office-bearer or Member who remains absent at the commencement of the meeting of House or Committee shall be entitled to the allowance only for Three days before the day on which the Office-bearer or Member is present.

(4) An Office-bearer and Member shall be provided with double travelling allowance of the travelling allowance to which they are entitled pursuant to Clause (b) of Sub-section (1) of Section 12, while coming to taking part to the Session of Parliament or meeting of Committee from their respective permanent residence and return to their residence after taking part in the Session or meeting.

(5) A lump sum amount equal to the remuneration of One month shall be given once in One Fiscal Year to an Office-bearer or Member to make visit the election constituency where he or she has been elected from or the district of his or her residence and study the state of affairs thereof in the period between Two sessions of any House of Parliament.

#### 5. Facility of newspapers

The Office-bearers and Members shall be entitled to the amount set forth in Schedule-1 to buy newspapers.

Provided that, if any Office-bearer has got any facility relating to newspapers from the Parliament Secretariat, such an Office-bearer shall not be entitled to the facility of newspapers pursuant to this Section.

#### 6. Not being entitled to allowance

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this Act, any Office-bearer or Member shall not be entitled to any kind of allowance receivable pursuant to this Act during the period of suspension if he or she is suspended from the House and during the period of detention or imprisonment if he or she is so detained or imprisoned pursuant to the decision of a court on a criminal accusation.

(2) Where the House and the Committee meet on the same day, the Office-bearer and Member shall be entitled to the allowance of only One day. Separate allowance shall not be provided for the meeting of the House and the Committee.

#### 7. Payment of remuneration and allowance

The Office-bearers and Members shall obtain the remuneration and other allowances receivable pursuant to this Act from the Parliament Secretariat.

Provided that, if any Office-bearer or Member opens an account with any bank and gives a notice in writing to deposit the remuneration and allowance receivable by him or her into that account, the Parliament Secretariat shall deposit the amount into such an account.

#### 8. Passport facility

The Office-bearer or Member shall be provided with a diplomatic passport while travelling abroad in the course of business of Parliament or representing the State of Nepal.

#### 9. Accommodation facility

(1) The Government of Nepal shall make arrangement for governmental buildings for the housing of Office-bearers and Members. Where arrangement of such buildings is not made, the Office-bearers and Members shall be entitled to such a monthly amount as set forth in Schedule-1 for the accommodation facility.

(2) The Government of Nepal shall repair and maintain the governmental buildings arranged by it pursuant to Sub-section (1).

(3) Where amount is provided for the accommodation facility pursuant to Sub-section (1), the Office-bearers shall be provided with the amount as set forth in Schedule-1 to arrange for their private secretariat and guard homes.

(4) The Office-bearers and Members shall be entitled to enjoy the facility provided pursuant to this Section until Fifteen days after the date of completion of their term of office.

(5) If the accommodation facility is not arranged by the Government of Nepal pursuant to Sub-section (1), a Member shall be provided with a lump sum of Fifteen Thousand Rupees for One tenure of Parliament for making arrangement of furniture in the residence.

Provided that, if a Minister or Office-bearer has once obtained the furniture related facility in that capacity, he or she shall not be provided with the furniture facility as referred to in this Sub-section within the same tenure of Parliament.

Explanation: For the purposes of this Sub-section, "Member" includes an Office-bearer.

#### 10. Motor and fuel facility

(1) Every Office-bearer shall be provided with one motor and transportation fuel and mobile as set forth in Schedule-1.

(2) The Office-bearers shall not be required to submit bills and vouchers of fuel and mobile received by them pursuant to Sub-section (1).

(3) The Office-bearers shall be entitled to enjoy the facility receivable pursuant to this Section until Seven days after the date of completion of their respective term of office.

(4) The Government of Nepal shall arrange for the repair and maintenance of the motor vehicles provided to the Office-bearers pursuant to this Section.

(5) A Member who has not been provided with the transport facility shall be provided with Fifty Rupees for each day of his or her taking part in the meeting of any House of Parliament, Committee or the Joint Meeting of both Houses.

#### 11. Electricity, water and telephone facilities

(1) The Government of Nepal shall bear the whole tariff of electricity and water supply installed with the residence of the Speaker and the Chairperson, deposit and other expenses to be incurred in installing Two lines telephone to their residence and the whole of telephone tariff (except fine).

(2) Where governmental buildings are arranged for the Office-bearers and Members other than the Speaker and Chairperson, the Government of Nepal shall bear the whole tariff of electricity and water supply installed with such buildings and deposit and other expenses to be incurred in installing One line telephone with such buildings. For the telephone tariff, tariff chargeable on Four Hundred local calls (except fine) per month and on One hour STD calls (except fine) per month within the State of Nepal shall be provided.

(3) Where governmental buildings cannot be arranged for the Office-bearers and Members other than the Speaker and Chairperson, the

Government of Nepal shall bear the deposit and other expenses to be incurred in installing One line telephone with their residence. For the telephone tariff, tariff chargeable on Four Hundred local calls (except fine) per month and on up to One hour STD calls (except fine) per month within the State of Nepal shall be provided.

(4) For electricity, amount of tariff of Two Hundred units (except fine) per month shall be provided.

(5) Where the residence of an Office-bearer or Member has to be shifted, the Government of Nepal shall bear the installation expenses to be incurred in shifting the telephone line for a maximum of two times in One tenure of Parliament.

(6) Even where an Office-bearer or Member who has got telephone pursuant to this Section ceases to be a member of Parliament for any reason other than disqualification and so wishes, he or she hold the telephone in his or her ownership by paying the deposit furnished for the installation of the telephone line within Six months after the date of cessation of his or her membership.

#### 12. Daily and travelling allowance

(1) The Office-bearers or Members shall be entitled to the following daily allowance and travelling allowance on tour within Nepal in the course of business of Parliament:

(a) Daily allowance: Amount as set forth in Schedule-1.

(b) Travelling allowance:

(1) In travelling on foot, amount as set forth in Schedule-1.

(2) In travelling by motor vehicle, fuel expenses as per bills.

Provided that prior to making travel by motor vehicle, any Office-bearer or Member, other than the Speaker and Chairperson, has to obtain permission in writing to make such travelling.

(3) In travelling by rail, air conditioned ticket expenses, where such air conditioned ticket is available, and failing it, first class ticket expenses chargeable for One person, and worth reserve, if any, made.

(4) In travelling by air, ticket expenses chargeable for One person.

(5) In travelling by other motor vehicle, expenses as per bills chargeable for One person.

(2) In travelling outside Nepal in order to participate in a ceremony, event, conference etc. on behalf of the Government of Nepal or in the course of business of Parliament, the Office-bearers, Members and other persons included in the travelling team shall be entitled to the following daily allowance and travelling allowance:

(a) Daily allowance: (1) Foreign currency as set forth in Schedule-2.

Provided that, while making a formal visit by the Office-bearers, Members and other persons included in the travelling team to any place outside Nepal as a guest of a foreign country, they shall be entitled to the daily allowance at the rate of half the daily allowance receivable pursuant to this Clause until they stay in such a foreign country.

(b) Travelling allowance:

(1) In travelling by motor vehicle, motor fare as per the bills or tickets.

(2) In travelling by rail, air conditioned ticket expenses, where such air conditioned ticket is available, and failing it, first class ticket expenses chargeable for One person, and worth reserve, if any, made.

(3) In travelling by air, first class ticket expenses chargeable for One person, to the Speaker or Chairperson, and economic class ticket expenses chargeable for One person, to the other Office-bearers or Members or other persons included in the travelling team.

(4) In travelling by sea, amount as per the bills or tickets, chargeable for One person.

(3) The Office-bearers and Members shall be provided with an amount required to make travelling insurance of Three Hundred Thousand Rupees, with the amount chargeable for airport tax, with a lump sum of One Hundred Rupees for every one way travel while travelling within Nepal and with a lump sum of Five Hundred Rupees for every one way travel while travelling outside Nepal.

#### 13. Leader allowance

While travelling abroad as the leader of delegation, an Office-bearer or Member shall be entitled to the leader allowance in an additional amount of Fifty percent of the daily allowance to which he or she is entitled.

#### 14. Provision of personal secretariat and employee

(1) The Office-bearers and Members shall be entitled to the employee facility as set forth in Schedule-3 for their personal secretariat during their tenure.

(2) Required furniture and other goods as set forth in Schedule-3 shall be provided to the Office-bearers for their tenure. The Office-bearers shall return the furniture and goods so provided after the expiration of their tenure.

(3) The Office-bearers and Members shall be provided with the amount as set forth in Schedule-3 for office expenses of the personal secretariat and guest reception.

#### 15. Security arrangement

The Government of Nepal shall arrange for security personnel as set forth in Schedule-3 for the security of Office-bearers.

#### 16. Provision of treatment

(1) For the treatment of the Office-bearers and Members, free service of senior doctors shall be made available in the Parliament Secretariat.

(2) The Parliament Secretariat shall bear the expenses as per the bills, incurred in undergoing treatment at a hospital by any Office-bearer or Member who has been recommended by the doctor of Parliament Secretariat for treatment at the hospital.

Provided that, where it is required to admit to a hospital in emergency or to admit to a hospital while outside the valley, the recommendation of such a doctor shall not be required. The information of such admission to hospital has to be given to the Parliament Secretariat by fastest means.

#### 17. Office facility

(1) The Office-bearers shall be provided with office space in the Parliament Secretariat to establish their respective offices.

(2) The office spaces as referred to in Sub-section (1) shall be provided with required furniture, typewriters and telephone facility.

#### 18. Entitlement to any One facility in the event of permanent residence and election constituency being different

If the area of his or her permanent residence and the constituency where he or she has been elected from are different, an Office-bearer or Member shall be entitled to the travelling allowance receivable by him or her pursuant to Sub-section (4) of Section 4 only while coming from and going to any one place out of these two places.

#### 19. Requirement to submit bills, vouchers etc

(1) For the payment of travelling allowances, bills and vouchers of expenses of air, rail and bus ticket fare while coming to attend the Session for the first time and evidence certifying the Kosh travelled where travel has been made on foot have to be submitted along with the travelling bills.

(2) Amount equal to the daily and travelling allowing bill accompanied by once certified bills and vouchers pursuant to Sub-section (1) may be provided in lump sum by the Parliament Secretariat to the concerned Office-bearers and Members for the daily and travelling allowance for coming to and going from the meeting of the House or Committee, from the second time onwards.

#### 20. Travelling allowance not to be received in duplicate

(1) Where a notice is published that the meeting of House is to commence while the meeting of Committee is going on or that meeting of Committee is to commence while the meeting of House is going on and the interval between the date of commencement of the meeting of the House or Committee according to that notice and the date of adjournment of the meeting of the ongoing House or Committee is not more than Seven days, the Office-

bearers and Members who are taking part in the on-going meeting shall be entitled to the facility of travelling allowance as referred to in this Act after the adjournment of the meeting of the House or Committee that has commenced subsequently.

(2) If any Office-bearer is nominated or elected to Two offices, he or she shall be entitled to the facilities of One office only.

#### 21. Facilities receivable by chairperson of ad hoc committee and coordinator of sub-committee and members thereof

(1) If any committee of ad hoc nature is formed in the House of Representatives or National Assembly for any specific purpose, the chairperson and members of such a committee and the coordinator and members of a sub-committee formed under that committee shall be entitled to the meeting allowance as referred to in Sub-section (1) of Section 4.

(2) The chairperson of the committee formed pursuant to Sub-section (1) shall be provided with One motor vehicle with a driver and One Hundred Fifty liter fuel per month, and the other members of the committee and the coordinator and members of a sub-committee shall be provided with Fifty Rupees per day for the transportation facility pursuant to Sub-section (5) of Section 10.

#### 22. Special facility to the blind and disabled Office-bearers or Members

(1) If the Speaker or Chairperson thinks that any Office-bearer or Member who, being blind or physically disabled, needs the service of attendant to make movement, the facility as referred to in Sub-section (2) shall be provided to one attendant who accompanies such an Office-bearer or Member.

(2) The following facility shall be provided to the attendants accompanying the Office-bearers or Members pursuant to Sub-section (1):

(a) Daily allowance: Half of the daily allowance receivable by the Office-bearers or Members.

(b) Travelling allowance: Facility equivalent to that receivable by the Office-bearers or Members while travelling.

(c) Cloth allowance: Four Thousand Rupees.

#### 23. Advance

(1) If any Office-bearer or Member requests for payment in advance of the remuneration and housing facility amount to which he or she is entitled pursuant to this Act, he or she may get an advance of a maximum of Three months on the condition of making settlement within the same Fiscal Year.

Provided that,

(1) If it is held that any Office-bearer or Member who suffers from a severe disease has to undergo treatment at a foreign hospital or there appears any reasonable ground that similar special circumstance has arisen, the Parliament Secretariat may, with the approval of the Speaker or Chairperson, make payment of a maximum of One year's remuneration in advance to such an Office-bearer or Member.

(2) Notwithstanding contained in Clause (1), if such advance payment cannot be made out of the sanctioned budget or if his or her tenure is to expire prior to Three months, advance may not be made or may be made only for lesser period, as the case may be.

(2) Settlement of advance shall be made by deducting the whole amount of monthly remuneration or accommodation facility to which the

Office-bearer or Member is entitled until the amount of advance made pursuant to Sub-section (1) is settled.

(3) If an Office-bearer or Member who has received advance pursuant to this Section ceases to hold the office of Member of Parliament for any reason, the amount due and recoverable from him or her shall be recovered in accordance with the laws in force.

Provided that, if an Office-bearer who has received an advance of remuneration dies prior to the settlement of advance, the advance of remuneration due and recoverable from him or her shall be remitted.

#### 24. Financial assistance

(1) If any Office-bearer or Member dies prior to expiration of his or her tenure, the Government of Nepal shall provide the financial assistance of Two Hundred Thousand Rupees to his or her families.

(2) If any Office-bearer or Member is physically or mentally incompetent for any reason and is thus unable to continue to hold the office of Member of Parliament, a financial assistance in a lump sum of Fifty Thousand Rupees shall be provided to such an Office-bearer or Member for such incompetency/disability.

#### 25. Special allowance

(1) The persons who have been elected or nominated to the House of Representatives or National Assembly pursuant to the Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, 1990 and completed the following tenure or term of office in the House of Representatives or National Assembly shall be provided with the following special allowance:

(a) Fifty percent of the then monthly remuneration receivable by them for each month, for the Members of Parliament who have completed one tenure or term of office.

(b) Sixty percent of the then monthly remuneration receivable by them for each month, for the Members of Parliament who have completed Two tenures or terms of office.

(c) Seventy Five percent of the then monthly remuneration receivable by them for each month, for the Members of Parliament who have completed Three tenures or terms of office or more than that.

Explanation: For the purposes of this Sub-section, "one tenure" means the period of the House of Representatives despite that the House of Representatives is dissolved prior to the completion of its tenure, pursuant to the Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, 1990.

(2) The allowance as referred to in Sub-section (1) shall not be provided in the following circumstance:

(a) Until a person, who is entitled to the allowance and re-elected or re-nominated to the House of Representatives or National Assembly, holds that office,

(b) If a person who is entitled to the allowance is receiving pension in accordance with the laws in force, he or she has to choose any one out of such pension or the allowance as referred to in this Section.

#### 26. Power to alter Schedules

The Government of Nepal may, by publishing a notice in the Nepal Gazette, alter the Schedules in a manner not to be to the disadvantage of the Office-bearers or Members.

#### 27. Repeal

Remuneration and Facilities of Some Office-bearers of Parliament Act, 2048 and the Regulation Relating to Remuneration and Facilities of Office-bearers and Members of Parliament, 2048 are, hereby, repealed.
