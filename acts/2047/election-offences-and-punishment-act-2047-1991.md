Act No. 21 of the Year 2047 (1991)

An Act Made to Provide for the Electoral Offences and Punishment.

Preamble : Whereas, it is expedient to make provisions for electoral offences and punishment;

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev, on the advice and with the consent of the Council of Ministers, pursuant to Article 129 of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, 2047 (1990).

### Chapter - 1

Preliminary 1. Short Title and Commencement: (1) This Act may be called as "the Election (Offences and Punishment) Act, 2047 (1991)".

(2) This Act shall come into force immediately.

#### 4. Prohibition on Undue Influence

No person acting on behalf of any political organization or party or Candidate or his agent or other person shall, in the course of Election, unduly influence or abet or even attempt to unduly influence, any Candidate or Voter or any person with whom any of them has concern or interest in the Election by committing any of the following acts:

(a) Causing any kind of damage,

(b) Threatening to boycott socially,

(v) Intimidating into being an object of natural calamity of any kind or sin,

(d) Causing to swear or promise, or

(e) Showing any kind of boast, threat or coercion or intimidation.

#### 5. Prohibition on Damaging of Character

No person acting on behalf of any political organization or party or Candidate or his/her agent or other person shall, with an intention of prejudicing the results of Election, damage, in any manner, the character of any Candidate or his/her family member by saying any matter which he/she knows or believes to be false in relation to the character or conduct of the Candidate or his/her family member or by making false accusation of any matter in a manner likely to make others believe such matter to be true.

#### 6. Prohibition on Propagation

No person acting on behalf of any political organization or party or Candidate or his agent or person acting on his/her behalf shall, while propagating or causing to be propagated, the policies and programmes of the Candidate in the course of Election, propagate or cause to be propagated with any of the following intentions:

(a) Undermining the independency, sovereignty or territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Nepal,

(b) Promoting feeling of enmity or hatred on the basis of any religion, community, caste, tribe, language or territorial region, or

(c) Voting or causing to vote, on the basis of any religion, community, caste, tribe, language or territorial region.

#### 7. Prohibition on Exchange of Cash or Kind

No person acting on behalf of any political organization or party or Candidate or his agent or other person shall, within the Period of Election, give or agree to give any Voter cash or kind as a present, reward, gratification, donation or gift to exercise or refrain from exercising his Right to Vote or for exercising or refraining from exercising his Right to Vote; and even the Voter shall not receive or agree to receive such cash or kind for himself or any other person for that purpose.

#### 8. Employee Not To Cause Influence

No Returning Officer, Polling Officer or other officer or employee or security police deputed at the Polling Station or observer deputed by the Commission or other official or employee shall, in the course performing any act of Election, perform or cause to be performed any type of act, except the act of giving his vote for any Candidate pursuant to law, with an intention of making any Candidate successful or unsuccessful in the Election.

#### 9. Prohibition on Disturbing Peace

No person shall, from Three hours prior to the commencement of the polling to the completion of the polling on the day for polling for any Election, disturb or cause to disturb the peace by doing any of the following acts in the house, building or place where the Polling Station is located or in any private or public house, building or land in the neighbourhood thereof so as to cause obstruction to the act of polling or to the Voters or the persons or employees engaged in the act of polling at the Polling Station:

(a) Using loudspeakers, megaphones or similar other devices, or

(b) Playing musical instruments, singing and dancing, holding assembly or function of any kind whatsoever, making or causing to make commotion or manhandling or shouting.

#### 10. Prohibition on Going Armed or Using Arms or Exploding

No person other than the government employee deputed in the act of security shall go armed with arms, poisonous or explosive substances or explode or use them at the Polling Station or place for counting of votes or in neighbourhood thereof.

#### 12. Prohibition on the Receiving or Giving of Ballot Paper Illegally

(1) No person shall, acquire or cause to be acquired or attempt to acquire or cause to be acquired any Ballot Paper to be used in any Election or abet to commit or cause to be committed such act, for the purpose of casting votes in the same Election illegally, by giving or agreeing to give cash, kind, service or any kind of gratification to anyone or by coercing, intimidating or threatening or by using or not using any kind of force.

#### 13. Restriction on Election Propagation

(1) The propagating materials including posters, pamphlets, bills, advertisements etc. prepared for the purpose of propagation relating to the Election shall have to bear the name and address of the printer and the political organization or party or person getting them printed.

(2) No person shall affix, or cause to be affixed, any posters, pamphlets, bills, advertisements or such papers to, or write, or cause to be written on, any religious, archaeological or historical buildings, monuments or structures for propagation relating to the Election.

(3) No person shall affix, or cause to be affixed, any posters, pamphlets, bills, advertisements or such papers to, or write, or cause to be written on, any private house, shop, wall or other structure without the permission of the owner thereof, for propagation relating to the Election.

(4) No person shall, with the object of soliciting or giving votes for or against any Candidate, convene or organize meetings, processions or raise slogans and propagate in any other manner within the Election area during the period from Forty Eight hours prior to the day for polling to the completion of the act of polling.

#### 14. Prohibition on Causing Obstruction to Counting of Votes or Other Acts of Election

No Candidate or agent of him or any other person shall grab, loot, damage or destroy in any manner the ballot boxes or Ballot Papers or any other documents relating to the Election remaining in the place for counting of votes during the counting of votes in any Election with or without the use of force, or take or attempt to

take or cause to be taken them elsewhere from the place for counting of votes without the permission of the Returning Officer or employee engaged in the act of counting of votes or abet to the commission of such act or cause obstruction of any kind in any manner to any other acts relating to the Election.

#### 15. Prohibition on Divulgence of Secrecy

In any Election, the Returning Officer, Polling Officer, observer or any other official deputed by the Election Commission or any employee engaged in the act relating to the Election, Candidate or any of this agents or Voter or any other person shall not divulge to anybody or write or disclose in any manner the matter as to whether any Voter has voted or not or for which Candidate he has voted or who has got how many votes or other matter pertaining to the counting of votes as well as any kind of symbol or sign or any other matters in a manner to identify the Voter from the Ballot Paper.

Provided that, this Section shall not be deemed to be a bar to publicly announcing by the Returning Officer or the official authorized by him of the number of votes received by Candidates, in the course of counting of votes.

#### 16. Powers to Arrest

(1) The Returning Officer or Polling Officer may order any person whoever causes obstruction or attempts to or aids or abets to cause obstruction to the polling or counting of votes or any other act of Election to refrain from doing such act and to go out of that place.

(2) The Returning Officer or Polling Officer may order the employee deputed for security in that place to arrest any person who commits any act prohibited under Sub-section (2) of Section 3, Section 10 or Section 14 or any person who does not carry out the order issued by the Returning Officer or Polling Officer pursuant to Sub-section (1).

#### 20. Recounting of Votes

(1) If the concerned Candidate in any Election files a petition to have the Election declared void on the following ground, the Election Court may recount the votes :

(a) That the petitioner Candidate or any other Candidate has duly received a majority of votes, or

(b) That the Ballot Papers to be invalid have not been invalidated or the Ballot Papers to be valid in accordance with the law have not been validated.

(2) If, while recounting the votes pursuant to Sub-section (1), the petitioner Candidate or any other Candidate is proved to have duly received majority of votes, the Election result of the elected Candidate shall be void; and the Election Court shall declare the petitioner Candidate or any other Candidate to have been elected in accordance with the law.

#### 21. Procedure in Case of Equality of Votes

(1) While recounting the votes by the Election Court in the course of trial of a petition referred to in Section 20, the votes obtained by all the Candidates of the concerned election constituency shall have to be counted.

(2) If, while counting the votes pursuant to Sub-section (1), there is an equality of votes obtained by Two or more than Two Candidates in accordance with the law, the Election Court shall have to decide by lot between the Candidates obtaining the equal number of votes; and the Candidate selected as per that decision shall be deemed to have been elected by obtaining an additional vote.

### Chapter - 5

Miscellaneous

#### 22. Power to Try Cases

(1) The Election Court shall have the powers to try and settle cases under this Act.

(2) An appeal may be filed against a decision made by the Election Court in the Court prescribed by His Majesty's Government upon a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette; and the decision of the Court so prescribed shall be final.

(3) In the case as referred to in Clauses (e) and (f) of Section 11, or Section 14, even if punishment has to be imposed after the holding of the commission of the offence, it shall have no effect on the re-polling held as per the decision of the Commission at all the Polling Stations or in election constituency.

(4) The Election Court, while making decision on any petition relating to Election filed in it, may also make decision, pursuant to the prevailing law, on a forgery or fraud case arisen from such petition.

#### 28. Petition to Be Rejected

If a petition filed pursuant to this Act appears not to have met the requirements referred to in the law or is not filed within the limitation, that petition shall not be tried.

#### 29. Period of Time and Procedure for Settlement of Case

(1) The Election Court shall have to try and settle a petition within one year from the date on which the petition is filed with it.

(2) While trying and settling any petition referred to in this Act, the procedures referred to in the Summary Procedures Act, 2028 (1971) shall have to be followed.

#### 30. Dismissal of Petition

In the event of the death of the petitioner or respondent of a case referred to in this Act, the petition referred to in Section 19 or 20 may be dismissed.

#### 31. Withdrawal of Petition

The petitioner may, with the leave of the Election Court, withdraw the petition filed by him pursuant to Section 19 or 20.

Provided that, if the Election Court is of the opinion that the petitioner has made an application to withdraw the petition for any undue benefit, it shall not grant leave for the withdrawal of the petition.

#### 32. Notice of Decision to be Given

The official trying the case pursuant to this Act shall have to send a notice, accompanied by a duplicate copy of the decision of the case, to the Commission and Parliament Secretariat within Fifteen days of deciding the case.

#### 33. His Majesty's Government to be Plaintiff

His Majesty's Government shall be plaintiff in the case bearing punishment pursuant to this Act.

#### 34. Inadmissibility in Evidence

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act or the prevailing law, no witness or other person shall in any case or legal proceedings instituted under this Act be required to state whom he has voted in the Election.

(2) An answer given by any witness to any question asked by the official trying the case in the course of proceedings of the case referred to in this Act shall neither be admissible in evidence in any case instituted against him nor be allowed to be furnished as an evidence.

#### 35. Settlement of Question relating to Disqualification

In cases where in any case filed pursuant to this Act there arises a question to be settled pursuant to Article 48 of the Constitution, the official trying the case shall have to hold necessary inquiries about it and forward the concerned case-file to the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court for final decision; and where the case-file is so received, decision shall be made according to that Article.

#### 36. This Act to Prevail

In respect of the matters contained in this Act, this Act shall prevail, and in respect of other matters, the prevailing law shall prevail. .
