#### 27. Disqualifications for Candidate

(1) The following person shall not be qualified to be a Candidate in Election:

(a) Who has not completed the age of Twenty Five years,

[12]

(b) Who is not a citizen of Nepal,

(c) Who is of unsound mind or insane,

(d) Who is declared insolvent or bankrupt,

(e) Who is an incumbent employee of His Majesty's Government or of an autonomous body owned or controlled by His Majesty's Government or of a body having received grants from His Majesty's Government,

(f) Who is serving in a political post to be filled by Election or nomination and who is holding an office of profit for which a remuneration or economic benefit is paid out of a Government Fund except a pensioner,

(g) Who has been convicted of an offence relating to Election by the Election Court constituted pursuant to the prevailing law and Six years have not passed since the date of completion of the punishment,

(h) Who has been convicted of a criminal offence involving moral turpitude carrying punishment of imprisonment and punished with imprisonment for two years or more than Two years, and Six years have not passed since the date of completion of the punishment,

#### 28. Computation of Age of Candidate

While computing the age of a Candidate to be eligible to be a Candidate in Election, he/she shall have to complete the age of Twenty Five years on the date when he/she attains the age for voting pursuant to Section 11.

#### 29. Termination of Membership

(1) If the Election of any Member is declared void by the judgment of the Election Court constituted pursuant to the prevailing law, the membership of such Member in the House of Representatives shall be terminated from the date of such judgment.

(2) If any Member is elected or nominated to the post of member of the National Assembly, his membership shall, ipso facto, be terminated from the date whereon he is so elected or nominated.

#### 30. Membership of Member Elected from More Than One Constituency

(1) If any Candidate is elected from more than one election constituency, the elected Candidate shall, at his own will, tender resignation in writing to the Speaker of the House of Representatives, retaining the membership of any one election constituency and relinquishing the membership of other election constituency within Thirty days of the commencement of the session of Parliament.

(2) If such Member fails to tender resignation pursuant to Sub-section (1), the Commission shall request the Speaker of the House of Representatives to make decision on the retaining of membership of any one election constituency and vacating the membership of other election constituency wherefrom he/she has been elected, and shall publish in the Nepal Gazette a Notification of vacation of his/her membership in other election constituency in accordance with the decision of the Speaker.

#### 34. Scrutiny of Nomination Papers

Provided that, a nomination paper shall not be invalidated only by virtue of minor error and omission contained in the nomination paper.

Explanation: For the purpose of this Sub-section, "minor error and omission" means any technical error or error of letters, numbers or vowel signs relating to the writing or printing of name, surname, address or serial number of Candidate, proposer or person related with them contained in the electoral roll or nomination paper, which does not cause any substantial difference.

(4) The Returning Officer shall not be entitled to postpone the proceedings on the scrutiny of nomination papers except in the following circumstances:

(a) If any mobbing or violence erupts in the place and at time of scrutiny of nomination papers, or

(5) If the proceedings on the scrutiny of nomination papers are postponed under Sub-section (4), the Returning Officer shall specify the time and place for scrutiny of nomination papers on the following day and scrutinize them on that day.

#### 35. Invalidity of Nomination Paper

A nomination paper shall be invalid in the following circumstances:

(a) If the Candidate does not possess the qualifications referred to in the Constitution and this Act,

(b) If there does not exist the consent of the person to be Candidate or the nomination paper does not bear his signature or the signature of the proposer and the seconder nominating him as a Candidate or contains forged signature,

(c) If the deposit required to be furnished pursuant to Section 74 is not furnished,

(d) If the nomination paper is not filed within the time specified pursuant to Section 26,

(e) If the nomination paper is not filed as mentioned in Sections 32 and 33, or

(f) If other requirements referred to in this Act are not fulfilled.

#### 36. Name-list of Candidates

After the nomination papers have been scrutinized, the Returning Officer shall have to prepare a name-list of the Candidates referred to in the nomination papers meeting the requirements as per the law in the prescribed manner and publish a copy of the name-list at his office.

(1) If any Candidate desires to withdraw his/her name to get his name removed from the name-list of Candidates,

(3) After the notice of withdrawing name pursuant to Sub-section (1) has been submitted to the Returning Officer, the notice shall not be allowed to be cancelled or withdrawn.

(4) The Returning Officer shall, if he is satisfied with the genuineness of the notice submitted pursuant to Sub-section (1), remove the name of the Candidate withdrawing his/her name from the name-list of Candidates, and immediately publish a notice of name removal at his/her office.

(5) If the authorised person of any political organisation or party so writes to the Returning Officer as to withdraw the name of any Candidate set up by that political organisation or party within the time-limit specified pursuant to Sub-section (1) of Section 26, he shall not be retained as a Candidate of that organisation or party and he shall be deemed to have stood as an independent Candidate.

#### 38. Final Name-list of Candidates

#### 39. Uncontested Election

(1) If a nomination paper of only one person is filed in any election constituency and the nomination paper is found to be due, or if more than one nomination paper have been filed but the other Candidates have withdrawn their names or the other nomination papers are declared invalid and there remains only one Candidate, the Returning Officer shall declare, as prescribed, that the Candidate has been elected unopposed.

(2) If the number of Candidate is more than one for Election in any election constituency, there shall be polling in that election constituency.

#### 50. Unlawful Seizure of Polling Station

(1) In the following circumstances, a polling station shall be deemed to have been unlawfully seized:

(a) If anyone, by using force or coercion, or showing intimidation or giving threat, takes possession of the polling station or the place fixed for polling and influences the act of polling or allows only the Voters supporting any Candidate to vote or prevents other Voters from voting,

(b) If anyone, with or without the use of force or coercion, intimidates or threatens the Voter to vote or not to vote for any Candidate or prevents the Voter from going to or entering into the polling station or the place for polling, to cast the vote,

(c) If anyone, with or without the use of force, prevents the official deputed to conduct the act of polling from discharging his duties or snatches the ballot papers, ballot boxes or materials related with polling which are in his custody or causes influence by committing similar other acts in a manner that the act of polling is not or cannot be held fairly, freely and normally.

(2) If any circumstance referred to in Sub-section (1) occurs, a report thereof shall have to be given to the Returning Officer by the

Polling Officer and to the Commission by the Returning Officer immediately.

(3) The Commission may, on receipt of a report referred to in Sub-section (2), depute an observer to hold inquiries in that place; and the Commission may cancel the polling of that polling station on the basis of the report of the observer, if such observer has been so deputed, and other circumstances as well. The Returning Officer shall, at the direction of the Commission, specify the date and time for re-polling at the polling station where the polling has been so cancelled.

(4) If, on the basis of the report referred to in Sub-section (2) and other circumstances, appears a possibility that the result of Election may be affected because of unlawful seizure of polling stations, the Commission may cancel the Election of that election constituency; and the Returning Officer shall, at the direction of the Commission, specify the date and time for re-polling in that election constituency.
