# Magistrate Act, 2008

www.lawcommission.gov.np

Authorisation of Emergency Powers to the Magistrate Act, 2008

An act to provide for emergency powers to the Magistrate for a period of Six months to control (daman) disorder (arajakata).

Whereas, it is expedient to provide for emergency powers to the Magistrates to control disorder, maintain law and order and to perform other ancillary acts relating there to, Be, it enacted the following Act by His Majesty the King on the advice of the cabinet 1. (1) This Act shall be called the “Authorisation of Emergency Powers to the Magistrate Act, 2008”.

(2) This Act shall extend throughout Nepal. This Act shall come into force immediately and shall be enforced for a period of Six months.

2. (1) If the Magistrate satisfies that someone is going to commit an offence relating to breach of peace or dacoity or any other serious offence, he/she may arrest and detain such person for a period not exceeding Three months. Such person shall be released in case sufficient guarantee (jamanat), in the satisfaction of the Magistrate, is furnished.

(2) A person arrested pursuant to Sub-section (1) may file a complaint (Ujur) before the Government against his/her detention and the government may, if finds it appropriate release such a person with or without any condition.

3. For a period of Six months from the date of commencement of this Act, out of the rights (Bishesadhikar) received by a person through the license of Arms issued by the officials subordinate to the Government, the other rights shall be postponed (Sthagit) except the right to keep such license within his/her house.

4. If a person appears along with the weapon, which is with or without a license, in a public place, such a person may be immediately arrested and shall be liable for vigorous (kada) imprisonment for a period of Six months or a fine up to One Thousand Rupees or the both, if found guilty.

5. The group (Daffa) of four or more persons along with the weapons which could be used for attack except the Khukuri (typical Nepali knife), other than the members of armed forces approved by the Government, shall be considered an armed group (Shasatra dal).

6. If a member of an armed group is found guilty, he/she shall be liable for a vigorous (kada) imprisonment for a period up to Two years or a fine up to One Thousand Rupees or the both.

7. The Magistrate, the armed force (Army) or police in the district, if finds it appropriate, may also use force against the armed group and disperse (Titar bitar) them.

8. This Act shall prevail even in a case the provisions of this Act are inconsistent with any other prevailing Acts.

17th Ashad, 2008 B.S.
