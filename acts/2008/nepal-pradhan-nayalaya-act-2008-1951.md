# Nepal Pradhan Nayalaya Act, 2008 (1951)

Nepal Pradhan Nayalaya Act, 2008 (1951) Preamble Whereas, it is expedient to amend and consolidate the prevailing Act, Sawal and Sanad relating to the constitution, power and functions of Nepal Pradhan Nayalaya, Be, it enacted, by His Majesty on the advice of the cabinet.

Short Title 1. (a) This Act shall be called "Nepal Pradhan Nayalaya Act, 2008 (1951)".

(b) This Act shall come into force throughout Nepal.

(c) This Act shall come into force on such date, as published later on, in the Nepal Gazette.

Establishment of the Pradhan Nayalaya 2. The Pradhan Nayalaya of Nepal shall be the Supreme Court (Nayalaya).

3. For the purpose of this Act, the contemporary (prevailing) Nayalaya exercising all the powers as a Pradhan Nayalaya shall be considered to be the Pradhan Nayalaya.

4. The decisions of Pradhan Nayalaya shall be recognised as irrefutable (Akatya) evidence (proof), and the Pradhan Nayalaya shall have all the powers to take action and punish in a case of contempt of court committed against it and other courts subordinate to it.

5. Pradhan Nayalaya shall be located in Kathmandu or in such place or places as appointed by the chief justice with the approval of His Majesty including the cabinet, from time to time.

Composition of Pradhan Nayalaya 6. There shall a chief justice and Four other justices in the Pradhan Nayalaya until and unless the number is increased by His Majesty along with the cabinet.

Appointment of chief justice and other justices of Pradhan Nayalaya 7. His Majesty shall appoint the chief justice and other justices on the advice of the cabinet and they shall remain in their post till they attend the age of Sixty Five years.

There is a restriction on it that,

(a) In appointing the justices except the chief justice, the opinion of chief justice shall always be sought.

(b) Such an appointment may be in an Ad hoc basis which would be confirmed after a year. There are additional restrictions on it that,

(a) The chief justice and other justices may tender resignation in writing to His Majesty along with the cabinet.

(b) The chief justice and other justices shall be removed from their respective posts, only by the command of His Majesty, upon considering the opinion of the Advisory committee, on the advice of the Two Third majority of the cabinet, when he/she is convicted on misconduct (not having good behaviour) or incompetency.

For not being a citizen of Nepal and whenever it is possible 8. (a) In case, one, has not served for a period of Ten years in the justice delivering post in Nepal, or (b) One who has not served as a lawyer for a period of Ten years before the Pradhan Nayalaya shall not be eligible to be appointed in the post of chief justice or a justice.

Provided that, in a special case where eligible persons for the post of chief justice and justices are not available from the Nepali citizens, a person other than a Nepali citizen shall also be considered eligible and may be appointed.

(c) In the course of appointing the justices, the specialty of the higher education (academic expertise) and experience regarding court affairs and law shall also be considered.

9. A person, appointed in the post of chief justice or justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya, before resuming his/her office, shall take an oath and get signature and submit it in front of His Majesty the King or a person so appointed for this purpose by his Majesty, as follows:

I.............................who has been appointed in the post of chief justice or justice of Pradhan Nayalaya take an oath in the name of God/Solemnly promise that I shall keep every respect and regard to the Nepal Antarim Bidhan. I shall abide by my official duty with all respect and my qualification, knowledge and wisdom and without any fear, partiality, love or hatered and I shall pay due respect to the Bidhan and Act of Nepal.

#### 10. The post of chief justice shall be considered bigger in comparison to the post of a justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya

The protocol of other justices shall be determined based on their seniority.

Appointment of Acting Chief Justice 11. In a case the post of chief justice is vacant, and if His Majesty could not make appoint due to His absence or any other reasons, the senior most justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya, who shall be appointed by His Majesty along with the cabinet, shall be the Acting Chief Justice.

Appointment of Ad hoc Justices for an Acting Justice 12. Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, the chief justice of Pradhan Nayalaya may appoint Ad hoc justices of the Pradhan Nayalaya with the approval of His Majesty along with the cabinet, at any time, as required, to a person who has worked as a justice of Pradhan Nayalaya or who deserves qualification to be appointed as the justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya. Such an Ad hoc justice so appointed shall receive allowance as determined by His Majesty along with the cabinet. The jurisdiction, authority and facilities of such justices shall be as good as the justices of the Pradhan Nayalaya subject to this provision.

Provided that, in addition to this, he/she shall not be considered to be the justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya.

13. (a) If required number of justices could not be fulfilled to set up a special Bench or to continue such a Bench of the Pradhan Nayalaya, the chief justice, with the prior approval of His Majesty along with the cabinet, may request to act as an Ad hoc justice of a Bench for a period so required for a judge of the immediately subordinate court (Adda) to the Pradhan Nayalaya or a person who deserves qualification to be a justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya.

(b) It shall be the primary duty of such justice (judge) in a priority, rather than other official duty, to appear in time and function in the Pradhan Nayalaya upto the prescribed period. Such a justice (judge)

shall receive allowances as determined by His Majesty along with the cabinet for the service so rendered. Jurisdiction, authority, facilities and duties of such a justice (judge) shall be as good as the justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya.

Provided that, subject to this provision he/she shall not be considered to be the justice of Pradhan Nayalaya.

Remuneration and Allowances of the justices 14. The chief justice and other justices of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall receive the remuneration, allowances and facilities as determined by His Majesty along with the cabinet. They shall have right on leave and Pension as determined by His Majesty along with the cabinet, from time to time.

Provided that, the provisions of remuneration, allowance, facilities, or leave and pension of the chief justice and other justices shall not be altered to their disadvantage.

Seal and Custody of Seal 15. There shall be a seal of the Pradhan Nayalaya in which Rajkiya Chinha, Rastriya Chinha and Rastriya Prateek shall be designed (Ankit) for its use and it shall remain into the custody of the chief justice.

Affixing Seal on the Notice etc.

16. The seal of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall be affixed in the notice, summon Rules, orders, decision and other mandatory commands (Agaya) to be issued by the Pradhan Nayalaya.

Jurisdiction and Authority and powers to frame Rules of the Pradhan Nayalaya 17. Subject to the Sawal or Rules issued or to be issued by His Majesty along with the cabinet, the Pradhan Nayalaya may, to manage for its internal system (Roharit), issue standing orders (Kayami Aadesh) and frame Rules. Such an order and Rules shall come into force after the approval of His Majesty along with the cabinet.

Jurisdiction and its exercise 18. Unless it is provided in the Bidhan (constitution) the Pradhan Nayalaya shall have no original jurisdiction except as referred to in Section 4.

19. Subject to the Act and Sawal to be issued by His Majesty along with the cabinet, the Pradhan Nayalaya may frame Rules to smoothly run the system of justice and to exercise the power of revision and final appeal through a single Bench, a Bench more than a single judge or Division Bench or Special Bench or Full Bench.

Composition of Bench 20. The chief justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall, from time to time, determine the composition of the Bench; in which there shall be a single Bench, Division Bench, Special Bench or Full Bench which shall be composed of with or without the chief justice as referred hereinabove.

Jurisdiction and Authority 21. The Pradhan Nayalaya shall have Revision and Appeal jurisdictions. The Pradhan Nayalaya shall hear Appeal Bintipatra and Reference. This court shall have authority to remand or to review and transfer of a case.

Filing Appeal before Pradhan Nayalaya in a case disposed of by a single judge 22. If a Division Bench of the Pradhan Nayalaya grants leave (Nissa) for a revision or an appeal of a case, an appeal or a revision petition may be filed before the Pradhan Nayalaya against the decree or order issued by the single Bench of a justice including the chief justice upon exercising the jurisdiction of an Appeal or Revision of the Pradhan Nayalaya.

Power to accept original jurisdiction in a special condition 23. In case both the parties to a case agree, the Pradhan Nayalaya, if it deems appropriate, in a special condition, upon mentioning the reasons thereof in the case file, may transfer or hear and dispose a case, which comes under the jurisdiction of a court subordinate to it, as if it is a court of original jurisdiction.

24. In a case, if there is a pertinent issue of interpretation of the Constitution (Bidhan) for the disposal of the case and such an issue is to be resolved by the Pradhan Nayalaya itself, the Pradhan Nayalaya may order to forward such case file before it, and it-

(a) may decide the case by itself, or (b) may resolve the legal issue and send back the case file along with the decision and the concerned court shall also start inquiry as pointed out in the decision.

Procedure regarding Dissenting opinion of Two or more justices 25. (a) If a case is heard by Two or more justices, the decision shall be made by the opinion of the justices; and if there is a dissenting opinion the majority decision shall prevail.

Provided that, the dissenting justices shall write their opinion separately.

(b) In a case where a decree or order of an appeal in fully or partly reversed (Ulti) and there lacks a majority in not doing the same, the decree or order on such appeal shall be upheld.

Provided that, if there are Two or more justices in the Bench hearing such a case and there is dissenting opinion between/among the justices on the point of law, they shall point out the legal issue so dissented. There after, such a case shall be heard by the single or Division Bench subject to the point of law so pointed out and such a point of law shall be resolved by the majority of the justices who heard it earlier and are hearing it at present.

Authority to issue order etc. to dispense complete justice (Pura Insaf) 26. The Pradhan Nayalaya may give a decree or issue an order, as required, to dispense complete justice in a case filed or matter submitted with it in the course of exercising its jurisdiction and such a decree or order shall be implemented throughout Nepal as prescribed by the law.

Authority to cause to appear a person or Submission of documents etc.

27. Subject to the Rules and laws to be framed by the government, the Pradhan Nayalaya shall have, each and all, authority to cause to appear any person or to disclose or submit any document or to issue an investigation (Tahakikat) order in any matter in relation to any proceedings.

Reference (Nirdesh) from the Government for an opinion 28. In a case where there arises or there is a possibility of arising any important question in a special public issue regarding any law or circumstance (Bastusthiti), and if the Government deems it fit to seek an opinion of the Pradhan Nayalaya, it may forward such issue or matter for the consideration of the Pradhan Nayalaya; and in such a case, Pradhan Nayalaya shall consider on it and submit (Jaher) its opinion (Raya) to the Government.

Language of the Court or Bench (Kachahari) 29. Transaction (Lekhapadhi) of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall be in Nepali Language.

Authority to issue writ (Purji) etc.

30. The Pradhan Nayalaya shall have authority to issue any of the writs (Aagyapatra) or order in which heabous corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto, certiorary are included against any person, persons, company, corporation or officer, as required, for the exercise of the rights guaranteed by the Bidhan in all matters which fall under its jurisdiction, wherever, all over Nepal.

Law declared by the Pradhan Nayalaya to be mandatory 31. Any case law declared by the Pradhan Nayalaya in the course of a lawsuit shall be mandatorily followed by the courts which are subordinate to it.

Cooperation to be extended to the Pradhan Nayalaya by the Authorities 32. The civil (Nijamati) and judicial Authorities of Nepal, in which the officers from criminal justice sector are also included, shall extend co-operation to the Pradhan Nayalaya as it requires.

Superior (Taluk) Authority 33. All the expenses of the officers and staffs or their remuneration, allowances and pension and remuneration and allowance of the justices, and expenditure to be made for the management of justice of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall be made from the state revenue (Rajya Kos). All the money recovered by the Pradhan Nayalaya as a fee or any other amount, shall be deposited (Sammilit) in the Rajya Kos.

34. (1) The Pradhan Nayalaya shall have Taluk (superiority) on all the courts and tribunals, within its jurisdictions; which are established in Nepal.

(2) Without any contradiction to the generality of the provisions as referred to in hereinabove, the Pradhan Nayalaya may perform the following functions:

(a) To collect the record (Phant) of the cases disposed of by such courts and the details (Lagat) of income and expenditure,

(b) To ascertain forms and formats to manage the process (Roharit) and procedure of such courts and issue General Rules therefor,

(c) To ascertain the forms and formats of the inventories (Sayaha Shresta, Bahi, Hisab Kitab) to be maintained by the officers of such courts.

(3) The Pradhan Nayalaya may fix the fees to be paid to the officers or staffs engaged in those courts and lawyers (Wokil) and attorney pleading the case.

Provided that, the Rules to be framed, forms and formats to be ascertained or fees to be fixed shall not contradict the prevailing laws.

(4) Anything provided in this Section shall not be considered to have any superior authority (Taluki Akhtiyar) to the Pradhan Nayalaya over any Court or Tribunal established pursuant to the Army Act.

Rules regarding lawyers etc.

35. The Pradhan Nayalaya may, without infringing the provisions of Act and laws, frame Rules on the fees of the lawyers who plead before the Pradhan Nayalaya and expenses to be incurred for appeal registration, Notice and other proceedings and it may specially frame Rules on disposal of an appeal through the Thado Karbahi (direct process) where, in the opinion of it, the general procedure is futile, Khichaulaya and lingering (Jhulayaune) in its nature.

Decision to be heard in the Open Bench 36. A decision shall be heard in the open bench by the Pradhan Nayalaya.

Appointment of Officers and Staffs of the Pradhan Nayalaya

37. (a) The employees of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall be appointed by the Pradhan Nayalaya and it shall be confirmed by the Government.

Provided that, in the post of Pradhan Nayalaya, which deserves a pay of more than Eight Hundred Rupees per anum the

appointment shall be made by the concerned (Taluk) Ministry on the advice of the Public Service Commission.

(b) Conditions of service of the officers of the Pradhan Nayalaya shall be as ascertained by the Pradhan Nayalaya in this regard.

Provided that, the Rules regarding remuneration, allowances, leave or pension, to be framed under this Section, shall have to be approved by the Government.

Subordinate Courts 38. (a) His Majesty along with the cabinet, with the opinion of the Pradhan Nayalaya, shall appoint the District judges; His Majesty shall accordingly, make placement and provide promotion to them.

(b) In judicial service, the appointment of other persons other than District judges, shall be made by the Taluk Ministry upon consulting the Pradhan Nayalaya and Nepal Public Service Commission.

(c) Employees in the District (Court) shall be appointed by the Pradhan Nayalaya in consultation with the concerned District Judge, in a manner as prescribed in Clauses (a) and (b) of Section 37 of this Act.

(d) The Pradhan Nayalaya, without contradicting the provision of Clause (Sub-section) (a), shall have control over placement, promotion and approval of leave of the persons belonging to judicial service working in District Courts and court subordinate to it and other persons employed in such courts.

Provided that (Kintu), no matter, provided in this Number, shall infringe any right of a person to file an appeal, which has been guaranteed by his/her service law. Furthermore, this Number shall not be construed to provide any additional power to the Pradhan Nayalaya so as provided by the service law in the course of taking any action against such person.

39. All the Acts, Sawals and Sanads enforced before the commencement of this Act, which are repugnant to this Act, in relation to the Pradhan Nayalaya, shall be deemed void.
