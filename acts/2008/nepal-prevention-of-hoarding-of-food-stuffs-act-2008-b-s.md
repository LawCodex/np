# Nepal Prevention of Hoarding of Food Stuffs Act, 2008

www.lawcommission.gov.np

Nepal Prevention of Hoarding of Food Stuffs Act, 2008

Whereas, it is expedient to stop the hoarding of food stuffs (Anna) to mitigate the scarcity of food items in the country.

Be, it enacted by His Majesty the King on the advice of the cabinet.

1. (1) This law shall be called as “Nepal Prevention of Hoarding of Food Stuffs Act, 2008”.

(2) This Act shall come into force immediately.

2. If a person stores any food stuff (Anna), with him/herself or within his/her control, exceeding the individual need or family need and to meet the need of a business or profession and the need of seeds in the case of a farmer (Khetiwal); it shall be considered as the hoarding of food stuffs.

3. Those persons, who are considered to have been hoarding food stuffs in the opinion of the Badahakim (Magistrate), shall be taken into custody (Thunai) pursuant to Clause (b), Sub-Section(1) of Section 2 of the Nepal Public Security Law for committing an act against the interest of the community for hoarding essential commodities.

4. If the Magistrate thinks that someone is hoarding food stuffs, such excessive (hoarded) food stuffs shall be sold to a person or persons, in a open market price or in an appropriate price fixed by the Magistrate, by his order. The Magistrate shall take this Action as an additional or alternative action as referred to in Nepal Public Security Law.

5. The Magistrate, if there are reasonable grounds for such a doubt that someone is hoarding the food stuffs, may take search of any house/building by a responsible person; such a search shall be conducted upon serving a notice to the owner of such house; and such a search shall be conducted with maintaining prestige, respect, politeness and decorum with such persons.

6. If a person causes any obstacle or hindrance in the course of conducting search as referred to in Section 5, such a person shall be liable for an imprisonment up to Six months or a fine up to Five Hundred Rupees or the both.

7. (1) If a person denies to sell the excessive food stuffs so hoarded or causes obstacle or hindrance in its sale as referred to in Section 4, he/she shall be liable for an imprisonment up to One year and a fine up to Five Hundred Rupees or the both.

(2) If someone causes any obstacle or hindrance in selling the excessive food stuffs so hoarded, the Magistrate shall be entitled to forcefully take into control the excessive food stuffs and to sale it by his/her order. The food stuffs below the limit of hoarding shall be returned to the owner of the food stuffs.

8. No case shall be filed or action shall be initiated in any court against the Magistrate or a person so authorized by him/her for acting pursuant to the authority of this Law.
