# Control Of Black Marketing And Smuggling Of Cotton Thread And Cotton Clothe Manufactured In The Mills Act 2008 B S

Control of Black Marketing and Smuggling of Cotton Thread and Cotton Cloth/e Manufactured in the Mills Act, 2008 Preamble Whereas, the attention of the Government of Nepal has been attracted to the scarcity of the stock of cotton thread and cotton cloth/e and in case of sufficient stock price is hiking day by day within the territory of Nepal; and

Whereas, the main reasons for such scarcity and price hike are the smuggling of those commodities from Tarai Sarahad to Hindustan, and to hoard or illegally export such commodities to sell in the self regulated (Manoman) improper rate without selling immediately those commodities even importing in a quota or without a quota pretending in the name of Nepal without paying any revenue (Mahasul) to the government and also concealing the information thereof to the government even within the territory of Nepal; and Whereas, few people are engaged in the black marketing with all their efforts of those commodities which are received as a quota or non quota goods in Nepal; and Now, therefore, be it enacted this law by His Majesty the King, on the advise of the cabinet.

1. (a) This Act shall be called "Control of Black Marketing and Smuggling of Cotton Thread and Cotton Cloth/e manufactured in the Mills Law, 2008".

(b) This Act shall extend throughout Nepal.

(c) This Act shall come into force upon its commencement/issuance.

2. (a) To carry (export) thread Dhago) and cloths, outside the territory of Nepal, without the order of the Government, shall be considered smuggling (Chori Nikasi).

(b) To sell in higher rate, to sell without abiding by the Rules, to sell or cause to sell against the Rule or not to sell in the government rate upon following the government Rules, the cotton thread (Dhago) and/cotton cloth/e to be sold in the government control rate as fixed by the government within the territory of Nepal shall be considered black marketing.

3. (a) If it is proved that someone is smuggling or attempting to smuggle cotton thread or cloth/e, the thread or cloth/e so attempted to smuggle shall be forfeited.

(b) If it is proved that someone is engaged in black marketing or attempting for the same of the thread or cloth/e even thread or cloth/e or the money involved in such purchase shall be forfeited.

(c) In a case, smuggling or black marketing is proved, one shall be liable for a fine upto One Hundred to One Thousand Rupees if such a cloth is Five Hundred yard (Gaj) and/or cotton is one bundle (Ganth) and if the quantity is more than that one shall be liable for an imprisonment of Three years and a fine upto Two Thousand Rupees; and if someone fails to pay the fine upto One Thousand Rupees he/she shall be liable for additional Six months of imprisonment and if such an amount is upto Two Thousand Rupees he/she shall be liable for an imprisonment for a period of One year.

4. If the government has appropriate (sufficient/Uchit) evidence that someone is hoarding cotton thread or cloth/e in any place with a purpose of smuggling or black marketing, government shall proceed search of such a place. If such a hoarding is proved while conducting such a search it shall be considered that such a person has violated this law and he/she shall be www.lawcommission.gov.np 2

liable for a penalty as if he/she has committed black marketing or smuggling of those commodities.

5. If someone causes obstacle or hindrance in the course of search as referred to in Section 4 or forfeiture of the commodity or money involved in the purchase as referred to in Section 3, such a person shall be liable for an imprisonment upto Six months or a fine upto Five Hundred Rupees or the both.

6. (a) If a person or a body (Sanstha) gives information regarding black marketing or smuggling and such an accused is arrested and an appropriate action is taken by the government and such an accused is convicted because of such an information, the informant either a person or a body, shall receive a reward of an amount which is equal to Twenty Five percent of the thread and cloth/e so confiscated.

(b) If a person or body provides active co-operation including the information to arrest and convict the accused such a person shall receive a reward of an amount which is equal to Fifty percent of thread and cloth/e so confiscated.

7. If a person or body furnishes false and baseless (Bebuniyad) information regarding black marketing or smuggling to the government such an informant shall be liable for an action pursuant to the prevailing Act/Sawal.

8. (a) A person or body shall not be entitled to take any other action except providing information and active co-operation to the government as referred to in Section 6.

(b) Any of the actions to be taken regarding black marketing or smuggling pursuant to this Act shall be taken only by the government officer or such an office.

(c) Except this, if a non-governmental person or a member or members of such body causes unauthorised encroachment (Anadhikar Dakhal)

in such government action, he/she/they shall be liable for an imprisonment not exceeding Three years.

Note (Tippani): For the purpose of Clause (b) of Section 6, the phrase "active co-operation" means to provide full co-operation upon involving in person with the government employee by the informant in the course of government action.
