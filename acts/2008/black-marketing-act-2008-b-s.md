# Black Marketing Act, 2008

www.lawcommission.gov.np

Black Marketing Act, 2008

Whereas, the government has exported or caused for the same different goods and commodities from different places upon suffering heavy loss and putting every effort to distribute essential goods and commodities of daily use in an easy manner and a fair rate to all the people within Nepal, and;

Whereas, some of the traders and few persons are being indulged in the personal interest and working against the objective of the government by selling such goods and commodities in the black market and also deceiving/cheating the government and the public at large;

Now therefore, to check such a black marketing, government has been compelled to issue this Act.

1. (a) This Act shall be called “Black Marketing Act, 2008”.

(b) This Act shall come into force immediately throughout Nepal.

2. To sell in a higher rates secretly upon violating the Rules by not selling any commodity in a rate so fixed upon considering all the facts and figures including the Parta Kharcha, profit, rent and charges etc from the place or shops in a rate as prescribed by the government shall be considered Black Marketing.

3. The Police or Chief of Goshwara in the Centre (Sadar/Kathmandu valley) or Magistrate of Gouda, Gadi or Goshowara, gets knowledge or receives information or a complaint, he/she shall take action by him//herself or by deputing any competent officer for the same; and if it is so proved by the evidence or Sarjamin, he/she may, in his/her

discretion, take actions, including fixing seal (lahachhap) for search in the shop.

4. If a person is found guilty for committing black marketing, he/she shall be liable for a fine up to Two Thousand Rupees or an imprisonment up to Fifteen days to Six months or the both, in the discretion of the chief of the office (Hakim).

5. The License of a person, who is convicted under this Act, shall be forfeited (Jafat); and Government shall confiscate the commodity used in black marketing and Government shall also manage the sale/distribution of such confiscated commodities.
