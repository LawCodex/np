Preamble : Whereas, it is expedient to so rearrange, including amendment to, the prevailing Nepal Law as to provide for acquisition, termination of, and matters pertaining to, the citizenship of Nepal;

Now, therefore, His Majesty the King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev has, with the consent and advice of National Panchayat, enacted this Act.

#### 1. Short Title and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called as "Nepal Citizenship Act, 2020 (1964)".

(2) It shall come into force immediately.

(a) "Minor" means a person who has not attained Sixteen years of age.

(b) "Person" shall not mean any company, organization or group of persons, whether incorporated or not.

#### 7. Citizenship In the Event of Incorporation of Territory

Whenever any territory is acquired by way of incorporation into Nepal, a person having his/her domicile within such territory shall be deemed to be a citizen of Nepal from such date as may be specified in such Notification published by His Majesty's Government subject to this Act.

#### 8. Renunciation of Citizenship of Nepal

After any citizen of Nepal has given a notice in the prescribed manner to the prescribed authority to renunciate the citizenship of Nepal, the prescribed authority may register such notice; and his/her Nepalese citizenship shall cease to be valid from the date of registration of such notice.

12 8A. Resumption of Citizenship of Nepal : If any woman citizen of Nepal has, after having obtained the citizenship of the country of her husband by way of getting married to such foreign citizen, renounced the citizenship of that country because of getting divorced from, or death of, the husband and come to and resided in Nepal and gives notice of such residing to the prescribed authority, her citizenship of Nepal shall be resumed from the date of registration of such notice by the prescribed authority.

#### 9. Termination of Citizenship of Nepal

(1) The citizenship of Nepal of any citizen of Nepal shall ipso facto be lapsed after he/she has voluntarily acquired the citizenship of any foreign country.

(2) If any person has become a citizen of a foreign country and a citizen of Nepal by birth and descent at the same time, such person has to choose citizenship of either country within Five years of his/her

#### 12. Revision

(1) Except as otherwise mentioned in other Sections of this Act, any person aggrieved by an order issued by His Majesty's Government or any prescribed authority under this Act may make a petition to His Majesty's Government within Thirty Five days of the issuance of the order for revision of such order.

(2) After any petition has been made under Sub-section (1), His Majesty's Government may, upon considering the petition and a report of the authority against whose order such petition has been made, give its decision or order; and that decision or order shall be final.

#### 16. Powers to Frame Rules

His Majesty's Government may, as per necessity, frame Rules from the to time, in order to carry out the objectives of this Act; and these Rules shall come into force only after being published in the Nepal Gazette.

#### 17. Consequences of Ineffectiveness of Nepal Citizenship Ordinance, 2020 (1963)

Unless otherwise purported, after the Nepal Citizenship Ordinance, 2020 (1963) has become ineffective, that ineffectiveness,-

(a) Does not give rise to any matter not in existence at the time of that ineffectiveness,

(b) Does not affect any matter in operation according to the ordinance or any act previously carried out or any thing already used by fulfilling the requirements as referred to in the Ordinance,

(c) Does not affect any right, facility, duty or liability, obtained, acquired or used in accordance with the ordinance,

(d) Does not affect any punishment imposed for any offence committed against the ordinance,

(e) Does not affect any legal proceedings or remedies taken in respect of any of such above-mentioned rights, facilities, duties, liabilities punishment; and any such legal proceedings or remedies may be instituted, kept on or enforced and such punishment may also be imposed as if the Ordinance were in force.
