# Remuneration and Facilities of the Ministers Act, 2049 (1993)

Remuneration and Facilities of the Ministers Act, 2049 (1993) Date of Authentication and Publication

2050.2.32 (14 June1993)

Amendment

1. Republic Strengthening and Some Nepal Laws Amendment Act, 2066 (2010) 1 2066.10.7.5(21 Jan., 2010)

Act Number 11 of the year 2050 (1993)

An Act Made to Provide for Remuneration and Facilities of Ministers

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to make legal provisions on the remuneration and facilities of Ministers;

Now, therefore, be it enacted by the Parliament in the Twenty-second year of the reign of His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev.

### Chapter-1

Preliminary

#### 1. Short tile and commencement

(1) This Act may be called as the “Remuneration and Facilities of the Ministers Act, 2049 (1993)."

(2) This Act shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Act,-

(a) “Minister" includes the Prime Minister, Deputy Prime Minister, Minister, Minister of State and Assistant Minister.

(b) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed or as prescribed in the orders issued by the Government of Nepal by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette.

### Chapter-2

Remuneration and Other Facilities

#### 3. Remuneration

(1) The following Ministers shall be entitled to the following remuneration:

<sup>2</sup>(a) Prime Minister Monthly Rs.9,000.00

(b) Deputy Prime Minister Monthly Rs.7,500.00

(c) Minister Monthly Rs. 7,000.00

(d) Minister of State Monthly Rs. 6,700.00

(e) Assistant Minister Monthly Rs.5,800.00

(2) The Government of Nepal may, if it so considers necessary, alter the monthly remuneration of Ministers, by publishing a Notification in the Nepal Gazette.

(3) If a Minister does not continue to hold the office of Minister upon his or her death while holding the office of Minister or having accepted the resignation tendered by him or her or upon dissolution of the Council of Ministers or for any other reason, he or she shall be entitled to additional remuneration of one month, in addition to the remuneration to

which he or she is entitled up to the day on which he or she holds the office of Minister.

(4) The remuneration or additional remuneration receivable by a Minister pursuant to Sub-section (3) shall be provided to him or her or his or her nominee, as the case may be. In the event of death of a Minister, the amount of such remuneration shall be provided to his or her near heir if he or she has not nominated any one or his or her nominee is not alive.

> 2 As per the decision of Council of Ministers of 2067-5-17 the remuneration commenced from 2067-4-1 is as follows and the Notification regarding remuneration is not published in the Nepal Gazette: Prime Minister Rs. 36,920/- Deputy Prime Minister Rs. 31,000/- Minister Rs. 28,920/- Minister of State Rs. 27,360/- Assistant Minister Rs. 26,730/-.

#### 4. Accommodation

(1) The Government of Nepal shall make arrangement for governmental buildings for the housing of Ministers. Where arrangement of such buildings is not made or where the Ministers have no appropriate buildings of their own in the Kathmandu Valley and buildings have to be rented, they shall be entitled to such a monthly amount as prescribed by the Government of Nepal for the housing rent.

(2) Ministers shall be entitled to half of their monthly remuneration each year for the maintenance and sanitation expenses of governmental buildings and compounds arranged pursuant to Sub-section (1).

(3) A Minister who has permanent abode in and an appropriate building in the Kathmandu Valley shall be provided with One Thousand Five Hundred Rupees per month for sanitation and maintenance of his or her building and compound.

(4) Ministers shall be entitled to enjoy the facility as referred to in this Section until Seven days after the date of expiration of their term of office.

#### 5. Electricity, water and telephone facilities

(1) The Government of Nepal shall bear the tariffs of electricity, water supply and telephone installed in the Prime Minister's quarters and the tariffs of one unit of

electricity, water supply installed in the residence of the other Ministers and tariff of telephone (except fine) call made within Nepal.

Provided that, tariff of telephone call made abroad in the course of government business as specified by the Government of Nepal may be provided to the Prime Minister, Minister for Foreign Affairs, Minister for Home and Minister for Finance.

#### 6. Transport and fuel facility

(1) The following Ministers shall be entitled to the following transport and fuel facility:

(a) Prime Minister:

(1) Two motor vehicles for transportation.

(2) Three Hundred Forty liter petrol per month.

(3) Ten liter mobile in every Three months.

(b) Deputy Prime Minister:

(1) One motor vehicle for transportation.

(2) Two Hundred Sixty Five liter petrol per month.

(3) Five liter mobile in every Three months.

(c) Minister:

(1) One motor vehicle for transportation.

(2) Two Hundred Thirty liter petrol per month.

(3) Five liter mobile in every Three months.

(d) Minister of State and Assistant Minister:

(1) One motor vehicle for transportation.

(2) Two Hundred liter petrol per month.

(3) Five liter mobile in every Three months.

(2) The Central Service Department shall arrange for the repair and maintenance, and grease of, the motor vehicles.

(3) It shall not be required to submit bills and vouchers of petrol and mobile.

(4) The Ministers shall be entitled to the facility receivable pursuant to this Section until Seven days after the date of their respective retirement from office.

### Chapter-3

Daily and Travelling Allowance

#### 7. Daily and travelling allowance

(1) The Ministers shall be entitled to the following daily allowance and travelling allowance on tour within Nepal in the course of government business:

(a) Daily allowance:

(1) Prime Minister Rs. 210.00

(2) Deputy Prime Minister Rs. 195.00

(3) Minister Rs. 190.00

(4) Minister of State Rs. 180.00

(5) Assistant Minister Rs. 145.00

(b) Travelling allowance:

(1) In travelling on foot:

(a) Twenty Five Rupees per Kosh (Two Miles) to the Prime Minister.

(b) Twenty Rupees per Kosh (Two Miles) to the Deputy Prime Minister.

(c) Fifteen Rupees per Kosh (Two Miles) to the Minister.

(d) Ten Rupees per Kosh (Two Miles) to the Minister of State and Assistant Minister.

(2) In travelling by motor vehicle, fuel expenses incurred from one motor vehicle as per bills and vouchers.

(3) In travelling by rail, air conditioned ticket expenses, where such air conditioned ticket is available, and failing it, first class ticket expenses, and worth reserve, if any made, the expenses thereof, chargeable for Two persons, to the Prime Minister, and air conditioned ticket expenses, where such air conditioned ticket is available, and failing it, first class ticket expenses, and worth reserve, if any made, the expenses thereof, chargeable for One person, to the other Ministers.

(4) In travelling by air, ticket expenses chargeable for Two persons, to the Prime Minister, and ticket expenses chargeable for one person to the other Ministers.

(5) In travelling by other motor vehicle, ticket expenses and wages chargeable.

(6) If a Minister considers it necessary, he or she may be accompanied by an employee of the personal secretariat in the tour. The employee

who so go on tour shall be provided with the daily and travelling allowance as per the rules of the Government of Nepal. The number of employees who accompany a Minister on tour may be specified by the Government of Nepal.

(2) In travelling outside Nepal in order to participate in a ceremony, event, conference etc. on behalf of the Government of Nepal or in the course of government business, the Ministers shall be entitled to the following daily allowance and travelling allowance:

(a) Daily allowance: (1) One Hundred Twenty Five US Dollars per day to the Prime Minister and One Hundred US Dollars per day to the other Ministers for all Asian countries other than Japan, South Korea and Philippines.

Provided that, in the case of India, daily allowance shall be provided in Indian currency equivalent to such convertible foreign currency.

(2) One Hundred Twenty Fifty US Dollars per day to the Prime Minister and One Hundred Twenty Five US Dollars per day to the other Ministers for all Asian countries Including Japan, South Korea and Philippines.

(b) Travelling allowance: (1) In travelling by motor vehicle, motor fare as per the bill.

(2) In travelling by rail, air conditioned ticket expenses, where such air conditioned ticket is available, and failing it, first class ticket expenses

chargeable for Two persons, in the case of the Prime Minister, and air conditioned ticket expenses, and failing it, first class ticket expenses chargeable for One person, in the case of the other Ministers.

(3) In travelling by air, first class ticket expenses of scheduled flight chargeable for Two persons, in the case of the Prime Minister, first class ticket expenses of scheduled flight chargeable for One person, in the case of the Deputy Prime Minister, and business class ticket expenses chargeable for One person, in the case of the other Ministers.

(4) In travelling by sea, amount as per the bill, chargeable for One person.

(5) While making a formal visit by the Ministers to any place outside Nepal as a guest of a foreign country, they shall be entitled to the daily allowance at the rate of half the daily allowance receivable pursuant to Clause (a) of Sub-section (2) until they stay in such a foreign country.

#### 8. Other provisions relating to travelling

(1) The Ministers shall be entitled to half the daily allowance for the day on which they return from a visit, and such an allowance shall be half the minimum daily allowance receivable for the place where they stayed on the previous day of return.

(2) The Ministers shall, for the payment of the travelling allowance, fill in the travelling bill and provide required evidences including distance evidence, logbook and receipts, within Thirty Five days after the date of return from a visit.

#### 9. Cloth allowance

(1) In going outside Nepal in order to take part in any ceremony, event, international conference etc. in the course of governmental business or on behalf of the Government of Nepal, the Ministers shall be entitled to the following cloth allowance once in Three years:

(1) Prime Minister Rs. 6,200.00

(2) Deputy Prime Minister Rs. 5,700.00

(3) Minister Rs. 5,670.00

(4) Minister of State Rs. 5,320.00

(5) Assistant Minister Rs. 4,320.00

(2) If, in going outside Nepal pursuant to Sub-section (1), a Minister obtains approval to be accompanied by his wife, the Minister shall be entitled to the cloth allowances for his wife, once in Three years in a sum equivalent to half of his remuneration of One month.

#### 10. Leader allowance

In going abroad as leaders of delegations, the Ministers shall be entitled to an additional leader allowance in a sum that is Twenty Five percent of the daily allowance receivable by them respectively.

#### 11. Insurance facility

In making travel, the Ministers shall be entitled to the amount chargeable for making insurance of Three Hundred Thousand Rupees and to the amount chargeable for airport tax.

#### 12. Treatment facility

(1) The service of one Doctor designated by the Government of Nepal shall be provided to the Ministers collectively.

(2) If the Doctor designated pursuant to Sub-section (1) recommends that a Minister has to undergo treatment upon being admitted to a hospital, and the Ministers admits to the hospital and undergoes treatment, the

Government of Nepal shall bear all expenses incurred in such treatment expenses according to the prescription and bills.

(3) If a Minister has to go aboard for treatment, the Minister shall be entitled to the treatment expenditure prescribed by the Government of Nepal.

### Chapter-4

Miscellaneous

#### 13. Provision of personal secretariat

(1) Provision for such furniture, office materials and employees are required for the personal secretariat during the tenure of Ministers shall be as prescribed.

(2) Each Minister shall, upon the expiration of his or her tenure, return the furniture and permanent goods in-kind as referred to in Sub-section (1).

#### 14. Miscellaneous and guest reception expenses

The amount of miscellaneous and guest reception expenses for the personal secretariat of Minister shall be as prescribed. It shall not be required to submit bills and vouchers of the miscellaneous and guest reception expenses so made.

#### 15. Facility of body guard and guard

The facility of body guards and guards required for the Ministers shall be as prescribed.

#### 16. Commencement of facilities

(1) The Ministers shall be entitled to the facilities receivable pursuant to this Act from the date of taking oath by them respectively.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in Sub-section (1), the Ministers may also obtain the amount of daily and travelling allowance incurred in travelling from outside Kathmandu Valley to Kathmandu for taking an oath of the office of Minister.

#### 27. Repeal and saving

(1) Remuneration and Facilities of the Ministers Rules, 2040 is, hereby, repealed.

(2) The facilities drawn by the Ministers prior to the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been drawn pursuant to this Act.
