Act Number 27 of the year 2050(1993)

…………………..3

An Act Made to Provide for Nepal Scout

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to make the Nepal Scout more competent and organized with a view to having the children and youths of ………..4 Nepal participate in the campaigns in conformity with the principles and norms of the world scout and making them disciplined, self-reliant and of good character through their physical, mental and intellectual development, thereby enhancing the feeling of service, and having them participate in the national building;

Now, therefore, be it enacted by Parliament in the Twenty-second year of the reign of His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev.

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be called as "Nepal Scout Act, 2050(1993)."

(2) It shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act:

(a) "Nepal Scout" means any the Nepal Scout established pursuant to Section 3.

(b) “National Council” means the national council as referred to in Section 7.

(b1) 5 “Regional Council” means the regional council as referred to in Section 9A.

(c) “National Executive Committee” means the national executive committee as referred to in Section 10.

(d) “Advisory Assembly” means the advisory assembly as referred to in Section 7.

(e) “Chief Scout” means the chief scout as referred to in Section 15.

(f) “Chief Commissioner” means the chief commissioner as referred to in Section 16.

(g) “Associate Chief Commissioner” means the associate chief commissioner as referred to in Section 17.

(h) “Commissioner” means the commissioner as referred to in Section 18.

(h1)6 “Member of Nepal Scout” means the member as referred to in Section 18.

(i) “Secretary” means the national secretary7 of the Nepal Scout.

(j) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed or as prescribed by the Rules framed under this Act.

#### 3. Establishment of Nepal Scout

(1) The Nepal Scout shall be established in order to have the children and youths of ………..8 Nepal participate, to the maximum extent possible, in the scout, make them disciplined, self-reliant and of good character through their physical, mental and intellectual development and have them participate in the national building.

(2) The Nepal scout established prior to the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been established under this Act.

(3) The head office of the Nepal Scout shall be situated in the Kathmandu Valley.

(4) The Nepal Scout may, as required, open its offices within………..9 Nepal.

#### 4. Nepal Scout to be autonomous body

(1) The Nepal Scout shall be an autonomous body corporate with perpetual succession.

(2) The Nepal Scout shall have a separate seal of its own for all of its activities.

(3) The Nepal Scout may, like an individual, acquire, use, sell and dispose of or otherwise deal with any movable and immovable property.

(4) The Nepal Scout may, like an individual, sue by its name and be also sued by the same name.

#### 5. Objectives of Nepal Scout

The objectives of the Nepal Scout shall be as follows:

(a) To make children and youths of ……….. 10 Nepal social servers while making them acquainted with the principles, norms and spirit propounded by the world scout.

(b) To make children and youths duty-bound, disciplined, self-reliant, of good character and good citizens through the Nepal Scout.

(c) To mobilize, or cause to be mobilized, the Nepal Scout in rescuing and assisting the persons meeting with accidents and natural calamities.

(d) To perform, or cause to be performed, community service and public interest related works.

(e)11 To launch, or cause to be launched, various programs including elderly education in order to make educated each class and group of the society.

(f) 12 To mobilize, or cause to be mobilized, the youth human resources to operate various public utility programs relating to health, environment, land-conservation and natural resources.

#### 8. Functions, duties and powers of National Council

(1) The functions, duties and powers of the National Council shall be as follows:

(a) To determine the policies and programs of the Nepal Scout.

(b) To give direction and guidance to the National Executive Committee.

(c) To pass the budget of the Nepal Scout.

(d) To frame necessary rules on the operation of the Nepal Scout.

(e) To evaluate, or cause to be evaluated, the work performance of the Nepal Scout.

(f) To discuss and pass the annual programs and reports submitted to the meeting of the National Council.

(g) To appoint and specify the remuneration of the auditor for the auditing of the Nepal Scout.

(h) To perform, or cause to be performed, such other functions as prescribed for the accomplishment of the objectives of the Nepal Scout.

(2) If the National Council thinks necessary, it may seek advice from any native or foreign expert or advisor.

#### 9. Meeting of National Council

(1) The meeting of the National Council shall be held generally once a year.

(2) The meeting of the National Council shall be chaired by the Chairperson of the National Council and, in his or her absence, by the Vice-chairperson of the National Council. The meeting of the National Council shall be chaired by the member selected by the members of the National Council from amongst themselves in the absence of both the Chairperson and the Vice-chairperson.

(3) Other Rules of procedures of the meeting of the National Council shall be as determined by the National Council itself.

(4) The Chairperson, Vice-chairperson, Member and Member Secretary of the National Council may receive the meeting allowance as prescribed for taking part in the meeting.

#### 11. Functions, duties and powers of National Executive Committee

(1) The functions, duties and powers of the National Executive Committee shall be as follows:

(a) To abide by, or cause to be abided by, the directions given by the National Council.

(b) To implement, or cause to be implemented, the decisions of the National Council.

(c) To prepare annual programs, budget, progress details, audit reports and other reports and proposals of the Nepal Scout, and submit the same to the National Council for approval or endorsement.

(d) To operate, protect, look after the movable and immovable properties of the Nepal Scout, and make agreements or contracts.

(e) To receive such financial and other assistance as provided to the Nepal Scout.

(f) To transfer, with the approval of the National Council, the movable and immovable properties of the Nepal Scout.

(g) To make scouting in humanitarian service works including rescue and assistance in natural calamities or accidents.

(h) To form, as required, committees, sub-committees or task forces to act in consonance with the objectives of the Nepal Scout.

(h1)27 To prepare necessary rules on the operation of the Nepal Scout and submit the same to the National Council for approval.

(i) To perform, or cause to be performed, such other functions as may be required for the accomplishment of the objectives of the Nepal Scout.

#### 12. Meeting of National Executive Committee

(1) The meeting of the National Executive Committee shall be held generally Four times a year.

(2) The meeting of the National Executive Committee shall be chaired by the chairperson of the National Executive Committee; and, in his or her absence, the meeting of the National Executive Committee shall be chaired by the Associate Chief Commission.

(3) Other rules of procedures of the meeting of the National Executive Committee shall be as determined by the National Executive Committee itself.

(4) The meeting allowance receivable by the chairperson, member and member secretary of the National Executive Committee for taking part in the meeting shall be as prescribed.

#### 18A. 34Member of Nepal Scout

(1) A person has to possess the following qualification in order to become a member of the Nepal Scout:

(a) Having obtained scout training as prescribed,

(b) Not having obtained membership of any political party, and (c) Not being involved in political activities.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in Clauses (b) and (c) of Sub-section (1), noting contained in these Clauses shall be deemed to bar the institutional representation in the National Council, Regional Council, Executive Council and Advisory Assembly.

(3) Other provisions relating to the Member of the Nepal Scout shall be as prescribed.

#### 20. Provisions relating to employees

(1) The Nepal Scout shall have employees as prescribed.

(2) The positions, qualifications, remuneration and terms and conditions of service and facilities of the employees as prescribed.

#### 21. Fund of Nepal Scout

(1) The Nepal Scout shall have a separate fund of its own; and the fund shall consist of the following amounts:

(a) Amounts received from the Government of Nepal,

(b) Amounts received from foreign institutions, organizations, foreign governments or agencies,

(c) Amounts provided by any persons or organizations in form of donation or aid,

(d) Amounts received as membership fees,

(e) Amounts received from any other source.

(2) In obtaining any amount pursuant to Clause (b) of Sub-section (1), the Nepal Scout shall obtain the approval of the Government of Nepal.

(3) The amounts of the fund of the Nepal Scout shall be deposited in an account opened with a bank within ………..36 Nepal, and the account shall operated as prescribed.

(4) All expenditures to be made by the Nepal Scout shall be chargeable on the fund as referred to in Sub-section (1).

#### 22. Accounts and audit

(1) The accounts of incomes and expenses of the Nepal Scout shall be maintained in accordance with prevailing laws.

(2) The audit of the Nepal Scout shall be performed by an auditor appointed by the National Council.

#### 23. Annual report to be submitted

The Chief Scout shall submit an annual report of the Nepal Scout to the Patron.

#### 24. Power to form committee, sub-committee or task force

(1) The National Executive Committee may, as required, form a committee, sub-committee or task force for the accomplishment of the objectives of the Nepal Scout.

(2) The functions, duties and powers of a committee, sub-committee or task force to be formed pursuant to Sub-section (1) shall be as prescribed by the National Executive Committee.

#### 25. Delegation of powers

(1) The National Council may, as required, delegate any of the powers conferred to it pursuant to this Act or the Rules framed under this Act to the National Executive Committee, Chief Commissioner or Secretary.

(2) The National Executive Committee, Chief Commissioner or Secretary may, as required, delegate any of the powers conferred to it or him or her pursuant to this Act or the Rules framed under this Act to anybody or office-bearer.

#### 26. Liaison with Government of Nepal

In making contact with the Government of Nepal, the Nepal Scout shall make such contact through the Ministry of Education and Sports37.

> 37 Amended by First Amendment.

#### 27. Power to frame Rules

The National Council may frame Rules to implement the objectives of this Act.

#### 28. Saving

(1) All acts done and actions taken prior to the commencement of this Act by the name of Nepal Scout shall be deemed to have been done and taken pursuant to this Act.

(2) All acts to be done and actions to be taken by the National Council and the National Executive Committee shall be done and taken by the Executive Committee of the Nepal Scout existing at the time of the commencement of this Act pending the formation of the said Council and Committee.
