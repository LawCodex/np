# Press and Publication Act, 2009 (1952)

Press and Publication Act, 2009 (1952) Objective and Reasons : Press Act, 2005 (1948) which was issued in our Country, on Baisakh 2, 2005, was repealed by the Press and Publication Registration Act , 2008 (1951) and at present the presspersons are in a dubious situation and confusion and publishing different articles frequently in the bulletin, booklets, Newspapers, pamphlets etc. It may endanger the law and order of the country; therefore, His Majesty has issued this Act to monitor (Rekhdekh) the press and to maintain law and order.

Preamble: Whereas it seems appropriate to issue an Act regarding the press for the bona fide control of the press and on publication;

Be it enacted by His Majesty, on the advice of the Advisors.

Press and Publication Act

#### 1. Short title and extent

This Act may be called as "Press and Publication Act, 2009 (1952).  2. This Act shall come in to force all over Nepal when published in Nepal Gazette.

#### 3. Definitions

Unless the context or subject otherwise provides, the following words (lawaj) shall be construed as following:

(1) "Book" this word means each book, booklet, pamphlet, memorandum (Bibaran patra), Sworlipi, drawing map etc., published, completely or partially, in all languages.

(2) "Document (Likhatpatra)" means any drawing, map, picture or any other direct observation (Chitran).

(3) "Pradhan Nayalaya" this word means the Pradhan Nayalaya of Nepal.

(4) (a) "Magistrate (Badahakim)" this word means the local administrator.

(b) "Chief of office (Hakim)" this word means the local administrator where the system of Magistrate does not exist.

(5) "Newspaper (Samacharpatra) " this word means any contemporary paper (Samayikpatra) in which public news or comments thereof are being published.

(6) "Newsmagazine (Samacharpatrika)" this word means any document which covers the public news, its comment or matters prescribed in Sub-section (1) of Section 5 (Clause (1) Sub-section 5 of Section 3); but which do not fall under the category of Newspapers.

(7) "Press" this word means printing machine, all machineries, tools, office, parts thereof and documents and instruments to be used in producing different copies of the document.

(8) "Printing machines" this word includes engine, machine, type, materials to be used for litho, equipments (Bhandabartan) and instruments to be used for printing.

(9) "Unauthorized item (Anadhikrit Patra)" this word means any newspaper published before preparing a legal document pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act and this word also includes the newspaper published before furnishing the deposit to be deposited pursuant to this Act, if so requested.

(10) "Unauthorized paper (Anadhikrit Patrika)" means any other newsmagazine (Samacharpatrika) except so published by a person who is authorized pursuant to Section 16 of this Act.

(11) "Unauthorized press" means any other unauthorized press which is being kept without preparing a bona fide document pursuant to Section 5 of the Press and Publication Registration Act.

#### 4. Need for Deposit for keeping a press

(1) Out of the owners of the press who has a duty to produce a document (agreement) pursuant to Section 5 of the Press and Publication Act, if the chief of the office or the Magistrate, finds it appropriate, may collect a deposit not exceeding One Thousand Rupees, upon showing reason for the same, from the owners who want to operate a press, within Fifteen days of submission of such document.

(2) In case the owner of the press submits an application to get back the deposit amount, where he or she is not charged with an offence pursuant to Section 5 within Three months of such deposit, such an amount shall be returned to such owner.

(3) In a case, the press, which has failed to deposit the amount or whose deposit has already been returned pursuant to Sub-section (2), publishes any book, newspaper or document using any word, sign or direct shape as referred to in Sub-section (1) of Section 5, the government shall inform the same to the owner of press and collect a deposit amount from Five Hundred Rupees to Three Thousand Rupees.

(4) While issuing a Notice pursuant to Sub-section (2), such a Notice shall be served in such a way that the concerned person shall appear within Fifteen days excluding the time to be taken for the journey. The person having responsibility to furnish deposit shall furnish it within due time.

#### 6. Need for a separate deposit

In a case of confiscation of the deposit, in part or as a whole, pursuant to Sections 5 or 7 of this Act, if someone enters into a bond to keep a press for next time, he/she shall deposit an amount from One Thousand Rupees to Ten Thousand Rupees as it is so ordered in the discretion of the local Magistrate.

#### 7. Power to confiscate the latest deposit and the publication

(1) After submitting deposit pursuant to Section 6, if the Government finds that a press is going to print or publish any word, sign or direct shape in any book, newspaper or any other document, the Government shall issue an order with a written Notice to the owner of such press with a content (a) that the deposit has to be submitted as a whole or in part (b) all the copies of such a newspaper, book or document may be confiscated from anywhere within the country.

(2) After the lapse of Fifteen days of a Notice issued pursuant to Sub-section (1), the bond (document) made pursuant to Section 5 of the Press and Publication Registration Act shall be void.

#### 8. Deposit to be submitted by the publisher of Newspaper

(1) The chief of the office or the Magistrate, if thinks appropriate, may ask for deposit not exceeding One Thousand Rupees, upon mentioning reason thereof, within Fifteen days of entering into the bond from the publisher of

the Newspaper who has to do a bond before the chief of the office or the Magistrate pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act.

(2) Upon furnishing deposit pursuant to Sub-section (1) relating to the Newspaper, the publisher of the Newspaper may request to pay back the deposit amount, in case if any allegation (Bat, Dos, Aarop) is not made by the Government.

(3) In the case, where no deposit has been taken pursuant to this Act or the deposit has been paid back pursuant to Sub-section (2), if the Government considers that a newspaper has published any word, sign or direct shape in a newspaper as mentioned in Sub-section (1) of Section 5, the Government shall issue a written Notice along with the copy of such publication so published, and cause to be deposited an amount of Five Hundred to One Thousand Rupees from the publisher to the chief of the office or the Magistrate of the area where from such a Newspaper is being published.

(4) In the course of issuing Notice pursuant to Sub-section (2), a Notice shall be issued giving Fifteen days to appear excluding the time to be taken for journey. A person, who has an obligation to furnish deposit, shall have to furnish it within the time frame.

#### 9. Authority to confiscate deposit in a special case

(1) In a case, any word, sign or direct shape as referred to in Section 5 is published in a newspaper from which a deposit is being ordered to be collected, the Government upon indicating such word, sign or shape, shall issue a written Notice and,

(a) may confiscate the deposit amount as a whole or partially,

(b) may cancel (revoke) the bond done by the publisher of the Newspaper pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act and confiscate the newspaper whenever it is found in Nepal, in case deposit has not been collected.

(2) In case the deposit, as a whole or partially, is confiscated, the bond done by the publisher of the Newspaper pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act, shall be considered void (revoked) after the expiry of Fifteen days of the Notice issued pursuant to Sub-section (1).

#### 10. Amount to be deposited afterwards

(1) After the confiscation of a deposit, as a whole or partially, pursuant to Section 9 or 11 of this Act, if a person enters into a bond to become a publisher of such or any other similar Newspaper pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act, he/she shall deposit an amount from One Thousand Rupees to Ten Thousand Rupees, on the discretion of the local chief of office or Magistrate.

(2) If the deposit is confiscated partially pursuant to Section 9 or 11, the amount so furnished may be counted for the due amount to be deposited later on pursuant to Sub-section (1).

#### 11. Confiscation of latest deposit and newspaper

(1) In a case , even after advancement of deposit pursuant to Section 10, if the government considers that any word, sign or direct shape has been used in the Newspaper, the Government shall inform to the publisher that,-

(a) Such a deposit as a whole or in part, and

(b) All the copies of Newspaper may be confiscated by an order of the Government wherever it is in found in the country.

(2) After the expiry of Fifteen days from the date of Notice pursuant to Sub-section (1), the bond entered into by the publisher of the newspaper pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act shall be deemed void (revoked).

#### 12. Penalty to a person keeping Press or Publishing Newspaper

(1) In the course of taking action to a person who keeps a press to print books or newspaper or magazine (Patra Patrika) without furnishing the deposit as requested by the local chief of office or the Magistrate pursuant to Section 4 or 6 and if someone is found guilty in the offence so mentioned, such a person shall be punished as if he/she has violated Section 5 of the Press and Publication Registration Act.

(2) In the course of taking action for publishing a Newspaper without advancing deposit or with a knowledge that the deposit has not been advanced as requested by the local chief of office or Magistrate pursuant to Section 8 or 10, and if someone is found guilty in the offence so mentioned, such a person shall be punished as if he/she has violated Section 5 of the Press and Publication Registration Act.

#### 13. Consequence of not furnishing deposit

(1) In a case where a press, having an obligation to furnish deposit fails to submit the deposit within the time frame as referred to in Section 4; after the expiry of such time frame, if fails to produce deposit as referred to in Section 6, no press shall print any book, newspaper and any other document without furnishing such deposit.

(2) In the case of violation of Sub-section (1), the Government may, upon issuing a written Notice, confiscate the press.

(3) In a case, where the publisher has to furnish deposit as referred to in Section 8, does not furnish it with in the time frame, the bond entered into pursuant to Section 6 of the Press and Publication Registration Act shall be deemed void.

#### 14. Release of deposit in some cases

If a person, who has furnished deposit for a press, gives up such function or such a person enters into a bond pursuant to Section 9 of the Press and Publication Registration Act upon being a publisher, files an application before the local chief of office or Magistrate to release such deposit, he/she shall, upon conducting an inquiry into the matter, if reaches to a conclusion that it is appropriate to release the deposit, and there is no objection under any other Sections of this Act, release the deposit.

#### 15. Power to search

Upon making a decision regarding confiscation of any press or newspaper, book or copies of documents by the Government pursuant to Sections 5, 7, 9, 11, and 12, the Government may order to the Magistrate to depute a police officer, at least a Sub-inspector, to hold (Rokka) and control the material, so ordered for confiscation and to search the place where such material is laying, where there is reasonable doubt of laying such materials, or where the copies of such newspaper or any documents are being sold, distributed or exhibited in the market or so kept.

#### 16. Power to give authority to publish a newspaper

(1) The Magistrate may, along with terms and conditions in his/her discretion upon quoting his/her name, authorize a person to publish a newspaper at a time or in appropriate intervals.

(2) The Magistrate may withdraw the order issued pursuant to Sub- Section (1) at any time.

(3) The person so authorized pursuant to Sub-section (1) shall be given a copy of such letter of authorization.

#### 17. Authority to confiscate and destroy the unauthorized newspaper or magazines

(1) Any police officer or a person so authorized by the Government may confiscate any unauthorized newspaper wherever he/she finds it.

(2) If the chief of office or Magistrate knows or has a reasonable doubt of being any unauthorized newspaper or magazine in any place, he/she upon issuing a written order to a police officer, at least Sub-inspector, authorize to search such place accordingly and the officer may confiscate the same if found unauthorized.

(3) All the documents so confiscated as referred to in Sub-section (1) or (2), shall be produced before the local chief of office or Magistrate who issued such an order, as soon a possible.

(4) If any document, out of the confiscated documents, is found unauthorized, in the opinion of the chief of office or Magistrate or Kachahari, such documents may be destroyed, and if it is not found unauthorized it shall be returned to the concerned person. In case the owner of such documents could not be found, the details of those documents shall be published in the gazette as a Notice.

#### 18. Authority to arrest the person publishing unauthorized Newspapers, Journals and to confiscate unauthorized press

(1) The chief of office or the Magistrate, upon issuing a written order, if there is reasonable ground of publishing unauthorized newspaper from an unauthorized press in his/her jurisdiction, may authorize to a police officer, at least Sub-inspector, to enter into and search the place where there is such press or there is reasonable doubt of being such press. If the police officer

so authorized finds in his/her discretion that the press or the printed materials are unauthorized, he/she may take into control (Pakdau) the press or the documents.

(2) The police officer shall give a report to the office issuing such an order and produce all the materials taken into control as soon as possible to such office. If the press so taken into control could not be produced easily as a whole, the press shall be hold (Rokka) and major parts of the press shall be produced before the Court/Bench (Kachahari).

(3) If the office upon an inquiry finds the press unauthorized so taken into custody (Kabja) for publishing unauthorized newspaper, such an office, by a written order, confiscate the press in a due manner. If it is not found unauthorized it shall be returned to the concerned person. If the owner could not be recognized, the details of the materials shall be published in the Gazette.

(4) In relation the documents produced pursuant to this Section, the office shall take action as referred to in Sub-section (4) of Section 17.

#### 19. Authority to hold any published book etc. imported from a foreign country

(1) Unauthorized newspaper, magazines shall not be imported through the postal service or the individual career (Bharia).

(2) In case the customs office has any doubt regarding the newspaper, book or document as referred to in Section 5 for being in the parcel arrived from water course, land or arial route, the customs officer shall hold the parcel and forward some of the copies of the same along with a referral letter (Chalani) to the chief of office or Magistrate.

#### 20. Authority to hold the goods to be forwarded from the postal service

Any chief of post office, may hold any parcel or bag (Poka) if there is any doubt for being a newspaper, book or document containing any

word, sign or direct shape as referred to in Sub-section (1) of Section 5 or any other unauthorized newspaper, except the letter or parcel already forwarded in the postal service, and submit such parcel or bag, so withheld, to the chief of office or Magistrate or prescribed officer.

#### 21. Authority to confiscate any published Book etc. and issue searching order (search warrant) thereof

In case any newspaper, book or document contains any word, sign or direct shape as referred to in Sub-section (1) of Section 5, wherever it may have been printed, the Government shall publish a Notice for the confiscation of such newspaper, book or document along with the reason for the same, in the Nepal Gazette and any police officer may take into control such newspaper, book or document from any place within Nepal, and the chief of office or Magistrate may issue a written order to a police officer, at least up to the rank of Sub-inspector, to enter into or search any place if there is reasonable doubt for being or possibility of being such newspaper or book or document.

#### 22. Regarding petition (Ujuri Bintipatra) to be filed before the Pradhan Nayalaya for the revocation of confiscation order

(1) The owner of the press when an order has been issued to produce deposit pursuant to Sub-section (3) of Section 4 or the publisher who has been ordered to furnish deposit pursuant to Sub-section (3) of Section 8 or the owner of the goods or materials which is going to be confiscated pursuant to Sections 5, 7, 9 and 21, may file a petition (Darkhasta) before the Pradhan Nayalaya for the revocation of such an order, and the Pradhan Nayalaya shall also make decision whether there exists the word, sign or direct shape as referred in Sub-section (1) of Section 5 or not.

(2) The owner of the press shall, in a case where an order of confiscation is being received pursuant to Sub-section (2) of Section 13 for

the violation of Sub-section (1) of the same Section, file a petition within Two months of such an order upon indicating that no such act has been committed through the press and requesting for the revocation of such order before the Pradhan Nayalaya.

#### 23. Hearing by a Special Bench

All such petitions shall be heard (Tahakikat) and decided by a Special Bench comprising Three judges of the Pradhan Nayalaya.

#### 24. Order of the Special Bench to revoke the confiscation order

(1) Upon receiving a petition field pursuant to Sub-section (1) of Section 22, if the Special Bench does not find that word, sign or direct shape used in the newspaper, book or document, which resulted confiscation of deposit, violating of Sub-Section (1) of Section 5, such a Bench may revoke such an order.

(2) In a case a petition is filed pursuant to Sub-section (2) of Section 22 and the Special Bench reaches to a conclusion that the press was not being operated upon violating Sub-section (1) of Section 13, such a Bench may revoke that order.

#### 25. Evidence to show the type and tendency (Prabriti) of the newspaper

In the course of taking action on the petition (Ujuri/Bintipatra) pursuant to Sub-section (1) of Section 22, where a charge has been made to confiscate the deposit for using the word, sign or direct shape, one may produce a copy of newspaper as a proof, which has published that types of word, signs or direct shapes, after the commencement of this Act.

#### 26. Action of Pradhan Nayalaya

Pradhan Nayalaya shall, as soon as possible, frame Rules regarding the expenses to be charged in the course of

taking action and execution of the decision. Until such a Rule is framed it shall be done pursuant to the prevailing Act and Sawal.

#### 27. Information to be given

Every information pursuant to this Act shall be given to the chief of office or Magistrate and he/she shall cause to serve Summon/Notice in a bona fide manner (duly). If a Summon/Notice could not be served even putting sufficient effort, the office deputed for serving the Summon/Notice shall, in the case of a press, fix (Tasne) a copy of the Notice in a main public place where the press is being located as referred to in the concerned Section of the Press and Publication Registration Act and in the case of the publisher of a newspaper, in a main public place where from the newspaper is published, as mentioned in his/her application, as referred to in Section 5 of the same Act; and a Notice so served shall deem valid.

#### 28. Authority to be continued

(1) The Notice of confiscation pursuant to this Act shall be deemed to be irrefutable (Akatya) proof of confiscation.

(2) In this process, after filing a petition pursuant to Section 23, no Court other than Pradhan Nayalaya shall proceed any action.

(3) If a person takes or attempts to take any action pursuant to this Act in a good faith, no civil or criminal action shall be taken against such person except provided in this Act.

#### 29. Other Acts may be applicable

If someone commits any act not be done or omits any act to be done which is considered crime pursuant to this Act it shall not be considered causing any hindrance to file a complaint pursuant to any other Act.

#### 30. Document to be prepared before the Kachahari of the magistrate pursuant to the Press and Publication Registration Act, 2008

The documents pursuant to Sections 5, 6 and 9 of the Press and Publication Registration Act shall be prepared by the designated officer in the Sadar (centre) and before the Bench(Ijlas)of chief of office or Magistrate in Mofasal (outside of the Kathmandu Valley).

Miscellaneous

31. A person, who possess any book, map or print (Chhapa) etc. published in an unauthorized manner, shall submit it before the local chief of office or Magistrate, within Thirty Five days of a Notification by the Government, in the Gazette. A person who violates this provision shall be liable for a fine upto One Thousand rupees or an imprisonment upto Six months or the both.

32. A person, who transports, imports or exports unauthorized book, map or print through the post or aeroplane or boat, shall be liable for an imprisonment upto Six months or a fine upto One Thousand Rupees or the both by the local chief of office or Magistrate.

33. The Government may frame necessary Rules to meet the objectives of this Act.

34. The Government, by publishing a Notification in the Nepal Gazette, may make alteration/amendment in this Act and may or may not implement this Act as a whole or in part for a specific period of time.

35. In a case where an unauthorized material is published without informing the owner or the manger of the Press Company, the publisher, printer or editor whosever is responsible for such a publication shall be liable for a fine upto One Thousand Rupees or an imprisonment for a period upto Six months for each commission by the local chief of office or the Magistrate.

36. The Government may, in the public interest, withhold any news, comment and publication.

37. Whosoever knowingly sales, distributes publishes, exhibits unauthorized book, newspaper, journal (Patra Patrika), map etc. shall be liable for an imprisonment upto Six months or a fine upto Fifty Rupees or the both.
