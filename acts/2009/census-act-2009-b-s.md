# Census Act, 2009

Census Act, 2009

Whereas, it is expedient to enact some laws to conduct Census, the following Adiniyams are being enacted-1. This Act shall be called the “Census Act, 2009”.

2. The Government may, if it finds necessary and appropriate, make public its intent to conduct Census all over the country or any part of it, and thereafter, the task of Census shall be commenced.

3. (a) An officer appointed by the line (Taluk) Ministry shall supervise (Dekhrekh) all activities relating to Census;

(b) The officer designated for an area (Ilaka) to conduct the activities of Census shall perform the task of Census in the areas so divided for the purpose of Census; and there shall be an assistant officer to help the officer.

(c) The Inspector appointed in each local unit within an area shall supervise the activities of Census.

4. Each officer, assistant officer or inspector, in his /her area, may issue written order to all of the-

(a) Thari, Mukhia, Patawari and Jimmawal (local heads) (b) Jamindar (Landlord) and Raiti (Tenant) (c) Members of local Panchayat (d) Employees of Mill, Factory, Karkhana, Hotel, School etc. in relation to the task of Census and such persons shall extend their necessary help in conducting the task of Census following such an order.

5. The inspector of the local unit shall appoint those persons who shall really take Census and such appointees shall be called the enumerator (Ganak) 6. (a) Each Census officer may ask necessary questions to the persons who come under his/her jurisdiction. Government shall publish the list of such questions in the Gazette.

(b) Every one, who is asked such questions, shall answer correctly as far he/she knows it.

Provided that, no one shall be compelled to take the name of his wife or husband or whose name is not taken by them. One who can write may give such name in writing.

#### 7. (a) No news (information) received in the course of Census shall be used for any other purpose

No one, except the employees involved in the process of census, shall be entitled to see the list, inventory (Lagat) or register prepared in the course of census.

(b) No information (matter) written in the census list, inventory or register shall be produced as an evidence in a law suit before any court except in a case filed pursuant to this Act.

8. (a) Every enumerator (Ganak) or any other person who denies to follow the order issued pursuant this Act or who does not show due care (Sabdhani) or promptness (Tatparata); or (b) Every enumerator (Ganak) who knowingly asks improper or objectionable (aapatijank) question, or knowingly writes false answer or makes public any information received in the course of census without the approval of the Government; or

(c) If, any employee engaged in the task of census, destroys or conceals or harms any document relating to census or uses such document in such a manner that it may lead to a false result; or (d) If, anyone denies to answer any question which is mandatory to answer pursuant to Section 6 of this Act or does not answer correctly as far he/she knows; or (e) If, a person joins to the post of census officer through any illegal means, such a person shall be liable for a fine up to Two Hundred Rupees and he/she shall be liable for an imprisonment of One month, in addition to such fine, if he/she found guilty for violating Sub-sections (Clauses) (b) and (c).

9. (a) If a person publishes any matter not to help in the function of census or causes any obstacle in this regard; or (b) If an inspector, in his/her local unit, does not show promptness (Tatparta) or shows negligence (asaabdhani) in abiding by the order issued in the course of census, such a person shall be liable for a fine up to Four Hundred Rupees or an imprisonment up to Two months or the both.

10. The government, if so required, may frame necessary Rules to meet the objectives of this Act.
