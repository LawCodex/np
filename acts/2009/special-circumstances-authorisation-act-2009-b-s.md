# Special Circumstances Authorisation Act, 2009

www.lawcommission.gov.np

Special Circumstances Authorisation Act, 2009

Preamble Whereas, it is expedient to make immediate arrangements not to cause any delay or lawlessness (Khukulopan) in the governance at the serious and critical (Najuk) condition of the country, and Whereas, the cabinet has already submitted its resignation and there seems no possibility of constituting an effective government to work for the welfare of the people upon enhancing harmonious relation amongst the people, and Whereas, His Majesty has expressed His desire and commitment to govern the Nation with the help of the Advisors in a Royal Message delivered on Thursday, 30th Shrawan, 2009.

Be, it enacted by His Majesty on the advice of the Royal Advisors.

1. (1) This Act shall be called as "Special Circumstances Authorisation Act, 2009".

(2) This Act shall come into force from 30th Shrawan, 2009 B.S.

(3) This Act shall extend throughout Nepal.

2. This Act shall remain enforced until His Majesty receives help and assistance of a cabinet which can maintain harmonious relation and strengthen stability, and which can fulfill the need of the general people being active, effective and securing trust and good faith of the people.

3. All the words and phrases used in this Act shall be construed as referred to in the Nepal Antarim Shasan Bidhan, 2007 (Interim Constitution, 2007).

4. Chapters 1 and 2 Part 3 of the Nepal Antarim Shasan Bidhan, 2007 shall remain in a suspended animation (postponed).

#### 5. (1) Executive powers of the country has been transferred to His Majesty the King

Such powers shall be exercised by His Majesty by Himself

or through His subordinates (Karmachari) pursuant to the Rules framed by His Majesty for this purpose.

(2) His Majesty, for his assistance, shall appoint consultants (Paramarshadata) or Advisors (Sallahakar) or the both.

(3) Functions and Duties of the Consultants and Advisors shall be prescribed in the command (Aagayan) of His Majesty to be issued from time to time.

(4) The meaning of the phrase- 'on the advise of the Minister or Cabinet'-referred to in the Nepal Antarim Shasan Bidhan, 2007 or any other prevailing Acts/Sawals/Rules etc., for performing any act by His Majesty shall be construed as -'His Majesty shall act'.

6. (1) His Majesty the King, at any time and any circumstance, may issue any types of Act, Sawal or Ordinance, as he deems necessary.

(2) The Act issued pursuant to this Section 6 shall be enforced in the country as good as any other Act.

(3) The Ordinance issued pursuant to this Section 6 shall be enforced in the country as good as any other Act;

Provided that, such an ordinance -

(a) Shall be repealed (Antaya) along with this Act,

(b) His Majesty may repeal such an Ordinance at any time.

7. To meet the objectives of this Act, His Majesty may frame Sawal and Rules.

8. Any provision of the Nepal Antarim Shasan Bidhan, 2007 or any other Act, Sawal or Rules which contradicts with or which is repugnant to the provisions of this Act shall be postponed (in a suspended animation) till the date this Act is in force.

Authenticated by a Royal Seal 1st Bhadra 2009 B.S.
