# Nepal Citizenship Act, 2009

www.lawcommission.gov.np

Nepal Citizenship Act, 2009 His Majesty the King has enacted the following Nepali Citizenship Act on the advice of the cabinet-1. (a) This Act shall be called "Nepali Citizenship Act, 2009".

(b) This Act shall come into force throughout Nepal.

(c) This Act shall come in to force immediately.

2. Every person, who meets the following terms and conditions upon being a resident (Adhibasi) of Nepal shall be considered to be a citizen of Nepal-

(a) One who was born in Nepal.

(b) One of whose parents was born in Nepal. A person with an object of a permanent domicile ipso facto resides within Nepal along with the family shall be considered to be the Adhivasi of Nepal.

3. A woman who has a marital relations with a Nepali Citizen pursuant to the Nepalese law and custom shall be considered to be a Nepali Citizen.

4. Any person falling under any of the categories as following may file an application before the Authority as prescribed in the Rules and receive the citizenship of Nepal-

(a) The children born in Nepal or the children who were born in a foreign country from the parents who have not received citizenship from such a foreign country where they are residing.

(b) A woman, whose husband has died or whose marriage has been broken or who has divorced or who is being left by her husband (Paritayakta) or separated for not taking care by her husband, who was born from the Nepali Citizen parents and married with a foreign person pursuant to Section 3.

(c) A person, who left Nepal and resided in a foreign country and received citizenship from such a country, and their children, who continuously reside/s upto One year within Nepal.

(d) A person who is residing in Nepal since at least Five consecutive years.

5. A person shall be issued a certificate (Ijajatpatra) only after an oath is taken as follows- I, hereby, take an oath that I shall accept the sovereignty (Aadhipataya) of Nepal and reside here upon abiding by the laws and traditions and customs (Ritiriwaj) and I shall not commit any act against Nepal.

6. All the persons who receive Nepali Ijjajatpatra (citizenship certificate) shall have equal rights, claims and responsibilities as good as a Nepali Citizen from the date of receiving such a certificate and they shall be considered to be the citizen of Nepal 7. A person, who is not entitled to receive a citizenship certificate as referred to in Sub-sections (Clauses) of Section 4, receives a right of citizenship based on any false statement or fraudulent act (Phareb), Government shall revoke all the rights and impose a fine upto One Thousand Rupees on its discretion. In such a case, the citizenship shall be deemed to have been revoked (void) from the very date of its issuance.

8. A Nepali, who has received the citizenship of any foreign country, shall not be entitled to be continued as a citizen of Nepal.

9. No person shall be entitled to receive the responsibility of posts of Prime Minister, Minister or Chief of the Army Staff, unless it expires at least Ten years after receiving the citizenship.

10. As referred to in the Treaty concluded with the Government of Bhot the male child born from the Bhoteni wife while a Nepali male was/is residing in Bhot shall be the Nepali citizen and female child so born shall be a citizen of Bhot.

11. Government of Nepal may frame Rules regarding the application to be submitted to receive the citizenship and procedure therefor.
