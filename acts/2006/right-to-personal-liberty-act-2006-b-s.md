# Right to Personal Liberty Act, 2006

Right to Personal Liberty Act, 2006

An Act drafted and forwarded by a legislative committee (Vaidhanik Samiti, and reported (Jaheri) by the Minister and Commander-in-Chief, West Zone Commanding General, East Zone Command General, other Generals and Nayab Badaguruju (Deputy priest) witnessed by the committee officers and consented by us in accordance with the Baidhanik Kanoon, Be, it enacted (by His Highness, Shree Teen) the following Right to Personal Liberty Act and enforce throughout Nepal.

Followings 1. This Act shall be called as the "Right to Personal Liberty Act".

2. This Act shall come into force throughout Nepal from 2nd of Kartik, 2006 B.S. (Date of issuance 2006-6-2-11).

#### 3. No office or a person authorised by an Act or Sawal shall arrest, imprison or take into custody anyone with or without using any physical force who have not committed any act against the Act/Sawal

If it is so done, it shall be considered to be an offence.

4. Except it is so authorised to detain by an authority of Act or Sawal, if someone arrests imprisons or detains to anybody else, the person so arrested, imprisoned or detained him/herself wants to file a case (Nales/Ujur) to release him/herself in a written form or verbally (Mukhjamani) upon mentioning the details of the officer or person who has illegally arrested, imprisoned or detained and place of such detention, may file a case before the Pradhann Nayalaya if its is in the centre (Sadar) and before the Court of Amini in the case of Mofsal (outside the Kathmandu valley). If someone appears to file a case in Mukhjamani (verbally) note and record it accordingly.

5. If, a case is filed pursuant to Section 4, the Pradhan Nayalaya or the nearby court or Amini finds somebody being detained as mentioned in the application; it shall issue a Notice (Purji) in the name of an office or the person who has so detained, to appear before it along with the detainee, and shall take Thado Karbahi (immediate action) to release such a detainee from the custody as soon as possible, if it is found to have been detained illegally and punish a person who so detains as referred to in the Chapter of 'To take into illegal detention of the Act (Muluki Ain).

6. In case any office or court imposes any punishment or takes action more than that has been referred to in the Act or Sawal, it (Pradhan Nayalaya/Court/Amini) shall issue an order to rectify the punishment/action and shall also punish who violated the law.

7. Upon being released after the Thado Karbahi pursuant to Section 5, the person who was arrested or imprisoned or detained illegally, may file a complaint (Nales, Ujuri) before the court or Amini of his/her locality within Fifteen days of such decision for the release, for a compensation (Harjana) as per his/her prestige (Ijjat) against the offender who has been so declared guilty as referred to in Section 3. If such a complaint is filed, the concerned court or Amini shall also initiate the proceeding and cause to recover (realize) compensation as so proved.

8. If a person, convicted as an offender for committing an offence as referred to in Sections 5 and 6 hereinabove, is found to have committed such offence by an order of a person to whom he/she has to pay respect (Mannu parne) or master, such a person shall be liable for the half of the punishment.

Provided that, if an offence as referred to in Sections 5 and 6 is committed on the order of a person he/she has to pay respect to execute the order of the Government he/she shall be liable for the punishment as prescribed by the Act.

9. If, in a lawsuit in which one has to be taken into custody in the course of trial and he/she is not convicted upon analysing the proof and evidence within Three months of such custody, the person in such custody may file complaint (Ujur) before the appellate jurisdiction, custody house, jail or the office having supervisory authority to cause it in accordance with the Act; such offices shall immediately accept such complaint and proceed and decide in accordance with the Act. Not to follow this Section shall result an offence.

10. In a case, one has to arrest or take into control to a person by an order of an office or a court, he/she shall compulsorily carry the legal notice (Purji) or warrant.

#### 11. If a Hulia of a person to be arrested for any reason has been published in a public Notice (Istihar) or in a Newspaper such a peson may be arrested without a warrant or summon (Pakraupurji)

Except them no one shall be arrested without a summon (Purji) or warrant.

12. No one shall be punished (Bat) for defying any arrest without a lawful warrant.

13. All the employee (Kamdar), Staff (Karinda), officer, Hudda, soldier (Sipahi) or police shall put on the uniform (Luga), turban (Feta), badge, belt (Peti), Chapras or any other types of Government logo, sign (Nissa, Chinha) so prescribed by the Act or Sawal who has a duty to arrest or take into custody to any other person and shall show the lawful arrest warrant issued by an office or the court. No one shall be punished (Dos) for defying any arrest without such uniform, turban, badge, belt, Chapras etc. as prescribed in the law (Dastur Maphik).

14. In a case where one has to be arrested pursuant to the Act or Sawal, such a person shall be produced before the concerned office or court within Twelve hours of after such arrest, excluding the time taken for the journey.

If a person causes any delay or expires the time limit of Twelve hours to produce the detainee before the office or court, without any reasonable grounds, he/she shall be liable for a penalty.

15. When a person so arrested pursuant to the Act, Sawal or order (Urdi) is produced before the concerned Authority, such an Authority shall initiate action to take into custody upon issuing Custody-Notice (Thunuwa Purji) in case he/she has to be detained or shall release on bail (Jamanat) as prescribed by the Act. To detain someone without taking any action shall be punishable as referred to in Section 3.

16. In case the office/court or chief of office or officer does not proceed any action and detains somebody without any reason, the detainee, his/her attorney or successor (Haquewala) may file a petition (Darkhasta) as referred to in Section 4.

17. While filing a petition (Nales, Ujuri) pursuant to Section 16, such a petition shall be filed before the Appeal Hearing office if someone is detained on behalf of the court; and it shall be filed before the case Hearing superior (Taluk) office or Gauda, Gowshwara in case someone was detained or caused for the same by the Shresta office.

18. If, the detainee (Thunai magne), could not file a petition while he/she is in the custody, he/she may file it after his/her release from the custody, within the time frame as prescribed in the Act, as referred to in the foregoing Sections.

#### 19. No one shall be punished for an act which was not punishable by the prevailing law when the act was committed

No one shall be accused in other matters as so stated and no one shall be subjected to a punishment greater than that prescribed by the law in force at the time of offence.

20. No one shall be punished by the same office/court, even if the penalty imposed against such person is insufficient and such a person has to be

imposed additional penalties for the same offence once he/she is so punished.

21. The citizens (Raiti) of Nepal shall be entitled to act as following, upon abiding by the Act and Sawal, within the territory of Nepal:

(a) To run a trade or to perform any business (Kamkaj).

(b) To receive or dispose of the property (Jayajetha).

(c) To reside in any place accommodating a house holding (Gharbar).

(d) To move freely here and there without any restrictions.
