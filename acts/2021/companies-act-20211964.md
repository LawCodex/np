### Chapter-1

Preliminary

#### 1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called the "Companies Act 2021(1964)".

(2) It shall extent to the whole of the Kingdome of Nepal.

(3) It shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act,

(a) “Company” means a Company incorporated under this Act as a limited company.

(b) “Private Company” means any Company incorporated under this Act with the restriction that 1it shall not issue invitations to the public to subscribe to its shares and debentures, that any transfer of its shares or debentures by way of security or otherwise shall not be made to persons other than to shareholders without the approval of the Board of Directors, and that the total number of its shareholders shall not exceed Fifty.

(c) “Public Company” means a Company other than a Private Company.

(d) “Subsidiary Company" means a Company which is controlled by principal Company 2................upon holding more than Fifty percent shares.

(e) “Foreign Company” means any Company incorporated in a foreign country.

(f) “Share” means a portion of the share capital or stock of a Company. <sup>3</sup>(f1) “Bonus Share” means a share issued as an additional share to the Shareholders by capitalizing the surplus from the profit or the reserve fund of a company, and this term includes the increase of the paid up capital of the share by capitalizing the surplus or reserve fund.

(g) “Preference Share” means a share having preference over ordinary shares.

(h) “Ordinary Share” means a share entitling its holder to dividends from the profits after the allocation of dividends to the holders of preference shares under this Act.

(i) “Memorandum” means the Memorandum of a Company.

(j) “Articles” means the Articles (of Association) of a Company.

(k) “Prospectus” means a Prospectus to be published by a Company pursuant to this Act.

(l) “Shareholder” means a person who owns any share of the Company.

(m) “Debenture” means any document issued by a Company under this Act pledging its assets for loans receivable.

(n) “Share Certificate” means the certificate issued by the Company to a person who purchases a share or shares.

(o) “Underwriting of Shares” means the undertaking by a person to purchase any shares when not sold to any other person.

(p) “Special Resolution” means a resolution which is moved as special resolution pursuant to this Act.

(q) “Director” means a Director of a Company appointed pursuant to the provisions of this Act and this term also includes an alternate Director. <sup>4</sup>(q1) “Managing Director” means the managing director of a Company appointed pursuant to Section 69A. <sup>5</sup>(q2) “Company Secretary” means the Company Secretary appointed pursuant to Section 69B.

(r) “Managing Agent” means a Managing Agent appointed pursuant to this Act. <sup>6</sup>(r1) “Securities” means the shares, bonds, debentures, stocks or loans issued by a Company and this term also includes the receipt relating to deposits of securities as well as the rights and interests in securities.

(s) “Concerned Department” means the Department of Industry of Government of Nepal or any other Department or office which may be designated by Government of Nepal.

#### 3. Incorporation of Company

(1) Persons desirous of undertaking any economic entrepreneurship on the basis of limited liability after raising capital through the sale of shears shall for this purpose incorporate (from) a company pursuant to of this Act.

(2) No Company shall be incorporated unless the minimum number of promoters is 7Three in the case of a Private Company and Seven in the case of a Public Company.

(3) No Company shall be incorporated unless a person (or some persons) has undertaken to subscribe at list Thirty percent of its issued Capital. In case a Company is incorporated pursuant to such undertaking

but at least Thirty percent of the issued Capital is not subscribed within Three months from the date of the incorporation, the incorporation shall ipso facto be null and void.

(4) No new Company shall be incorporated with a name similar to (resemblance) that of a Company which has previously been registered.

(5) In case the concerted Department deems any proposed name inappropriate or undesirable, may refuse to permit the Company to be incorporated under such name.

(6) Unless otherwise provided for in the Act, the maximum liability of the promoter or shareholder of any Company in respect to its business shall be limited to the (nominal or face) value of the shares which he/she has undertaken to subscribe.

(7) The word "Limited'' shall be added to the name of a public Company , and the words "Private Limited" to the name of a Private company, incorporated under this Act.

(8) No firm, not incorporated as a Limited Company under this Act, shall use the word" Limited" after its name.

#### 17. Rights and Liabilities under this Act not to be affected by anything contained in the Prospectus

Nothing contained in the Prospectus shall affect the rights and liabilities of any persons pursuant to the provisions of this Act.

#### 18. Responsibilities for Particulars contained in Prospectus

The promoters and the directors signing the Memorandum shall be held responsible for the matters contained in the Prospectus. If the published Prospectus contains false statements, made deliberately or with mala fide intentions, the said promoters and directors shall be personally liable to pay compensations to any person for any loss he/she may have sustained by purchasing shares or debentures on the faith of that Prospectus.

Provided that, the promoter or director who resigns before the publication of the Prospectus, or who after having come to know about any false statement contained there-if publishes a notice to that effect for the information of the public, prior to the sale or allotment of shares or debentures, shall not be so liable.

#### 19. Issue of Duplicate Copies

If a share holder or any other concerned person demands a copy or printed duplicate of the Memorandum, the Articles or the Prospectus, it shall be provided to him/her on payment of One Rupee per copy.

#### 20. Application for Shares

(1) Persons desirous of purchasing shares of a Company shall submit an application as set out in form No. (1) of Schedule-1.

(2) The applicant shall submit, together with his/her application, an advance of at lest Ten percent of the value of the shares applied for by him/her. Any application which is not so accompanied by such an advance shall be rejected.

#### 21. Allotment of Shares

(1) Shares shall be allotted in a way that all the applicants may receive it as far as possible, within Six months after the registration of the Company, and a notice to that effect shall thereupon be sent to the shareholders as set out in form No (2) of Schedule-1.

Provided that, such an allotment shall not be made until Fifty percent of the issued share capital as stated in the Memorandum is subscribed.

(2) If allotment could not be made within Six months from the date of the registration of the Company for the reasons stated in Sub-section (1), and the promoters submit an application explaining the reason for non-allotment of shares within Seven days after the expiry of that time limit, the concerned Department may extend the time limit by a period of upto Six months, if it so considers proper.

(3) Any amount paid on share, plus interest calculated at 24Ten percent per annum from the date when such amount was received shall be refunded to the persons who have paid such advance installment within a period of Thirty Five days from either of the following dates. If the time limit was extended by the concerned Department and allotment

could not be made even within that time limit, at the date of the expiry of the time limit so extended, and if the time limit was not extended by the concerned Department, at the date of the expiry of the Six months time limit referred to in Sub-section (1). If the funds are insufficient to make such repayment the balance which remains outstanding shall be personally repaid by the promoters.

(4) Where an application is received for extension of time limit under Sub-section (2), the concerned Department shall communicate its decision within Fifteen days.

#### 28. Refusal to register notice of Transfer of Shares

(1) The company may refuse to record any pledge or if title is relinquished, the transfer in respect of shares in the following circumstances:-

(a) In case the shares are not fully paid up;

(b) In case the deed and the share certificate to be submitted under Section 27 are not submitted together with the application;

(c) In case the Articles of the Company contain an restriction regarding the transfer of shares;

(d) In case fees are not paid under Sub-section (5) of Section 27.

(2) If any application for recording a pledge or transfer in respect of shares is rejected under Sub-section (1), both the transferor and the transferee of the shares shall be notified accordingly within Thirty Five days after the receipt of the application.

#### 29. Other Circumstances when shares may be transferred

If any shareholder dies or becomes insolvent or if the title to the shares held by him/her devolves on any other person under exciting Nepal laws in any manner, and if the person thus acquired title thereto submits an application together with evidence therefor, the shares shall be transferred to his/her name subject to the provisions of Sub-section (1) of Section 28.

#### 32. Lien on Shares

The Company may freeze all shares including dividends payable thereon which payment of installments is due from shareholders and recover the arrears from such dividends.

#### 33. Payment on Shares

(1) Payment in respect of shares shall be made whenever calls of payment of installment are made in accordance with the Articles.

(2) When making calls for payment of installment as mentioned in Sub-section (1), a Public Company shall send a notice in writing as set out in Form No. 9 of Schedule-1 to each shareholder, prescribing a time of Thirty days and place and time for payment, such notice shall also be published in the leading newspaper of Nepal.

(3) In case any shareholder fails to pay the amount due on shares within the prescribed time limit, a notice shall be sent to him/her and shall also be published in the leading newspapers, extending the time limit by Fifteen days and specifying clearly that in case payment is made within that time limit, it shall be accepted subject to payment of interest at Five per cent per annual. In case the amount due is not paid even

within the time so notified, the shares in respect to which the notice was given shall be forfeited.

Provided that, in case the shareholder submits an application within Three months of the expiry of this time limit showing reasonable cause for his/her failure to pay the installment due from him/her, the Board of Directors may rescind the forfeiture regarding such number of shares as have (fully) paid up by his/her (previous) installment.

(4) Shares forfeited under Sub-section (3) may be sold or other wise disposed of or cancelled on such conditions and such manners as the Board of Directors, subject to the Articles of the Company, may deem appropriate.

#### 34. Payment of Installment regarding Shares held jointly

Where shares are held jointly, the joint holders shall be jointly an individually liable to pay the installment due thereon.

#### 35. Equal portions to be presumed

Where there is no deed made specifying the portion owned by each holder in respect to shares held jointly, the holders shall be presumed to own equal portion therein.

#### 36. Owner of Shares and Debentures

Any person in whose name the shares, debentures etc. are registered in the Register of the Company shall be regarded as the owner thereof.

On the expiry of this term, the provisions of Sub-section (1) (of this Section) and Section 75 shall also be applicable to such Managing Agent.

(3) The provisions of Sub-section (1) and Clauses (a), (c), (f) and (g) of Sub-section (2) and Sub-section (3) of Section 66, relating to the appointment and holding of office of directors, shall also be applicable to the Managing Agent.

#### 75. Term of Appointment of Managing Agent

The term of appointment of the Managing Agent under Section 74 shall not exceed a period of Fifteen years with effect from the date of his/her first appointment. His/her term may be extended by a maximum of Ten years at One time with the approval of a General Meeting of the Company and concerned Department.

#### 76. Remuneration of Managing Agent

(1) Where the remuneration and commissions of the Managing Agent, these shall be as fixed by the General Meeting pursuant to the agreement entered into between the Company and the Managing Agent; the commissions shall not exceed Ten percent of the net profit of the Company.

Provided that, such remuneration and commissions shall also require the previous approval of the concerned Department.

(2) For the purposes of Sub-section (1), the net profit of the Company shall be calculated after making the following deductions (for

payments made or to be made) from the gross income of the Company during any financial year:

(a) Bonuses to employees and workers of the Company;

(b) Taxes, fees, dues, revenue, etc. levied or imposed by Government of Nepal of any other agency pursuant to nay law;

(c) Loss carried forward form the preceding financial year in so far as such loss constitutes excess of expenditure over income;

(d) Expenses of litigation;

(e) Any amount of money (including) damages, compensations, fines etc., paid or payable by the Company according to orders of a court;

(f) Premiums paid or payable to any insurance Company;

(g) Expenses of running the business of the Company;

(h) Interest on loans and debentures;

(i) Expenses for repairs and maintenance;

(j) Depreciation;

(k) Contributions or donations by the Company;

(l) Premiums on the sale of shares;

(m) Value of forfeited shares;

(n) Amounts invested by the Company as loans, which are considered to be irrecoverable;

(o) Profits from the sale of Company's assets;

(p) Salaries, remunerations, fees allowances, etc. of directors, Managing Agent and other employees of the Company;

(q) Compensation paid voluntarily by the Company;

(r) Other necessary expenses.

(3) In case any amendment in the contract relating to the managing agency between the Company and the Managing Agent is required, a resolution to this effect shall be passed by a General Meeting and submitted to the concerned Department for approval. No such amendment shall be made unless approval of the concerned Department has been obtained.

#### 77. Powers and Functions of the Managing Agent

(1) Unless otherwise provided for in the Memorandum or in the Articles, the Managing Agent shall have such powers and perform such functions of the Board of Directors as the Board may prescribe.

(2) The Managing Agent shall exercise his powers and perform his/her functions in the name of the Company, subject to the control of the Board of Directors.

(3) In case any function of the Company is held up on account of temporary absence of the Managing Agent for any reason, it may be performed by the Board of Directors during the period of his absence and any function so performed shall be deemed to have been performed by the Managing Agent.

#### 78. Shares to be purchased by the Managing Agent

The Managing Agent shall subscribe to at least Twenty percent of the issued shares capital of the Company and shall not be entitled to transfer or mortgage these shares during his term of office. In case, he/she owes any money to the Company at the time he ceases to be a Managing Agent, the Company may recover by the sale of such shares.

#### 79. Limitations of Powers of Managing Agent

Unless otherwise provided for the Memorandum or the Articles, the Managing Agent or (if the Managing Agent is a firm) any partner thereof shall be prohibited form engaging in the following activities or from causing others to engage in such activities with the intention of obtaining personal gain:

(a) To undertake in competition with the Company any business which the Company is already doing or any business similar to that of the Company.

(b) Obtain a monopoly or sales agency for the disposal of goods manufactured or to be manufactured by the Company.

#### 80. Office of the Managing Agent to be deemed Vacant

The office of Managing Agent shall be deemed to be vacant if:

(a) The Managing Agent being a firm, any of the partners thereof or the firm itself becomes insolvent or the firm is dissolved; or

(b) The Managing Agent being a corporate body, it is being wound up voluntarily or by the orders of a court; or

(c) The Managing Agent is held by any court to be suffering from any disqualification mentioned in Sub-section (3) of Section 74.

#### 81. Suspension of Managing Agent

In case insolvency proceeding are pending against the Managing Agent or in case any creditor files any claim against the Managing Agent on the basis of any document executed by the Managing Agent and in case the court before pronouncing a final judgment thereon directs that the personal property of the Managing Agent shall be placed under the charge of receiver, such Managing Agent shall be ipso facto suspended.

#### 82. Dismissal of Managing Agent

(1) In case after commencement of this Act the Managing Agent of any Company or, if the Managing Agent is a firm, any of its partners, of if the Managing Agent is a corporation, the director or any other person who holds a power of

attorney, is sentenced to imprisonment by any court, such Managing Agent may be dismissed by the Board of Directors with the approval of the concerned Department.

Provided that, in case the concerned firm or corporation removes the concerned partner, director or person holding a power of attorney within Thirty Five days from the date on which he is sentenced to such punishment, the provision of this Sub-section shall not be applicable to such firm or corporation.

(2) In case the Managing Agent causes loss to the Company as a result of his/her negligence or mismanagement of the business of the Company, any General Meeting of the Company may, by special resolution, remove him/her with the approval of the concerned Department.

#### 83. Convening of General Meeting

(1) At least Two directors of the Company may send a notice in writing to the Board of Directors, requesting the convening of General meeting to consider the resolution mentioned in Section 82.

Provided that, nothing contained in this Sub-section shall affect the convening of a General Meeting pursuant to other provisions of this Act or the Articles.

(2) On receipt of the notice mentioned in Sub-section (1), the Board of Directors shall duly send a copy thereof to the Managing Agent and convene an extra-ordinary General Meeting.

#### 84. Resignation of Managing Agent

(1) Unless otherwise provided for in the Memorandum, the Articles or the Agreement (between the Company and the Managing Agent), the Managing Agent may resign from his post by giving notice to the Board of Directors Three months in advance.

Provided that, the Managing Agent shall not cease to perform his duties unless his resignation is accepted under Sub-section (5).

(2) On receipt of the notice of resignation from the Managing Agent, the Board of Directors shall direct him to prepare and submit a report on the business and transactions of the Company until the day preceding the date specified in such notice of resignation or the date when his term of office expires, as well as a balance sheet and a profit and loss account for the period between the date of submission of the last balance-sheet and the date of the termination of the managing agency.

(3) In case the Managing Agent fails to prepare the report, balance-sheet or profit and loss account as directed by the Board of Directors under Sub-section (2) the Board shall itself prepare them.

(4) The Board of Directors shall place such report, profit and loss account, balance-sheet, as well as the report of the auditor before the General Meeting of the Company together with the letter of resignation tendered by the Managing Agent.

(5) After the resignation of the Managing Agent is placed before it in accordance with Sub-section (4), the General Meeting, by means of resolution, may accept the resignation or refuse to do so.

#### 85. Liability of Managing Agent

The Managing Agent shall not be absolved from his/her liability in respect to any loss incurred by the Company as a result of his failure to fulfill his/her duty or of any unauthorized action taken by him/her during his/her term of office, even after the termination or relinquishment of managing agency.

#### 86. Notice to be given to concerned Department

If the office of Managing Agent falls vacant for any reason, the Company shall notify the concerned Department accordingly.

#### 87. Managing Agency not subject to Succession or Transfer

(1) No successor of the Managing Agent shall be entitled to make any claim on or seek partition in the managing agency.

(2) The Managing Agent shall not transfer in any manner his managing agency without the prior approval of a General Meeting and the concerned Department.

#### 88. Claim for Compensation not to be entertained

In case the Managing Agent is suspended or dismissed, or the managing agency is terminated or his resignation is accepted pursuant to this Act, he/she shall not be entitled to make any claim against the Company for compensation.

Provided that, in the case of any other kind of transaction between him/her and the Company, this Section shall not be deemed to debar the Managing Agent from making claims in respect to such other transaction.

#### 89. Personal Liability for Payment

In case the Managing Agent of a Public Company is operated jointly by more than one person, such managing agency shall be registered at concerned Department pursuant to the provisions of this Act. In case compensation is to be paid for loss or damage caused by such Managing Agent, such amount as cannot be paid from the assets of the managing agency shall be personally borne by every partner thereof in the manner prescribed in Section 72 in respect tot he directors.

#### 90. Directors also to be Liable

In case the Managing Agent of a Public Company causes any loss or damage tot he Company deliberately or through negligence or by acting in contravention of law, the Managing Agent shall be liable for such loss or damage.

Provided that, in case such loss or damage has resulted from the failure of the Board of Directors to pay proper attention or make proper

arrangements, the directors also shall be liable in the manner prescribed in Section 72 for such portion of the loss as cannot be recovered from the Managing Agent.

(2) The Government liquidator may prescribe a time limit to creditors to prove their respective claims and may prevent any creditor from receiving any payment until their claims are proved.

#### 123. Government Liquidator to Maintain Accurate Records

The Government liquidator shall maintain accurate minutes or records of meetings convened by him and of other prescribed matters. In case any creditor of the concerned Company so desires the Government liquidator shall allow him to inspect such minutes or records.

#### 124. Government Liquidator to Submit Accounts

The Government liquidator shall submit to the concerned Department accounts of income received and expenditure made by him/her in his capacity of liquidator at least Twice a year, and the concerned Department shall have such accounts audited.

125 Order of Priority in Settlement of Liabilities: (1) The liabilities of a Company which is under liquidation shall be settled in the following order of priority:

(a) Expenses incurred ion liquidating the Company;

(b) Loans raised by Company on the assets of the Company as collateral;

(c) Salaries and wages of the employee and worker not exceeding Five Hundred Rupees each;

(d) Land and other taxes, or dues payable to the Government or to any local self government body;

(e) Loans obtained against such assets of the Company which had been attached within Three months prior to the issue of liquidation order; or

(f) Other debts.

(2) Always excluding the claims for which the time-limit prescribed pursuant to Sub-section (2) of Section 122 has expired. No debts shall be paid nor other liabilities fulfilled which are lower in the order of priority unless the Government liquidator is satisfied that those higher in the order of priority can be paid off.

(3) In case any assets remain in the possession of the Company after all obligations are settled, remaining assets shall be divided among the shareholders in portion to the shares held by them.

Provided that, in dividing such assets, priority shall be given to the preference shareholders. Only the balance left after payment is made to preference Shareholders, shall be divided among other shareholders on proportionate basis.

#### 126. Notice to be given of Fraud against Company or Creditors

In case the Government liquidator finds t hat any director or employee of the Company has committed any fraud against the Company or its creditors, he/she shall immediately notify the concerned Department, giving particulars of the unlawful acts committed in this regard. In case the Department, after receiving such information, so deems proper, it may order the Government liquidator to take legal action against persons suspected of such fraud.

#### 127. Submission of Final Accounts

After completing the liquidation proceedings of the Company, the Government liquidator shall submit the final accounts in respect thereto to the concerned Department, and in case such accounts are deemed correct after being audited, the concerned Department shall issue an order for dissolution and the Company shall then be deemed to have been dissolved with effect from the date of such order.

#### 128. Voluntary Liquidation

(1) The Company may go into voluntary liquidation by a resolution of the General Meeting in the following circumstances:

(a) In case the period prescribed in the Articles for running the Company, if any, has expired; or

(b) In case the Company decides to go into voluntary liquidation in view of its debtor position, or for any other reason.

(2) In case the General Meeting passes a resolution for voluntary liquidation of the company under Sub-section (1), it shall appoint one or more liquidators for the purpose. The remuneration of the liquidator so appointed shall be as prescribed by the General Meeting. The provisions

(3) The General Meeting shall also appoint one or more auditors for the purpose of auditing the accounts prepared by the liquidator. The remuneration of the auditor so appointed shall be as prescribed by the General Meeting.

#### 129. Concerned Department to be notified of Resolution for Voluntary Liquidation

In case the Company passes a resolution for its voluntary liquidation it shall forthwith notify the concerned Department accordingly and shall also publish Notification in this regard in the leading newspapers of Nepal within Fifteen days form the date when the resolution is passed.

#### 130. Business to be discontinued after Voluntary Liquidation

Unless otherwise provided elsewhere in this Act, after a resolution is passed for its voluntary liquidation the Company shall discontinue its business except in so far as it is necessary to continue such business to insure its liquidation in profitable manner.

#### 131. Power of the Liquidator

Except when any restriction or limitation is imposed by the General Meeting, the liquidator shall exercise all such powers as are exercised by the Government liquidator under Section 120 and Sub-section (1) of Section 121.

#### 132. Payment of Company's Debts

(1) In case the liquidator is of opinion that the Company may not be able to pay in full the debts incurred by it even within Twelve months from the date when the Company has gone into liquidation, he shall forthwith convene a meeting of the creditors of the Company and present therein all particulars relating to its assets and liabilities.

(2) In case such meeting is unable to determine that the debts incurred by the Company can be paid off in full, payments shall be made according to the order of priority specified in the Section 125.

#### 133. Application to concerned Department for Decision

The liquidator or any creditor may submit and application to the concerned Department for deciding any question arising in the course of the liquidation proceedings. In case the concerned Department receives such application it shall make a decision thereon within One month and communicate it to the applicant within Three days making the decision. In case the applicant is not satisfied with such decision, he/she may file an appeal with the Supreme Court within Thirty Five days form the date of receiving such decision.

#### 134. Voluntary Liquidation not to affect Power of the concerned Department

The Company's decision to go into voluntary liquidation shall not be itself prejudice the powers of the concerned Department to issue such orders as it may deem necessary in respect tot he liquidation of the Company under Section 128-133 on receipt of applications from creditors or otherwise.

#### 135. General Meeting to be convened if Liquidation Proceedings last more than One Year

In case it takes more than One year to complete the liquidation proceedings, the liquidator shall convene a general Meeting of the Company at the end of the first year of the commencement of those proceeding, and within Three months after the expiry of every year thereafter. At such meeting, he/she shall submit all particulars regarding the liquidation proceedings during the previous year as well as the accounts audited by the auditor.

#### 136. Report to be submitted to the General Meeting on completion of Liquidation Proceedings

Subject to the provisions of Section 135, after completing the liquidation proceeding, the liquidator shall prepare a report thereon, clearly stating the manner in which liquidation was carried out and in which the liabilities of the Company were settled. He/she shall (furthermore) have the accounts properly audited and then

convene a General Meeting of the Company for the purpose of submitting such report and accounts.

#### 137. Dissolution of the Company

On completion of the liquidation proceedings, the liquidator shall forthwith submit a report to this effect to the concerned Department. On receipt of such report, the concerned Department shall publish a Notification dissolving such Company.

#### 147. Fine not exceeding Two Hundred Thousand Rupees or Imprisonment for a term not exceeding Two Years or both

A fine not exceeding Two Thousand Rupees or imprisonment for a term not exceeding Two years, or both may be imposed upon any to the following persons for the following offences:-

(a) Upon any director, managing agent or employee of the Company who causes or attempts to cause any loss to the Company or any person by inserting false particulars in any concerned documents to the Company deliberately or through negligence or with mala fide intentions;

(b) Upon any directors, managing agent or employee of the Company who fails to maintain or to cause to maintain accounts, records or registers, required under this Act, or suppresses, conceals or damages such accounts registers or records deliberately or through negligence or with mala fide intentions, and thereby is likely to cause loss to the Company or to any person;

(c) Upon the auditor of the Company who inserts false particulars in any report made in his capacity as auditor or omits necessary

comments while auditing the accounts deliberately, or through negligence or with mala fide intentions;

(d) Upon the Government liquidator or any other liquidator who fails to convene the meeting of the creditors under Section 112 or 132 or makes payment contrary to the order of priority prescribed in Section 125, or fails to maintain accounts, records or registers under Section 123 or fails to take over accounts and (other) documents, or maintains false records, or fails to submit any report which he is required to submit or submits a false report, or fails to handover cash, store or accounts to be handed over by him/her on the termination of his/her assignment;

(e) Upon any director, managing agent, government liquidator or any employee who fails to handover documents, records, cash and stores in his/her charge on the termination of his/her office or on receipt of order to liquidate the Company, to the person appointed to receive them, and upon the latter in case he fails to accept them.
