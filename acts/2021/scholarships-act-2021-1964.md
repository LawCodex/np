An Act to provide for some legal provisions relating to scholarship to be given by Government of Nepal or to be given through Government of Nepal

Preamble: Whereas, it is expedient to make some legal provisions with regard to scholarships to be given by or through Government of Nepal;

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty the King Mahendra Bir Bikram Shahdev in consultation with, and consent of, the Rastriya Panchayat.

#### 1. Short Title, Extension and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called "Scholarships Act, 2021(1964)."

(2) This Act shall be enforced all over Nepal and shall also be applicable to every Nepalese citizen wherever he/she may be residing outside Nepal.

Definition: Unless the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act "scholarship" means economic aid (donation) or facility provided by Government of Nepal for any study, research or training, economic aid (donation) or facility or technical assistance provided by any foreign country, international or other donor agency or any national or foreign educational institution to the Government of Nepal and this term also includes any other facility prescribed by the Government of Nepal stating scholarship by a Notification in the Nepal Gazettee.

#### 4. Duty of Recipient of Scholarship

(1) The person getting scholarship shall, within Three months after the study period is over, submit a report to Ministry of Education and Sports or his/ her concerned department.

 (2) Government of Nepal may, within One year after submission of report under Sub-section (1), engage the person in any post in an organization or corporation which is an undertaking of Government of Nepal or to which Government of Nepal has provided financial assistance for a period up to Five years, and it shall be the duty of the concerned person to be engaged in the service accordingly.

#### 5. Punishment

(1) Whoever, being a foreign national, receives or scholarship lying that he/she is a Nepalese citizen, shall be liable to a punishment with recovery of the amount of scholarship and an additional imprisonment up to Five years or a fine up to Ten Thousand Rupees or both in addition to the punishment according to other Nepal laws.

(2) Whoever certifies or causes to certify a foreign citizen as Nepalese citizen and in case such foreign citizen receives a scholarship, the person so certifying or causing to certify shall be punished with an

#### 6. Special Provisions with Regard to Recipients of Scholarship Prior to Enforcement of this Act

(1) Even the persons studying or under going training in any scholarship from Government of Nepal or through Government of Nepal prior to enforcement of this Act shall also abide by the terms and conditions referred to in section 4 and if any such person does not abide by those terms and conditions, the cost of scholarship shall be recovered from him/her and Government of Nepal shall also have powers to cancel his/her scholarship if he/she is still getting the scholarship.

(2) Save in case of exemption granted by having published a notice in the Nepal Gazettes, any person who has completed study or training under any scholarship from Government of Nepal or through Government of Nepal prior to the enforcement of the Act shall, within One year from the date of enforcement of this Act, engage in the post or service prescribed by Government of Nepal at least for a period of Five years. #### 10. Government of Nepal's Powers to Frame Rules

(1) Government of Nepal may frame Rules for implementing objectives of this Act.

(2) Without causing any adverse effect on the generality of powers conferred by Sub-section (1), Rules may be framed in the following matters as well: –

(a) Approval of subjects of scholarship,

(b) Method to select candidates,

(c) Extending duration of scholarship,

(d) Converting scholarship from one subject to another by a person,

(e) Submitting report upon completion of study or training,

(f) Providing study leave to a government employee.
