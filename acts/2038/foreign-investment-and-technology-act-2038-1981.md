# Foreign Investment and Technology Act, 2038

↔ Foreign Investment and Technology Act, 2038 (1981) Date of the Royal Seal and Publication

2038.6.4 (20 Sept. 1981)

Act Number 9 of 2038 (1981)

An Act made to provide for matters relating to Foreign Investment and Technology

Preamble: Whereas, in order to maintain comfort and well being of the general people and to contribute in the economic development of the country, it is expedient to make legal provision for promotion and regularization of the foreign investment and technology, and thereby mobilize the capital, human resource and the natural resources to the optimum.

Now, therefore, be it enacted by His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev, on the advice and with the consent of the Rastriya Panchayat.

#### 1. Short Title and Commencement

(1) This Act may be called "Foreign Investment and Technology Act, 2038 (1981)".

(2) It shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act,-

(a) "Industry" means the medium or large industry for which an agreement for foreign investment, foreign loan or transfer of technology has been approved pursuant to this Act or the Rules made thereunder.

<sup>1</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np Explanation: For the purpose of this Clause, the medium or large industry shall mean the medium or large industry as defined in the Industrial Enterprise Act, 2038.

(b) "Foreign Investment" means the share investment in the form of foreign currency or tangible asset, made by the foreign investor in the concerned industry and it also includes the reinvestment.

(c) "Foreign Loan" means the loan or loan facility in the form of foreign currency or tangible asset obtained by the concerned industry from the foreign investor.

(d) "Technology Transfer Agreement" means an agreement entered into between the concerned industry and the foreign investor on the following matters:-

(1) To use any technological knowledge of foreign origin,

(2) To use any trade mark of foreign ownership,

(3) To get foreign technological service, consultancy service, management service and marketing service.

(e) "Foreign Investor" means any foreign firm, company, individual, government or international institution.

(f) "National Investor" means any national firm, company, institution or Nepali citizen.

(g) "Department" means the Department of Industry of His Majesty Government.

(h) "Committee" means the Industrial Promotion Committee as established pursuant to Industrial Enterprises Act, 2038.

(i) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed or as prescribed in the Rules framed hereunder.

Obtaining of Permission: Any foreign investor or the foreign investor and the national investor desirous of establishing any joint venture industry shall have to file an application in the prescribed format to the Department to obtain the permission of such joint venture.

#### 4. Granting of Permission

In case an application is filed pursuant to Section 3, the Department may, on the basis of the recommendation of the Committee, grant permission to establish the industry.

#### 5. Matters to be considered by the Committee while making any Recommendation

The Committee, while making any recommendation for the purpose of Section 4 shall have to consider the following matters:-

(a) Whether the concerned industry is appropriate for Nepal or not.

(b) Whether it has the nature of import substitution or export promotion or not.

(c) Whether it has the nature to utilize the local raw material or not.

(d) Whether it has the nature to make available the employment, management and technical training to the Nepalese citizen or not.

(e) Whether the terms and conditions of the agreement for the foreign investment, foreign loan and the transfer of technology are reasonable or not.

#### 6. Matters to be mentioned in the Letter of Permission

While granting permission to establish any industry pursuant to Section 4, the Department shall have to grant the letter of permission to the applicant mentioning the following matters:-

(a) The name, particulars and place of the industry;

(b) The objective and the capacity of the industry;

<sup>3</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np (c) The ratio (percentage) of the total investment and the foreign investment,

(d) The amount and the source of foreign loan and the payment of principal and interest of such loan;

(e) The value, as assessed by the Department, of the tangible assets obtained in the form of foreign investment or foreign loan;

(f) The agreement for transfer of technology and the payment to be made thereunder;

(g) The concerned foreign currency;

(h) The period for the use of foreign investment or foreign loan; and

(i) Other matters as deemed necessary by the Department.

#### 7. Obtaining of Approval

(1) No industry shall be authorized to do any of the following acts without the prior approval of the Department:

(a) To obtain foreign loan;

(b) To increase the ratio (percentage) of the foreign investment;

(c) To enter into an agreement for the transfer of technology;

(d) To diversify the objective, expand the capacity or modernize the industry or to arrange new or extra foreign investment for such purpose;

(2) While granting approval for the purpose pursuant to Sub-section (1), the Department shall take an action on the basis of the recommendation of the Committee.

#### 8. Recognition of the already established Industry

Any industry, the agreement of which has been approved for the foreign investment, foreign loan or transfer of technology, by Government of Nepal, any

<sup>4</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np Department or Office of Government of Nepal prior to the commencement of the Act, shall be deemed to be an industry under this Act.

#### 9. Facility, Concession and Protection

Any industry established pursuant to this Act shall also be granted the same facility, concession and protection as is provided by the Industrial Enterprises Act, 2038.

#### 10. Right to Repatriate the Amount

Any foreign investor, the agreement of whom has been approved for foreign investment, foreign loan or transfer of technology, may repatriate, outside Nepal the amount received by him in lieu of the agreement in foreign currency at the prevailing rate of exchange in the following manner:-

(a) The amount received by sale of the whole or any part of the foreign investment,

(b) The amount received as dividend in lieu of the foreign investment,

(c) The amount received as the payment of the principal and interest of the foreign loan,

(d) The amount received under the agreement for the transfer of technology,

(e) The amount received as compensation in lieu of nationalization or acquisition of any property.

Provided that, the amount received pursuant to Clauses (a) and (b) may be repatriated outside Nepal at the time of special situation, only in such installment as is fixed by Government of Nepal.

#### 11. Establishment of Industry by Government of Nepal

(1) Government of Nepal may, by entering into agreement with the foreign or national investor, establish industry in the form of a public company.

<sup>5</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np (2) Any industry established pursuant to Sub-section (1), shall enjoy the facility, concession and protection as mentioned in the agreement.

#### 12. Establishment of Joint Venture Industry with the Foreign Government

(1) Government of Nepal may establish industry by entering into agreement with the foreign Government.

(2) The registration and operation of the industry established pursuant to Sub-section (1), and the facility, concession and the protection to be enjoyed by it shall be as mentioned in the agreement.

#### 13. Prohibition to Nationalization

Industry established under this Act and any property of such industry shall not be nationalized.

Provided that, Government of Nepal may, if necessary for the national interest and defence, nationalize by giving just compensation.

#### 14. Government of Nepal may Issue Directives

Government of Nepal may issue directives to any industry in the following matters and it shall be the duty of the concerned industry to follow such directives:-

(a) Matters relating to the quality standard, price and the distribution system of the product and services of the industry,

(b) Matters relating to management of the industry,

(c) Matters relating to maintenance of the harmonious relationship between the labour and the management,

(d) Matters relating to the pollution of the environment,

(e) Matters relating to the protection of public welfare.

#### 15. Submission of Report

Industry under this Act shall have to submit report as prescribed to the Department.

<sup>6</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np 16. Power of Government of Nepal: (1) Government of Nepal may take following action against any industry which takes any action in contravention to any provision of this Act or Rules framed thereunder:

(a) To impose a fine extending from One Thousand Rupees to Fifty Thousand Rupees,

(b) To decrease, suspend or stop the facility, concession and the protection, provided to the concerned industry,

(c) To operate the concerned industry under the direction of the representative designated by it,

(d) To close down.

(2) Before taking any action under this Section, the concerned industry shall be given a reasonable opportunity to submit an explanation on the matter or to correct the situation.

#### 17. Arbitration

(1) Any dispute arising out of the enforcement of this Act or Rules framed thereunder if not settled amicably between themselves by the foreign investor, the concerned industry and the Department, it shall be settled by arbitration.

(2) Unless otherwise provided by the Department on the request of the foreign investor, the prevailing arbitration Rules of the United Nations Commission for International Trade Law (UNCITRAL) shall be followed or arbitration.

(3) The Arbitration shall be held in kathmandu. The law of Nepal shall be applicable in the arbitration.

#### 18. Power to make Rules

(1) Government of Nepal may frame Rules to carry out the purposes of this Act.

<sup>7</sup>www.lawcommission.gov.np (2) Without prejudice to the generality of the power conferred by Sub-section (1), Rules may be framed particularly on any or all of the following matters:-

(a) To specify the industries which may be established or operated on foreign investment.

(b) To prescribe the terms and conditions relating to the foreign investment.

(c) To evaluate the tangible asset obtained in the form of foreign investment or foreign loan.

(d) Matters relating to the payment of the principal and interest of the foreign loan.

(e) Matters relating to the guarantee and protection of the foreign loan.

(f) Matters relating to the classification of the foreign investors and other facilities to be provided to them.

#### 19. Saving

In matters specifically provided for in this Act, the provisions of this Act shall prevail and in others the provision of prevalent Nepal law shall apply.
