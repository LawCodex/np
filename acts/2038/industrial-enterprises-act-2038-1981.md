Act No. 11 of 2038 (1981)

An Act to make Provision Relating to Industrial Enterprises

Preamble: Whereas, for the speedy economic development of the country it is expedient to facilitate the employment opportunities by creating an environment of industrial investment which may be more facilitative, easy and encouraging; to increase the productivity, to encourage the private sector to greater extent and to maintain the comfort and economic interest of the people in general

Now, therefore, His Majesty King Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev has made this Act, on the advice and with the consent of the Rastriya Panchayat.

#### 1. Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the "Industrial Enterprises Act, 2038".

(2) It shall come into force immediately.

#### 2. Definitions

Unless the subject or context otherwise requires, in this Act,-

(a) "Industry" means any industry as referred to in Section 9.

(b) "Cottage Industry" means any industry having machinery, equipments and tools valued up to Two Hundred Thousand Rupees with a fixed asset valued up to Eight Hundred Thousand Rupees, if it is located in the city area, and with a fixed asset valued up to Five Hundred Thousand Rupees, if it is located anywhere other than the city area.

(c) "Small Industry" means any industry having machinery; equipments and tools valued more than Two Hundred Thousand Rupees with a fixed asset valued up to Two Million Rupees.

(d) "Medium Industry" means an industry with a fixed asset valued more than Two Million Rupees but not exceeding Ten Million Rupees.

(e) "Large Industry" means an industry with a fixed asset valued more than Ten Million Rupees.

(f) "Fixed Asset" means the asset owned by the industry such as land or land improvement (e.g. land leveling, filling, fencing), physical infrastructures associated with land (e.g. drainage, sewerage, internal road), building and housing, machinery, equipments and tools, means of such transportation which may be an inseparable part of the industry, electrical equipments, furniture, office equipment, water and telephone distribution system. This term shall also includes the expenses incurred on such technical consultancy and supervision which may be engaged prior to or during different stages of construction and the pre-investment and pre-operation cost and the amount of interest incurred during the construction period which is to be capitalized.

Provided that, fixed asset in case of a cottage industry, shall include only such machinery, equipments tools and premises which are either owned or taken on rent by such industry.

(g) "Committee" means the Industrial Promotion Committee constituted under Section 7.

(h) "Operation Date" means the date from which the concerned industry either starts its production or provides its services.

(i) "Value- Added" means the ratio that may remain after deducting the total amount consisting of the cost of the raw material, auxiliary raw material and chemicals imported from any foreign country for such production, any wage remuneration, consultancy fee, royalty, given to any foreigner and the depreciation amount of the imported fixed asset, from the cost of the annual gross production of the concerned industry. <sup>1</sup>(i1) "Raw Material" means the raw material to be used directly in the production, assimilation and processing procedure of an industry. <sup>2</sup>(i2) "Auxiliary Raw Material" means the auxiliary raw material to be used directly in the production, assimilation and processing procedure of an industry. <sup>3</sup>(i3) "Chemicals" means the chemical substances to be used directly in the production, assimilation and processing procedure of an industry. <sup>4</sup>(i4) "Local Raw Material" means the local raw material or the raw material produced by local industry by adding at least Fifty percent value to the foreign raw material.

(j) "Export Promotion Area" means the area where such industries which export cent percent of their products after they are value-added by more than Twenty percent are established.

(k) "Tourism Area" means the area which for the purpose of development of tourism industry, Government of Nepal may designate, by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette.

(l) "Underdeveloped Area" means the area which Government of Nepal may designate by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette.

(m) "Concerned Department" means the Department o Industry of the Department of Cottage and Village Industry of Government of Nepal or such other Department or office as may be designated by Government of Nepal.

(n) "Prescribed" or "as prescribed" means prescribed or as prescribed in the Rules made under this Act.

#### 4. Order of preference in granting permission for the establishment of industry

While granting permission for the establishment of the following industries, preference shall be given to the following firm, institution or company established under the prevailing law:

(a) For the establishment of any cottage industry as may be specified by Government of Nepal by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette to any private firm, partnership firm, institution or company.

(b) For the establishment of the small industry to any partnership firm, institution or company.

(c) For the establishment of the medium and large industry to any company.

#### 5. Granting of permission

When permission is sought for the establishment of an industry under this Act, the Department of Industry, in the case of small, medium or large industry and the Department of Cottage

and Rural Industry, in the case of any cottage industry as specified by Government of Nepal by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette, may grant permission.

Provided that, the Department of Industry shall take into consideration of the recommendation of the committee while granting permission for the establishment of medium and large industry.

6. Restriction in the establishment of defense industry:

Notwithstanding anything contained in other Sections of this Act, nobody except Government of Nepal shall be entitled to establish such defense industry which may produce such items like military armament, ammunition or explosive.

#### 7. Constitution, functions, duties and powers of the committee

(1) Government of Nepal shall constitute an Industrial Promotion Committee by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette.

(2) The functions duties and powers of the committee shall be as follows:

(a) To make recommendation for the establishment of medium or large industry in pursuant to the policy of Government of Nepal and the prevailing law.

(b) To make necessary arrangement for effective attainment of the facilities granted in connection with industrial development.

(c) To constitute sub-committees as may be required, with the representation of concerned agencies in industries for the sake of industrial promotion and facilitation of entrepreneurship.

(d) To review the industrial policy in every Five years.

(3) The other functions, duties, powers and procedures of the committee shall be as prescribed.

#### 8. Prohibition in the establishment of industry close to the international border

(1) No Industry shall be established within Eight kilometers of the international border irrespective of the nature of such industry which may or may not require permission for the establishment under this Act or any other prevailing law.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in Sub-section (1), any of the following industries may be established within Eight kilometers:

(a) Cottage industry.

(b) Any industry to be established within such Industrial Estate as may be established either by Government of Nepal or any other person with the approval of Government of Nepal.

(c) Tourism Industry.

(d) Service Industry.

(e) Entertainment industry.

Provided that, Government of Nepal, if it deems necessary, may also impose prohibition referred to in Sub-section (1) as regards to the above mentioned industries.

#### 9. Classification of industry

(1) For the purpose of this Act, industries are classified as follows:

(a) Production oriented industry: Any of the following industries engaged in processing or production by

making use of raw material or semi-processed material or by-product or waste material.

(1) Industry producing essential consumer goods:

Industry product rice, pulses, flour, oil, sugar, molasses. milk, ghee, dairy product, loaf, biscuit, tea, cloth, shoe, slipper, soap, matches, paper, pens and pencil, ink, stationary goods and medicines and cotton ginning and spinning industry.

(2) Any other production oriented industries.

(b) Energy oriented industry: Industry generating energy out of water resources, solar power, coal, natural oil, gas, biogas and other resources.

(c) Agro based special industry: Animal husbandry, poultry, fishery, bee-keeping, sericulture dairy farm and such other industries as Government of Nepal may specify as agro based special industry.

(d) Mineral excavation and processing industry.

(e) Tourism industry: Industry directly supplementing, helping or assisting in the promotion of tourism by providing special type of entertainment and facilities such as tourist cottage, tourist lodge, motel, hotel, restaurant of tourist standard, bar, trekking, travel agency, skiing, rafting, cable care complex etc.

(f) Service industry:

(1) Public transportation system (truck, bus service, rope way, tram way, trolley bus, water way transportation).

(2) Construction industry (any civil, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, extracting under ground water, drilling, boring etc. that may be equipped with machinery and equipment.)

(3) Cold storage, large go-down, rental apartment,

(4) Machine shop, workshop and repairing shop,

(5) Printing press,

(6) Consultancy services relating to industry,

(7) Enterprise relating to hospital, nursing home and X-ray,

(8) Enterprise relating to packing, welding and ginning,

(9) Enterprise relating to photography,

(10) Such other industries as Government of Nepal may specify as service industry.

(g) Entertainment industry:

(1) Enterprise relating to Cultural dance and music.

(2) Enterprise relating to production of motion picture.

(3) Enterprise relating to Circus.

(h) Assembling industry: Industry producing finished goods by assembling different components.

(2) If any industry either falls under more than one category mentioned under Sub-section (1) or does not fall under any one of them Government of Nepal shall make the decision as to the category under which such industry is to be reckoned.

Notwithstanding anything contained in the 8acts relating to income tax, sales tax customs and excise duty and other prevailing law, the following facilities, concessions and protection shall be granted to the industries to be established under this Act:

(a) Facility as to income tax: (1) Production oriented cottage industry shall be granted full exemption from income tax for a period of Six years starting from the operation date.

Provided that,

(1) No cottage industry shall be entitled for such exemption if Government of Nepal by a Notification published in the Nepal Gazette, debarred such industry from tax exemption.

(2) Every industry entitled to enjoy facilities pursuant to Sub-clause (1) shall enjoy such facility during the entire stipulated period.

(2) Production oriented small, medium and large industries shall be granted full exemption from income tax for the following period starting from the operation date.

(a) Five years for any industry which has Twenty to Fifty percent value added.

(b) For any industry which has more than Fifty percent value-added, at the rate of one additional year for every unit of Ten percent of such value addition. <sup>9</sup>(2a) Notwithstanding anything contained in Sub-clause (1) and (2) if the standard/status of any industry is changed, such industry from the date of the change of its standard/status, shall be entitled only to those remainder of the facilities which comes out of a deduction of the facilities which it has already enjoyed from those facilities which it is entitled to enjoy under the new standard/status.

(3) In addition to the period of exemption mentioned in Sub-clause (1) and (2), 10the industry based on local raw material as prescribed by Government of Nepal by publishing a Notification in the Nepal Gazette shall enjoy income tax exemption for additional Two years.

(4) Energy oriented and mineral excavation and processing industries shall be granted full exemption from income tax, for a period of Six years starting from the operation date.

Provided that, such industry shall be granted full exemption from income tax for an additional period of Six years if it has more than Twenty Five percent value-added.

(5) Tourism industry shall be granted full exemption from income tax for the following period starting from the operation date:-

(a) Seven years for those which are established in the tourism area.

(b) Five years for those which are established in any other area.

(6) Machine shops producing parts of components, tools and finished products by the help of semi-automatic or automatic machines (e.g. lathe, shaping, milling etc) cold storage, trolley bus and large go-down, hospital, x-ray enterprise large scale rental apartment, printing press, waterway transportation, of such standard as Government of Nepal may specify shall be given full exemption from income tax for a period of Three years starting from the operation date. Any other service industries expect those mentioned shall not be granted income tax exemption.

(7) Industry operating within export promotion zone shall be granted full exemption from income tax for a period of Ten years starting from the operation date and Fifty percent thereafter for another period of Five years.

(8) Agro based special industry shall be granted full exemption from income tax for a period of Ten years starting from the operation date.

(9) Industry operating within underdeveloped area shall be granted full exemption from income tax for an additional period of Three years over and above the facility as to income tax enjoyed by

any other industry of similar nature established within any other areas.

(11) Any industry exporting more than Twenty Five percent of its annual production shall be granted Fifteen percent exemption on the amount of income tax to be levied on such industry for every such year and similarly Twenty Five percent exemption shall be granted if it exports more than Fifty percent of its annual production.

#### 13. Approval required for renovation, modernization, diversification or extension of the industry

Every industry shall be required to obtain approval of Government of Nepal for its renovation, modernization, diversification or extension.

#### 14. Facilities to be enjoyed by industries already established

(1) If any industry, established prior to the commencement of this Act, has been enjoying or entitled to enjoy any facility, concession or protection under the provision of the then 25prevailing law such industry shall be entitled to enjoy the same facility, concession or protection as provided by 26that law.

Provided that, this Act shall prevail in case of the facility for which the period is not stipulated.

#### 15. Government of Nepal may issue directives

Government of Nepal may issue directives to any industry in the following matters and it shall be the duty of the concerned industry to follow such directives-

(a) Matters relating to the quality standard, price and the distribution system of the product or services of the industry.

(b) Matters relating to the management of any industry which is either closed or running at a capacity less than Twenty percent of its total production.

(c) Matters relating to be maintenance of harmonious relationship between the labour and the management.

(d) Matters relating to the pollution of environment.

(e) Matters relating to the protection of public welfare.

#### 16. Report to be submitted

Every industry established 28under this Act or under the prevailing laws prior to the commencement of this Act shall have to submit to the concerned department such report as may be prescribed.

#### 17. Power of Government of Nepal

(1) Government of Nepal may take any of the following actions against any person for establish 29any industry without obtaining permission or against an industry for non-compliance of the directives issued pursuant to Section 15 or for non-submission of report pursuant to Section 16 or for violating any other provisions of Act or for abusing the facilities provided under this Act:-

(a) To impose a fine extending from One Thousand to Fifty Thousand Rupees.

(b) To suspend (hold) or restrict the facility, concession and protection.

(c) To depute representative to operate the industry under his/her supervision.

(d) To close down.

(2) Before taking any action under Sub-section (1), the concerned industry shall be given reasonable opportunity either to submit explanation or to correct the shortcoming.

<sup>30</sup>(3) Government of Nepal may impose a fine of Twenty Five Thousand Rupees on any industry which closes itself without obtaining approval of the concerned department pursuant to Section 10C.

#### 18. Arbitration

(1) Every mutual dispute with regards to any industrial matter shall be referred to arbitration consisting of the following arbitrators:

(a) On arbitrator to be appointed by each of the concerned parties.

(b) One legal expert selected by the arbitrators appointed pursuant to Clause (a).

(2) The arbitrator selected pursuant to Clause (b) of Sub-section (1) shall act as Chairperson in the arbitration proceeding.

(3) If any party fails to appoint its arbitrator another party may serve a written notice to the defaulting party to appoint the arbitrator within a period of Three months. If the party concerned fails to appoint its arbitrator within the stipulated period, Government of Nepal shall appoint an arbitrator.

(4) If the arbitrators appointed pursuant to Clause (1) of Sub-section (1) fail to select the arbitrator pursuant to Clause (b) within Three months from the date of their appointment, Government of Nepal shall appoint such arbitrators.

(5) No suit or complaint may be instituted in any office or court in connection with any matter referred to the arbitrators pursuant of this Section. The award delivered b y the arbitrators on such matter shall be binding on the concerned parties subject to other provisions contained in this Section.

(6)The arbitrators in connection with their proceeding shall have the same powers as that of a court under the prevailing law in respect of the attendance and examination of witness and in respect of the production of documents.

(7) In case any arbitrator dies or if he/she shows inability or negligence to act, the party appointing such arbitrator may appoint another arbitrator. If the party fails to appoint such other arbitrator, the other party may serve a written notice to make appointment within a period of Fifteen days. If the concerned party fails to appoint such arbitrator within the stipulated period or the arbitrator thus appointed also either shows inability

or negligence to act, the remaining arbitrators alone may proceed with the arbitration proceedings and make their award.

(8) In the event of divergence of opinion among the arbitrators, the decision of the majority shall prevail. In case of a tie, the opinion endorsed by the Supreme Court shall prevail.

(9) The award made by the arbitrators pursuant to this Section shall be final and be executed as good as the decision of a court. No suit, complaint or appeal may be instituted against such award in any office or court.

(10) Except as provided in this Section, other procedures as to arbitration shall be as prescribed.

#### 19. Power to make Rules

(1) Government of Nepal may frame Rules to implement the objective of this Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of the power conferred by Sub-section (1), Rules may be made particularly in respect of the following matters:

(a) Matters relating to the classification of industries and the facilities to be provided to them.

(b) Matters relating to fixing of percentage in respect of value-added.

(c) Matters relating to the evaluation of the fixed asset of an industry.

(d) Matters relating to the establishment and management of the export promotion zone.

#### 20. Delegation of Authority

Government of Nepal may delegate the authority, either conferred upon it or upon the Department of Industry or

the Department of Cottage and Rural Industry under this Act or under the Rules made under this Act, to any department, office or officer.

#### 21. Saving

Notwithstanding anything contained in the prevailing law, the provisions of this Act and the Rules made thereunder shall prevail.

#### 22. Repeal

The Industrial Enterprises Act, 2030 is, hereby, repealed.
